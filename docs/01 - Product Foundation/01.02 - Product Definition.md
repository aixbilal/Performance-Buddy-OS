# Product Definition

## Purpose

Provide a precise operational definition of Performance Buddy OS and distinguish its core system from individual modules and future intelligence.

## Canonical definition

Performance Buddy OS is a **private, personal-first, desktop-first, offline-first Personal Performance Operating System**. It coordinates goals, adaptive daily plans, focused execution, evidence, reviews, and domain-specific records so the user can decide and act with better context over time.

## What “operating system” means here

The term is a product metaphor, not a computer kernel. The application supplies a control and intelligence layer across personal domains. It organizes priorities, schedules, sessions, trackers, calculations, evidence, and recommendations while leaving specialist systems in their proper roles—for example, Obsidian remains the external knowledge-storage layer.

## Core product loop

1. Represent goals and the systems intended to advance them.
2. Create a feasible plan under fixed and protected constraints.
3. Support execution and capture what actually happened.
4. Preserve evidence and provenance.
5. Analyze outcomes with deterministic logic first and AI only within its capability boundary.
6. Propose understandable adjustments.
7. Let the human approve meaningful changes and form the next plan.

## Included domain context

The product spans daily performance, goals, academics and grades, study planning, knowledge, software-development learning, language learning, fitness and recovery, spiritual/personal routines, lightweight money tracking, analytics, and AI assistance. Domain ownership remains separate; cross-domain coordination does not erase specialist rules.

## Current and future distinction

The definition applies across the vision, but initial `CORE` value must not depend on `LOCAL`, `V1`, `V2`, `V3`, `CLOUD`, or `MOBILE`. Future intelligence enhances the loop; it does not create the basic product.
