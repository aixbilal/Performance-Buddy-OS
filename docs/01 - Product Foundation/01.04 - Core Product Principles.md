# Core Product Principles

## Purpose

Define the permanent principles that govern product, design, architecture, and prioritization decisions.

## Principles

1. **Personal-first.** Optimize for the owner’s real life before generic market breadth.
2. **Desktop-first.** The desktop is the initial command center; mobile may later become a capture companion.
3. **Offline-first CORE.** Essential planning, tracking, execution, records, and deterministic calculations work without internet or cloud AI.
4. **Goal → System → Action.** Daily work remains connected to a repeatable method and meaningful direction.
5. **Living plans.** Plans adapt to evidence and changed conditions instead of punishing deviation.
6. **Evidence over time alone.** Duration is useful context, not automatic proof of learning or progress.
7. **Consistency over fragile streaks.** Rest, excused states, partial completion, and maintenance modes reflect reality.
8. **Protected constraints.** Sleep, essential health/safety boundaries, and fixed commitments are not casually traded away.
9. **Human control.** AI proposes; deterministic rules validate; the human decides meaningful writes.
10. **Deterministic authority.** Mathematics, policy application, validation, and hard constraints are implemented as testable rules, not LLM guesses.
11. **Explainability.** Important recommendations and changes expose reasons, evidence, uncertainty, and constraints.
12. **Privacy by architecture.** Local authority, data minimization, permission zones, and provider boundaries are first-class.
13. **Calm intelligence.** Use clear, supportive language without shame, guilt, or aggressive motivation.
14. **Domain integrity.** Coordinate domains without forcing them into one score or duplicated rule set.
15. **Simple before elaborate.** Avoid premature services, frameworks, multi-agent systems, and enterprise complexity.
16. **Developer understanding.** AI-assisted code must remain reviewable and learnable by the project owner.

## Application

When principles conflict, protect safety, source authority, privacy, and human control first. Record material trade-offs rather than silently choosing convenience. Later phases may operationalize these principles but may not weaken them without an approved decision.
