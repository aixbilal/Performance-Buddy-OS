# Product Success Criteria

## Purpose

Define measurable and reviewable conditions for judging whether the product creates value without using misleading vanity metrics.

## Success dimensions

### Utility

- The user can plan, execute, capture, reconcile, and review offline.
- Common daily actions require low friction and do not demand excessive logging.
- The product remains useful when all AI and cloud services are unavailable.

### Trust

- Academic and financial mathematics is reproducible and tested.
- Important values retain provenance and applicable rule versions.
- Recommendations expose reasons, evidence, uncertainty, and constraints.
- Meaningful AI-proposed changes are never committed without approval.

### Sustainable behavior

- Plans reflect fixed commitments, sleep, recovery, and realistic capacity.
- Rest, excused, partial, and maintenance states prevent false failure.
- Reviews encourage learning and adjustment rather than guilt.

### Cross-domain value

- Domains remain independently meaningful while relevant interactions are visible.
- The system can show why one area deserves attention without creating a universal life score.
- Academic focus does not erase development, fitness, language, routines, or recovery.

### Learning and maintainability

- The owner can explain key architectural and implementation decisions.
- AI-assisted code is reviewed, tested, documented, and maintainable.
- Scope remains simple enough for a personal project to deliver incrementally.

## Evidence of success

Use task completion quality, plan-versus-reality reconciliation, repeated use, error rates, calculation tests, time-to-capture, recommendation acceptance/rejection outcomes, and qualitative user review. Metrics must be interpreted in context; longer usage time or more records are not automatically better.

## Failure signals

Cloud dependence, data distrust, ignored recommendations, burdensome capture, recurring sleep sacrifice, shame-inducing language, or architecture the owner cannot understand indicate failure even if feature count grows.
