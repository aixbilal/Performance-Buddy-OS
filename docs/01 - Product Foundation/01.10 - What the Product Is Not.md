# What the Product Is Not

## Purpose

Prevent misleading positioning and architecture by stating categories Performance Buddy OS must not silently become.

## The product is not

- **A generic habit tracker.** Trackers are one mechanism; the product also coordinates goals, plans, evidence, domain rules, and adjustment.
- **Only an academic tracker.** Academics are important but coexist with development, knowledge, language, fitness, recovery, routines, and money.
- **A motivational punishment system.** It does not shame missed work or optimize fragile streaks.
- **An autonomous life manager.** AI recommends; the human retains authority.
- **An LLM wrapper.** CORE value and deterministic correctness do not depend on a model.
- **A medical, therapeutic, or financial-advice service.** It records and supports bounded personal decisions without claiming professional authority.
- **A full accounting or banking platform.** Money tracking is lightweight and separate from an overall performance score.
- **A replacement for Obsidian.** Obsidian remains the external knowledge-storage layer.
- **A public social network or collaboration platform.** Social features are not part of the initial personal-first scope.
- **A cloud-first SaaS product.** Accounts, tenancy, subscriptions, and remote authority are not required for CORE.
- **An employee surveillance or institutional grading system.** The data and recommendations serve the individual owner.
- **A pretext for enterprise complexity.** Microservices, queues, multiple databases, heavy orchestration, or agent swarms require demonstrated need.

## Boundary rule

Similar features do not change these exclusions by stealth. If a future proposal materially changes the product category, it must be labeled `FUTURE`, evaluated in Phase 47, and approved through the decision and baseline processes.
