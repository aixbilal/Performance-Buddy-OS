# Product Naming Status

## Purpose

Record the canonical product name, historical name, usage rules, and conditions for any future naming revision.

## Canonical name

**Performance Buddy OS** is the approved product name under `DEC-001` and Locked Documentation Baseline v1.0.

Use:

- `Performance Buddy OS` in prose, headings, interface copy, and product references;
- `Performance-Buddy-OS` for a hyphenated repository/root identifier where spaces are undesirable;
- `PERFORMANCE-BUDDY-OS-BLUEPRINT.md` for the locked root blueprint filename.

## Historical name

**Personal Performance OS** is superseded. It may appear only when identifying Candidate v0.1, preserving historical quotations, or documenting migration. New files and product copy must not use it as the current name.

## Naming rationale

“Performance Buddy” emphasizes an assisting relationship rather than surveillance or autonomous control. “OS” expresses the cross-domain control-layer metaphor: goals, daily action, evidence, review, and adjustment. It does not claim to be a computer operating system.

## Tone implications

The name commits the product to calm, supportive, explainable interaction. “Buddy” must not lead to false personhood claims, manipulative emotional language, or concealment of automated limitations.

## Revision status

Naming is `APPROVED`, not provisional. A future rename requires an approved decision, baseline/path impact review, migration plan, update to terminology and branding owners, and a documentation change-log entry. Implementation convenience or stylistic preference alone is insufficient.

## Acceptance criteria

All current authoritative documents use the canonical name, historical references are clearly marked, and no alternate short name becomes authoritative without approval.
