# Target User & Personal-First Strategy

## Purpose

Define the founding user and explain how personal-first scope improves usefulness while preserving a future path beyond one user.

## Founding user

The primary user is the project owner: a BS Artificial Intelligence student at COMSATS University Islamabad pursuing sustained academic, technical, physical, spiritual, linguistic, and personal development. Personal academic records and exact university policies remain provenance-controlled; this foundation does not invent them.

The user values local ownership, deep desktop workflows, Obsidian-based knowledge, understandable AI assistance, and gradual independent skill development. The product should support normal semesters, examination pressure, recovery, and changing daily conditions rather than assuming an ideal routine.

## Personal-first strategy

Personal-first means:

- solve concrete recurring problems for one real user;
- support configuration where behavior is personal rather than universal;
- avoid accounts, tenancy, organization roles, billing, and public collaboration in initial scope;
- keep private data local by default;
- prefer direct useful workflows over generic customization platforms;
- test ideas against actual use and evidence before broadening them.

## What may be generalized

Reusable concepts—goals, systems, actions, plans, sessions, evidence, trackers, provenance, approvals, and capability boundaries—should be modeled cleanly. Generalization must not introduce multi-user infrastructure before a validated need.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Personal-first is not permission to ignore accessibility, clarity, recovery, or data portability. The owner’s evolving abilities, workload, devices, and preferences require flexible interaction and configuration.

## Future boundary

Distribution or multi-user productization belongs to Phase 47 and `FUTURE`. Any move beyond personal-first requires explicit risk, privacy, identity, synchronization, support, and migration analysis.
