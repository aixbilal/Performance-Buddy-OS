# Jobs To Be Done

## Purpose

Describe the outcomes the user hires Performance Buddy OS to achieve, independent of specific screens or technology choices.

## Primary job

When life contains competing academic, development, fitness, recovery, spiritual, language, and personal demands, help me form a realistic plan, act on it, understand what actually happened, and decide what deserves attention next.

## Functional jobs

- Translate goals into systems, milestones, and executable actions.
- Build and revise a day around fixed commitments and protected constraints.
- Capture sessions, results, marks, routines, expenses, and relevant evidence with low friction.
- Reconcile university exposure and completed work with later study requirements.
- Track academic progress using authoritative rules and deterministic calculations.
- Connect learning evidence and Obsidian knowledge without replacing the vault.
- Maintain development, fitness, recovery, prayer, routines, reading, and practical language learning alongside academics.
- Review trends and friction without flattening domains into one score.
- Receive explainable assistance while retaining approval over meaningful changes.

## Emotional jobs

- Reduce uncertainty about what to do next.
- Replace guilt after plan changes with honest reconciliation.
- Build trust through visible reasons and source provenance.
- Feel progress accumulating without dependence on perfect streaks.
- Preserve autonomy rather than surrendering judgment to AI.

## Social and learning jobs

- Demonstrate a serious portfolio-quality system and architecture.
- Become a stronger developer by understanding the implementation.
- Support independent study when a physical study partner is unavailable.

## Job acceptance test

The system succeeds when it improves decisions and follow-through with less mental coordination cost. More logging is not success if it does not improve action, understanding, or adjustment.
