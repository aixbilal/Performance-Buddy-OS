# Today Experience

## Purpose

Define the operational experience for orienting, acting, capturing, reconciling, and closing the current day.

## Questions Today answers

- What is fixed or protected today?
- What currently deserves attention and why?
- What is planned, active, completed, deferred, or unresolved?
- What changed since the plan was formed?
- What evidence has been captured?
- What decision is needed next?

## Experience zones

Current context includes date, mode, available time, fixed commitments, protected constraints, and scoped status. The living plan shows candidate order/time without pretending certainty. A next-action area exposes deterministic reason codes. Quick capture/start links to owner workflows. Reconciliation and closure remain visible when relevant.

## Rules

Today is a projection of domain records, not their owner. It references goals, courses, sessions, workouts, routines, and other items using stable identities. No domain receives permanent dominance. Critical conflicts may override layout preference but use calm language.

## AI states

CORE works without AI. AI recommendations are labeled, show evidence and before/after effect, and remain separate from the confirmed plan until approved. Provider failure does not block Today.

## Empty and uncertainty states

“No plan,” “no available time,” “missing configuration,” “insufficient evidence,” and “optional service unavailable” are distinct. The product offers a safe planning or repair action without fabricating tasks.

## Acceptance criteria

Within a short scan the user can understand current constraints, choose a valid next step, and see whether a displayed item is confirmed, proposed, derived, or unknown.
