# Plan Change Explainability

## Purpose

Define the explanation record and user-facing presentation for every material plan change.

## Explanation contract

Each material change records:

- change ID, actor, time, and plan versions;
- trigger and source/provenance;
- before/after items, timing, scope, and state;
- dominant reason codes and constraints;
- alternatives considered where generated;
- missing inputs/uncertainty;
- deterministic validation result;
- AI capability/run reference if applicable;
- approval/override outcome.

## User-facing layers

Summary: “Reduced German to maintenance and moved development to Saturday.” Reason: “A new assignment is due tomorrow; protected sleep remains unchanged.” Detail: source deadline, available window, rule version, alternatives, and validation trace.

## Reason categories

New evidence, commitment/deadline, constraint protection, capacity change, dependency, priority/risk, completion elsewhere, user preference/override, mode change, and correction.

## Rules

Explanations do not expose hidden model reasoning or private context unnecessarily. They present verifiable inputs and concise rationale. A changed AI suggestion is not evidence by itself. Similar changes may be grouped only if individual effects remain inspectable.

## Dispute and correction

Users can correct source data, override the change, or restore/recreate a prior feasible version. Correction triggers revalidation rather than editing the explanation history.

## Acceptance criteria

Users can answer what changed, why, based on what, under which rules, who approved it, and how to correct or reverse it.
