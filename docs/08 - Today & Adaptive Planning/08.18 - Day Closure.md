# Day Closure

## Purpose

Define a truthful end-of-day workflow that preserves evidence, unresolved work, reflection, and next-day inputs.

## Closure inputs

Plan versions, actual completion states, sessions, evidence, unplanned work, unresolved conflicts, commitments, and optional reflection.

## Workflow

1. Resolve active sessions or explicitly carry their state.
2. Review planned versus actual without moral judgment.
3. Confirm/correct evidence and completion states.
4. Decide each unfinished item: defer, reschedule candidate, skip, excuse, maintenance, or retain in backlog.
5. Record key friction, wins, and changed assumptions if desired.
6. Generate next-day candidates without confirming tomorrow automatically.
7. Close with a versioned snapshot.

## Rules

Closure should be brief by default and deeper only when useful. Missing data remains unknown. Items are not auto-completed. Carry-forward does not preserve priority blindly. Private reflection is permission-protected and optional.

## Late closure

A day may be closed later with original local-time context. Reopening/correction records reason and preserves the prior closure. Active midnight-crossing work is handled explicitly.

## Analytics boundary

Closure produces records; analytics later derives trends. AI may summarize but cannot convert reflections into facts or judgments.

## Acceptance criteria

Closed days remain auditable, unfinished work has explicit disposition, and tomorrow begins from honest evidence rather than accumulated hidden backlog.
