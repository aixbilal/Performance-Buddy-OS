# Terminal Mode

## Purpose

Define the high-focus policy for terminal/final examination preparation and recovery-safe workload.

## Activation

Terminal Mode is a temporary mode tied to confirmed terminal/final examination schedules or explicit user activation. It is more restrictive than Midterm Mode but not permission to abandon safety or sleep.

## Policy direction

- Prioritize examinations by date, consequence, prerequisite risk, current evidence, and remaining coverage.
- Emphasize active recall, problem solving, mock/past-paper practice, and mistake correction.
- Reconcile university revision and completed topics to avoid duplicate effort.
- Defer low-consequence optional projects where necessary.
- Place development, language, and secondary skills in maintenance/pause by explicit plan.
- Use recovery sessions, breaks, meals, hydration, prayer, and protected sleep as feasibility inputs.

## Infeasibility

When remaining demand exceeds safe capacity, the system states the gap and offers scope/risk alternatives. It does not fabricate a schedule that fits by deleting buffers or sleep.

## AI boundary

AI may tutor, generate grounded tests, and propose allocation. Academic calculations and source truth remain deterministic. Recommendations cannot claim guaranteed grades.

## Exit and recovery

After the final examination, plan a transition/recovery review before restoring deferred load. Do not immediately schedule accumulated backlog as one day.

## Acceptance criteria

The mode is exam-focused, evidence-driven, source-grounded, transparent about risk, and safe under protected constraints.
