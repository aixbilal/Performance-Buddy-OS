# University Day Reconciliation

## Purpose

Define how classes, lectures, labs, assessments, travel, and new academic information update the remaining day.

## Inputs

Attendance/exposure, topics covered, lecture/lab work, assignments, deadlines, marks, quizzes/tests, travel delay, fatigue/context, and tasks completed at university. Sources and timestamps remain explicit.

## Workflow

1. Capture only essential university facts quickly.
2. Match exposure/work to existing courses, topics, and plan items.
3. Determine whether requirements are satisfied, partially satisfied, or newly created.
4. Update remaining academic demand through owner rules.
5. Recompute available time and conflicts.
6. Present minimal evening changes for confirmation.

## Examples

A class problem session may satisfy part of planned practice if evidence rules allow. A new assignment creates demand and deadline but does not automatically displace tonight’s protected sleep. A delayed commute reduces capacity rather than compressing every task.

## Authority

User capture is a personal record; official materials/policies retain their source authority. AI extraction from notes or screenshots creates a draft for review.

## Duplication control

One university activity can support multiple evidence relationships but is not double-counted as separate time. Topic exposure is not necessarily mastery.

## Acceptance criteria

Reconciliation prevents duplicate study, captures new obligations, preserves original plan/history, and produces an explainable feasible remainder.
