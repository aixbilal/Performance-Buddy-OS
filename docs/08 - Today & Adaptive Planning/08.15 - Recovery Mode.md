# Recovery Mode

## Purpose

Define a temporary low-load policy that protects safety and supports a gradual return after fatigue, illness, overload, or disruption.

## Activation

Recovery Mode is user-activated or proposed from permitted evidence and confirmed by the user. The product does not diagnose illness or injury. Trigger reasons may include unusual fatigue, poor sleep, soreness, illness, emotional overload, or post-examination recovery.

## Policy direction

- Preserve essential fixed commitments only after feasibility review.
- Reduce duration, complexity, and number of optional tasks.
- Prefer rest, mobility/recovery, hydration/nutrition routines, and essential preparation.
- Convert appropriate development/language/routine work to maintenance or pause.
- Keep a small restart action only when it supports recovery and user preference.
- Suppress guilt-based backlog reminders.

## Safety

Serious or uncertain symptoms require appropriate qualified help outside the app. AI cannot diagnose, prescribe treatment, or override a user’s choice to stop.

## Exit

Return gradually based on user-confirmed condition and evidence. Do not restore all deferred tasks automatically. Review estimates and system design if overload caused the mode.

## Acceptance criteria

The mode reduces planned load, preserves privacy, clearly distinguishes maintenance/rest from failure, and prevents other domains from treating recovery as unused capacity.
