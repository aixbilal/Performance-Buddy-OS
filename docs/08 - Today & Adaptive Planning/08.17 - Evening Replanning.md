# Evening Replanning

## Purpose

Define a focused replanning workflow after the day has materially changed.

## Trigger

Evening replanning occurs when university outcomes, delays, unplanned work, energy/recovery, missed/extended sessions, new deadlines, or user preference make the current plan stale or infeasible.

## Workflow

1. Show remaining time and protected sleep boundary.
2. Reconcile completed/partial/unplanned work.
3. List unresolved fixed commitments and high-consequence items.
4. Identify movable, reducible, maintenance, and deferrable items.
5. Present a small set of feasible alternatives.
6. Confirm the selected plan and preserve reasons.

## Rules

Do not rebuild the entire day unnecessarily. Preserve active/completed/pinned items. Reduce optional scope before violating protection. Do not frame deferral as failure. A task requiring deep attention may be deferred instead of squeezed into an unusable fragment.

## AI role

AI may explain trade-offs or propose alternatives. CORE validates time, constraints, commitments, dependencies, and permissions. Approval applies to the resulting material changes.

## Failure states

If no feasible plan exists, show the conflict and require an explicit scope/constraint decision. The interface must not hide infeasibility behind overlapping items.

## Acceptance criteria

Replanning is quick, calm, constraint-aware, undoable/versioned, and produces a clear remainder without rewriting the day’s actual history.
