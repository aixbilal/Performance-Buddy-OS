# Fixed vs Flexible Commitments

## Purpose

Define commitment types and how they may be scheduled, moved, reduced, or deferred.

## Commitment types

| Type | Meaning | Change behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed | Time and obligation are not ordinarily movable | Requires explicit source correction/exception |
| Windowed | Must occur within a range | Can move inside range |
| Flexible-day | Required that day without exact time | Can reorder around constraints |
| Optional | Useful if capacity permits | First candidate for deferral |
| Maintenance | Minimum viable continuity action | Reduced scope is intentional |

Commitment type is separate from importance. A highly important study action may be flexible; a minor appointment may be fixed.

## Rules

Commitments record owner domain, source, duration/effort estimate, recurrence, deadline/window, dependency, and flexibility. Splitting is allowed only when the action supports it. Moving an item does not change its deadline or domain status.

## Conversion

Changing fixed/flexible type is consequential configuration and records reason/effective date. AI can propose but not convert silently. Repeated manual movement may suggest the original classification or estimate needs review.

## Conflicts

Fixed commitments reserve capacity first after protected constraints. When fixed demand exceeds available time, the product explains infeasibility; it does not compress every flexible item into unsafe fragments.

## Acceptance criteria

Scheduling and UI consistently distinguish type, window, deadline, and priority; all changes preserve provenance and are validated.
