# Midterm Mode

## Purpose

Define a temporary examination-focused policy that increases academic preparation while preserving essential constraints and maintenance.

## Activation

Midterm Mode may be scheduled from confirmed examination dates or activated manually. Exact start/end and affected courses are explicit. Unverified dates cannot silently trigger it.

## Policy direction

- Elevate imminent examined courses using risk, coverage, assessment weight, and evidence.
- Increase revision, retrieval practice, problem solving, and source-grounded tests.
- Reduce or defer optional work before protected sleep/recovery.
- Keep development/secondary skills at deliberate maintenance if feasible.
- Maintain prayer, essential routines, nutrition/hydration, and safe movement/recovery.
- Preserve buffers and avoid unrealistic all-day planning.

## Academic authority

Assessment weights, coverage, and schedules use official/user-confirmed sources. AI cannot invent likely topics as official facts. Past-paper frequency is labeled evidence/inference.

## Daily reconciliation

Study completed at university or through assessments updates remaining demand. Weakness evidence may reallocate future blocks after validation and user confirmation.

## Exit

Mode ends by configured date or explicit action. Deferred items receive review rather than automatic overload on the next day.

## Acceptance criteria

Plans focus academic preparation without sacrificing protected constraints, and maintenance states remain honest rather than counted as full progression.
