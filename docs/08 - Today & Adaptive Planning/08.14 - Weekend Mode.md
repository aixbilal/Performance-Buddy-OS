# Weekend Mode

## Purpose

Define a configurable weekend policy for deeper work, catch-up, review, leisure, and recovery.

## Principle

Weekend Mode recognizes different availability without treating weekends as unlimited catch-up capacity. It balances deeper blocks, weekly review, recovery, social/leisure time, and preparation for the next week.

## Candidate structure

- Weekly review and next-week orientation.
- One or more deep academic/development blocks where feasible.
- Longer fitness session or recovery depending on load.
- Routine, reading, and language practice.
- Deliberate leisure and unallocated buffer.
- Deferred work only after re-evaluating relevance and capacity.

## Configuration

Weekend days, work windows, family/social commitments, prayer, sleep, exercise, and preferred review timing are personal configuration. University classes or examinations override the generic weekend assumption.

## Carry-forward

Weekday incompletion does not automatically consume the weekend. Items are reviewed by consequence, deadline, goal alignment, and remaining need. Some may be deferred, reduced, or dropped with reason.

## Mode interaction

Midterm/Terminal/Recovery Mode may combine with a weekend calendar context. The more specific safety/exam policies apply without duplicating modes as competing owners.

## Acceptance criteria

Weekend plans remain feasible, include restoration/buffer, and avoid using free time as evidence of unlimited capacity.
