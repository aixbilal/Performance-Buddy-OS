# Priority Calculation Concepts

## Purpose

Define transparent inputs and ordering principles for deterministic priority without prematurely locking a scoring formula.

## Status

Concepts are approved. Exact weights, normalization, tie-breaking formula, and thresholds are `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED` until representative data and Phase 23 tests support approval.

## Candidate factors

Protected/safety state, fixed deadline, consequence, prerequisite impact, academic risk, user importance, goal alignment, dependency readiness, overdue duration, opportunity/window, expected effort, recent attention, evidence of trajectory, recovery/load compatibility, and mode.

## Ordering principles

Hard constraints filter before scoring. Fixed commitments reserve time. High-consequence urgent work competes within feasible windows. Long-term systems receive continuity protection, but no rigid domain always wins. Secondary skills may rise when core areas are on trajectory.

## Anti-bias rules

Do not reward items merely for having more data. Missing records are unknown. Do not use money, prayer, or personal reflection to form a moral total score. Avoid feedback loops where past selection permanently suppresses another domain.

## Output

Provide rank/band, dominant reasons, relevant constraints, ties/alternatives, missing inputs, and what would change the result. False precision is avoided.

## Overrides

The user may pin or reprioritize with a reason. Overrides are configuration/evidence, not proof the formula is wrong; repeated patterns become review inputs.

## Acceptance criteria

Priority is reproducible, explainable, stable under small irrelevant changes, and validated against protected constraints before scheduling.
