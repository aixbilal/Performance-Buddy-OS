# Assignment & Deadline Capture

## Purpose

This workflow records newly announced work quickly while preserving source, uncertainty, and planning authority.

## Capture contract

Record course, item type, title or raw announcement, due date/time and timezone if known, source class/reference, announced and captured times, assessment relationship, estimated effort if explicitly entered, attachments or links, confidence, and verification state.

An ambiguous phrase such as “next week” is preserved verbatim and remains unresolved until anchored to a sourced date. OCR, voice, messages, or AI create reviewable candidates. Duplicate detection compares course, item, date, source, and existing revisions without silently merging.

## Planning boundary

A confirmed deadline creates academic demand but does not automatically displace fixed commitments, protected sleep, or existing plan items. Phase 08/14 produces an explainable proposal after reconciliation.

## Offline behavior

Draft and confirmed captures save locally with pending sync state. Later source correction creates a revision and triggers impact review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unresolved dates cannot masquerade as exact deadlines.
- Every confirmed deadline retains provenance and timezone.
- Capture never silently replans the day.
