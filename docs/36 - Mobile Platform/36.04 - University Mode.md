# University Mode

## Purpose

Mobile University Mode supports the on-campus day with verified schedule context, low-friction capture, and later reconciliation. It is the companion surface for the CORE University Mode defined in Phase 14.

## Surface

Show today's sourced classes, labs, assessments, travel buffers, deadlines, and compact course actions. Allow quick lecture logging, questions, assignments, marks, attendance where configured, and study-session start without exposing the full planning system.

## Rules

Offline capture is mandatory. Schedule items show source and as-of time; an observed cancellation or room change remains personal information until authoritative context confirms it. Class exposure does not imply mastery, and duplicate class/study time is prevented.

New work creates demand but does not silently replan the day, remove protected sleep, or override fixed commitments. At a suitable transition, captures are reconciled to courses/topics and candidate evening changes are sent to Phase 08.

## Privacy

Lock-screen and campus-visible screens minimize marks, personal routines, money, and private notes.

## Acceptance criteria

- The mode remains usable without network.
- Captures preserve course, source, time, and verification state.
- Reconciliation proposes explainable changes rather than causing schedule churn.
