# Expense Quick Capture

## Purpose

Expense Quick Capture records a personal transaction rapidly under the intentionally bounded Phase 21 money tracker. It is not accounting, banking, tax, investment, or financial-advice software.

## Record

Capture amount, currency, transaction time, category or `UNCLASSIFIED`, payment method if configured, note, receipt reference, recurring candidate flag, actor, provenance, and revision. Amount and currency are required before confirmation; a suggested category remains a suggestion.

OCR, receipt scan, notification reading, or AI extraction creates a draft and requires permission and review. The application does not access bank accounts or infer a transaction from location. Duplicate detection warns using amount, time, merchant text, and source without silently deleting records.

## Privacy and offline behavior

Expenses save locally, remain excluded from performance scoring, and use sensitive preview controls. Sync follows the user's data-zone and cloud consent.

## Acceptance criteria

- Acknowledged capture survives offline and restart.
- Currency and amount ambiguity blocks confirmation.
- Suggestions cannot become financial advice or automatic budget enforcement.
