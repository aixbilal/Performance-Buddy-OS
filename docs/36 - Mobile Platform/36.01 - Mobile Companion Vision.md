# Mobile Companion Vision

## Vision

The mobile companion helps the user record reality at the moment it happens and review what matters next without opening the full desktop command center. It should feel calm, fast, dependable, private, and useful under intermittent connectivity.

## Primary value

Mobile prioritizes Today, Capture, Review, Search, notifications, and compact university workflows. It supports quick study sessions, lecture logs, marks, assignments and deadlines, routines, expenses, and short tests. Exact navigation labels remain subject to usability validation.

## Relationship to desktop

Desktop remains the primary surface for complex configuration, deep analysis, architecture administration, bulk editing, and advanced planning. Mobile may expose shared CORE commands, but its `MOBILE` surface cannot redefine domain behavior or silently take ownership.

## Experience principles

- Reach frequent capture in minimal steps.
- Confirm local durability separately from synchronization.
- Preserve interrupted drafts and provide quick correction or undo.
- Show only context useful on a small screen.
- Degrade optional AI, cloud, and remote-source features without blocking local capture.

## Acceptance criteria

- Representative common captures complete quickly and accessibly.
- The companion is understandable without cloud or AI availability.
- No mobile-only behavior creates conflicting domain meaning.
