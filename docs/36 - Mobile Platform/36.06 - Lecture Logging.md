# Lecture Logging

## Scope

Lecture logging records what occurred in a class or lab without claiming that exposure equals understanding. It supports later knowledge linking, academic planning, and reconciliation.

## Record

A log references course, scheduled occurrence where known, actual start/end or attendance context, lecturer/source if configured, covered topics, materials, assignments announced, questions, personal notes, event time, capture time, source class, and verification state.

Topic suggestions from timetable, prior lecture, OCR, voice, or AI remain editable candidates. Original captured text or media is retained only with permission and policy. A cancelled, moved, or substituted class is recorded as an observation until an authoritative source confirms schedule change.

## Offline and duplication

Logs commit locally. Reconciliation detects the same scheduled occurrence, overlapping capture, and multiple-device revisions without discarding either. Assignment and deadline candidates route to their owning workflow for confirmation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Lecture exposure cannot automatically raise knowledge confidence to mastery.
- Logs remain traceable to occurrence and capture provenance.
- Duplicate and conflicting logs are surfaced safely.
