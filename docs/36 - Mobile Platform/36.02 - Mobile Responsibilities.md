# Mobile Responsibilities

## Owned responsibilities

The mobile platform owns application lifecycle, mobile navigation, device-secure local storage, capture presentation, camera/microphone/file permission prompts, notification and deep-link handling, background-transfer integration, biometric re-entry where configured, and platform accessibility support.

## Delegated responsibilities

Academic, session, routine, testing, and money phases own records and validation. Phase 32 owns schemas, Phase 33 offline guarantees, Phase 34 device and sync behavior, and Phase 30 AI/cloud permission. Mobile adapters invoke these shared contracts.

## Prohibited responsibilities

Views, widgets, notification actions, background handlers, OCR, and AI cannot write around application commands, promote drafts without review, change protected schedules, or invent source authority. Device names and biometrics do not replace account/device authorization.

## Lifecycle obligations

Startup verifies local schema, keys, migrations, and pending recovery before writes. Background suspension preserves acknowledged local changes. Sign-out and device revocation explain which local records remain and which remote operations stop.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every entry point maps to an authorized shared command.
- Permission denial has a usable non-destructive fallback.
- Platform failures cannot silently lose an acknowledged capture.
