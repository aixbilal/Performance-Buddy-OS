# Routine Quick Capture

## Scope

Routine Quick Capture exposes configured Phase 10/20 tracker actions such as prayer checks, hydration, nutrition habits, skincare, smoking avoidance, reading, language practice, and reflection without redefining their semantics.

## Interaction

The surface shows only due or favorited actions and their actual tracker type: boolean, count, duration, numeric target, multi-check, milestone, or reflection. One-tap input is allowed only when its meaning is unambiguous. Partial, rest, excused, skipped, and complete remain distinct.

Defaults cannot fabricate completion. Repeated taps are idempotent or explicitly additive according to tracker definition. Corrections and undo preserve revision history. Language practice may record duration and skill areas, but time alone is not proof of communication ability.

## Privacy and interruption

Private routine categories and values are hidden from widgets, lock screens, and notifications by default. Offline writes are locally durable and sync only within the configured data zone and consent.

## Acceptance criteria

- Mobile state matches the canonical tracker definition.
- Multi-check and partial states cannot collapse into false completion.
- Private routine data is minimized outside the authenticated app.
