# Spiritual & Routine Objectives

## Purpose

Define the role of spiritual practice and personal routines without turning private life into a punitive score.

## User-confirmed direction

The operating model supports the five daily prayers and simple personal routines such as hydration, nutrition habits, skincare, smoking avoidance, reading, language practice, and reflection.

## Objectives

- Represent prayer through appropriate multi-check tracking while respecting privacy.
- Make routine capture quick and proportionate.
- Support boolean, count, duration, numeric, multi-check, partial, excused, maintenance, and reflection states where appropriate.
- Distinguish essential, important, and flexible routines.
- Allow routines to adapt during university days, examinations, illness/recovery, travel, or unusual conditions.
- Use calm review language without shame or moral judgment.

## Domain integrity

Spiritual practice must not be reduced to a generic productivity score. Private routine data remains permission-scoped. Money stays separate from overall performance scoring. Completion is evidence of a recorded action, not proof of character, intention, health, or spiritual quality.

## Planning relationship

Fixed or time-sensitive practices may constrain the schedule. Flexible routines can move or enter maintenance mode. Repeated friction should prompt system redesign, not harsher reminders by default.

## Ownership

Phase 20 owns detailed routines, including dedicated language learning at `20.09`. Phase 10 owns tracker mechanics. AI may assist with review or proposals but cannot moralize, infer sensitive meaning, or modify routines without approval.
