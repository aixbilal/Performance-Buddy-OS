# Consistency Over Perfection

## Purpose

Operationalize sustainable repetition, honest completion states, and recovery from disruption without fragile streak logic.

## Principle

Consistency means returning to useful systems often enough for progress to accumulate. It does not mean perfect daily completion or protecting a streak at the expense of honesty, sleep, safety, or important context.

## Required states

The product should distinguish complete, partial, skipped, deferred, excused, rest, maintenance, and not-applicable states where appropriate. Each state preserves what actually happened. A minimum session may count as maintenance without pretending full progression occurred.

## Recovery protocol

After disruption:

1. Record reality without retroactive checkmarks.
2. Identify whether the cause was estimation, friction, capacity, priority, interruption, or system design.
3. Protect fixed and essential constraints.
4. Choose the smallest useful restart.
5. Rebuild normal load based on evidence.

## Metrics

Use rolling consistency, completion-state distribution, return time after interruption, planned-versus-actual patterns, and evidence quality. Streaks may be displayed only with clear excused/rest policy and must not dominate review language.

## AI boundary

AI may observe patterns and propose adjustments. It must not shame, diagnose motivation, or equate missing records with failure. Unlogged activity remains unknown, not automatically incomplete.

## Acceptance criteria

The system rewards truthful records and sustainable return, supports examination/recovery maintenance, and never encourages harmful action merely to preserve a metric.
