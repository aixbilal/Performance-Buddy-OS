# Fitness & Endurance Objectives

## Purpose

Record personal physical-development direction with progressive, evidence-aware, and safety-bounded behavior.

## User-confirmed direction

The user intends to improve endurance, running capability, strength, calisthenics, mobility, and wrist/forearm/hand capacity. A long-horizon aspiration discussed is the ability to complete a run around 20 km and perform multiple push-up or calisthenics variations.

These are personal objectives, not medical advice or a prescribed training plan.

## Operating objectives

- Build gradually through repeatable sessions and progressive improvement.
- Represent training type, duration/load, perceived difficulty, completion, and recovery signals proportionately.
- Include strength, running, calisthenics, explosive work, mobility, stretching, and targeted hand/forearm work as configurable categories.
- Schedule rest and recovery as valid states.
- Avoid using simple minutes or repetitions as the only performance evidence.
- Adjust training around academic load, sleep, soreness, and recent intensity.

## Safety boundary

The product does not diagnose injury, prescribe medical treatment, or encourage progression through warning signs. The user can stop, reduce, or seek qualified guidance. AI recommendations cannot override safety rules or present uncertain health interpretation as fact.

## Evidence

Progress may include consistency, safe load progression, distance/pace trends in context, strength or skill milestones, mobility practice, and recovery response. Comparisons remain personal and longitudinal rather than punitive.

## Ownership

Phase 19 defines fitness/recovery behavior and safety details. CORE records and validates; later AI may propose explainable adjustments subject to human approval.
