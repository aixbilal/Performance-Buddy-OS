# Academic Objectives

## Purpose

Record the founding academic direction while reserving official policy and calculations for authoritative academic phases.

## User-confirmed direction

The user intends to improve academic consistency and outcomes across the remaining BS Artificial Intelligence semesters, with a long-term aspiration toward a strong graduation CGPA, discussed as approximately 3.5+ if realistically achievable.

This is an objective, not a guaranteed projection. Current records, applicable curriculum, assessment weights, grading thresholds, prerequisite rules, and repeat/grade-replacement policies must come from official sources and imported personal records.

## Operating objectives

- Attend and capture relevant university exposure consistently.
- Replace last-minute preparation with repeatable study systems.
- Translate courses into topics, assessments, deadlines, and study actions.
- Reconcile class/assignment work with the remaining study requirement.
- Use evidence of recall, problem solving, assessments, and tests—not time alone—to judge understanding.
- Detect prerequisite, grade, and examination risk early.
- Preserve independent problem-solving and AI-off practice.

## Modes

Normal semester, midterm, terminal/final examination, weekend, and recovery modes may change allocation. Mode changes do not alter official academic rules or historical evidence.

## Authority boundaries

Phases 12–14 own academic models and planning; Phase 13 performs deterministic mathematics; official CUI sources outrank AI reports. AI can tutor, explain, generate grounded practice, or propose allocation, but cannot invent policy or calculate official results independently.

## Evidence of progress

Course coverage, practice quality, test attempts, assessment results, error patterns, and trajectory are stronger evidence than logged hours alone.
