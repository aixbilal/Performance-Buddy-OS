# 32.06 — Plans and Plan Items Schema

## Entities

`Plan`: date/range, timezone, mode, status, generation source, constraint/rule manifest, revision. `PlanItem`: plan ID, action/session/reference, title snapshot for display, scheduled start/end or flexible window, duration, fixed/flexible, priority band/reasons, status, sequence, dependencies, and source. `PlanChange`: before/after references, reason codes, actor, approval, and outcome.

## Invariants

Intervals use absolute instants plus scheduling timezone. Overlap and capacity follow Phase 23. Fixed items cannot move through ordinary flexible commands. Display snapshots never replace referenced owner facts. Replanning creates revisions/change records; completed historical plans remain reproducible.

## Acceptance criteria

- Midnight, timezone, overlap, stale-revision, and recurring-source cases pass.
- Every removed/displaced item is represented in a change record.
- AI proposals remain separate from committed plan revisions.
