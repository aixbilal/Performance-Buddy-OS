# 32.26 — Data Retention

## Policy dimensions

Retention is defined per entity/category, purpose, authority, privacy/data zone, legal/user requirement, backup/sync state, derived dependency, and deletion request. Exact durations are `RESEARCH REQUIRED` until approved privacy/security/legal requirements exist.

## Defaults

Canonical personal records persist until user deletion/archival policy; audit required for integrity uses minimum metadata; AI context/raw output is short-lived or not stored; derived indexes/caches are disposable; external packages/checkpoints expire; backups have independent rotation.

## Rules

Retention is not provider retention. Expiry jobs are deterministic, resumable, auditable, and permission-safe. Legal/security requirements may restrict immediate purge but must be disclosed. Derivatives cannot outlive their permitted source purpose silently.

## Acceptance criteria

- Policy inventory identifies owner, duration/event, action, exceptions, and verification.
- Expiry/purge tests cover caches, embeddings, logs, packages, checkpoints, sync, and backups.
- Users can inspect/export/delete eligible data without hidden indefinite copies.
