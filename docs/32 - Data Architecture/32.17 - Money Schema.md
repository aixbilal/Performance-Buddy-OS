# 32.17 — Money Schema

## Entities

`MoneyAccount` (optional logical bucket), `IncomeRecord`, `ExpenseRecord`, `MoneyCategory`, `BalanceSnapshot`, `SavingsGoal`, `SavingsContribution`, `RecurringExpenseRule`, and reconciliation records. Amounts use decimal minor-unit-safe representation plus ISO currency; original imported text is preserved.

## Invariants

Income/expense transactions are records, not bank execution. Currency conversion is explicit with rate/source/time; mixed currencies never sum silently. Balance is derived/reconciled and cannot invent opening values. Corrections preserve history. Transfers avoid double-counting when supported.

Money data is private, excluded from overall performance scoring, and inaccessible to AI without task-specific permission. Baseline performs no financial transactions or regulated recommendations.

## Acceptance criteria

- Decimal, sign, currency, recurrence, duplicate import, transfer, and reconciliation fixtures pass.
- Savings progress traces to contributions/balances and rules.
- Deletion/restoration preserves audit and recalculates derived totals.
