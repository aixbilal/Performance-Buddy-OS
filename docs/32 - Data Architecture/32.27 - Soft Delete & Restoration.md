# 32.27 — Soft Delete and Restoration

## Delete model

Canonical records normally enter a tombstoned soft-delete state with deleted time/actor/reason, prior revision, retention/purge deadline, and dependency summary. High-sensitivity or legally required erasure may use verified hard deletion under policy.

## Rules

Delete commands preview dependents and choose restrict, detach, archive, cascade, or invalidate per relationship. Deleted records disappear from ordinary queries, AI context, schedules, and analytics but remain recoverable only to authorized users until purge. Restoration revalidates uniqueness, references, permissions, current schema, and conflicts; it creates a new revision/event.

Sync tombstones prevent resurrection by stale devices. Derived projections are invalidated/rebuilt. Backups follow their disclosed deletion lifecycle.

## Acceptance criteria

- Delete/restore/purge is idempotent and audited.
- Parent deletion never silently destroys unrelated evidence.
- Restore conflict and stale-device tests preserve integrity and user choice.
