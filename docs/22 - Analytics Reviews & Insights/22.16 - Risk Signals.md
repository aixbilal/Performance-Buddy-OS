# Risk Signals

## Purpose

Surface explainable conditions that merit review while preventing a signal from becoming diagnosis, certainty, or automatic intervention.

## Signal contract

Stable signal ID, owner domain, type, severity, evidence records, rule/threshold version, window, coverage, confidence, missing data, first/last detected time, current state, explanation, and safe review action.

## Examples

Academic deadline/prerequisite risk, plan infeasibility, protected sleep conflict, abrupt training-load change, recurring save failure, unresolved financial record discrepancy, or stale knowledge source. Health and financial signals retain their domain advice boundaries.

## Rules

Urgency matches consequence. Signals consolidate rather than escalate emotionally. False positives can be dismissed with reason. `V2` may propose interpretations but cannot alter the deterministic signal or perform medical/financial diagnosis.

## Acceptance criteria

Signals are testable, source-linked, dismissible/reviewable, uncertainty-aware, and unable to mutate records or plans without the proper owner and approval path.
