# Time Allocation Analysis

## Purpose

Explain how recorded time was planned and used across compatible activities while acknowledging that time is only one resource and not equivalent to value or progress.

## Measures

Planned duration, active session duration, fixed commitments, travel, breaks, rest, unplanned work, interruptions, unknown gaps, and domain/type distribution. Wall-clock and active time remain distinct.

## Rules

Overlaps are flagged and not double-counted. One activity linked to several goals/domains keeps one time record with several relationships. Missing time remains unknown. Background processes do not inflate active work. Private routines may be hidden or aggregated. Different weeks/modes are compared with context.

## Interpretation

Show allocation, variance, capacity, and trade-offs—not a productivity score. More time can coexist with weaker evidence; less time may reflect efficiency, maintenance, or constrained capacity.

## Acceptance criteria

Totals reconcile to intervals, overlap and timezone rules are versioned, source sessions are inspectable, and time never substitutes for domain completion or quality evidence.
