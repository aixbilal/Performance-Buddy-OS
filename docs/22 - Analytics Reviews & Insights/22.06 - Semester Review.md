# Semester Review

## Purpose

Review an academic term across official outcomes, study evidence, planning accuracy, workload, development continuity, fitness/recovery, and system changes.

## Inputs

Verified enrollment/attempts, Phase 13 marks and GPA classifications, topic coverage, study sessions/tests, academic risk history, plan modes, development load states, recovery context, and user decisions. Unverified university policy remains visible.

## Rules

Official records, local derivations, and scenarios are never mixed. Grades are outcomes with context, not intelligence labels. Study hours are not equated with learning. Cross-domain analysis asks how workload interacted with capacity but cannot infer causation from correlation.

## Outcomes

Preserve useful systems, change estimates, identify prerequisite or evidence gaps, revise learning paths, plan recovery/transition, and record open questions. The next semester plan is a separate approved action.

## Acceptance criteria

Every academic value cites source/classification, missing terms do not become zero, broader context is permission-aware, and review language remains factual and constructive.
