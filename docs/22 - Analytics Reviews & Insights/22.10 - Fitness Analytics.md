# Fitness Analytics

## Purpose

Describe training, recovery, and progress patterns without producing medical diagnoses, injury predictions, or automatic clearance.

## Metrics

Modality-specific frequency/volume; effort/intensity distribution; comparable performance changes; rest/recovery sessions; sleep context; soreness/fatigue self-reports; exercise variation; training-load projections; and missing/device-data coverage.

## Rules

Incompatible modalities are not collapsed into one score. Device/manual sources remain distinct. Sudden load change is a review signal, not proof of injury risk. Personal records require comparable conditions. Pain and safety events are not gamified or exposed unnecessarily.

## Boundary

Readiness and recovery summaries are non-medical cues owned by Phase 19. Analytics never diagnoses, prescribes rehabilitation, or recommends training through concerning symptoms.

## Acceptance criteria

Units and source devices are visible, provisional formulas are labeled, rest is legitimate, privacy is enforced, and safety guidance overrides analytic goals.
