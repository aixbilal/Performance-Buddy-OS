# Knowledge Analytics

## Purpose

Describe topic coverage, evidence quality, confidence, retrieval, mistakes, and knowledge gaps while honoring Knowledge OS authority.

## Metrics

Entities/topics by status; coverage stages; evidence type and freshness; confidence-band distribution with uncertainty; retrieval/practice frequency; contradictory evidence; mistake/weakness patterns; source coverage; and broken or stale references.

## Rules

Unknown confidence is not weak confidence. Exposure and duration remain separate from demonstrated evidence. AI-assisted and independent attempts are distinguishable. Confidence rules/versions and evidence coverage accompany aggregates. Private note content is not exposed through labels or snippets.

## Interpretation

Analytics may identify under-evidenced topics or stale sources, not declare that the user “does not know” something. Knowledge confidence remains owned by Phase 15.

## Acceptance criteria

Results open evidence and sources, definitions are compatible across comparisons, uncertainty is visible, and inferred gaps cannot silently change confidence or study plans.
