# Performance Review Language

## Purpose

Define calm, factual wording for analytics and reviews so insight supports agency rather than shame, fear, or exaggerated certainty.

## Required pattern

State the observation; name scope/window and source coverage; distinguish inference; state uncertainty/missing context; explain relevant consequence; offer bounded options; leave the choice with the user.

## Preferred language

Use “2 completed, 1 deferred, 1 unknown,” “the plan exceeded recorded capacity,” “evidence is limited,” and “consider reducing, moving, or retaining this item.” Avoid “failed again,” “lazy,” “bad spending,” “perfect week,” “you must,” or “the AI knows best.”

## Domain protections

Marks do not label intelligence. Prayer does not measure faith. Fitness signals do not diagnose. Money does not measure worth. Reflection is not psychological assessment. Rest, maintenance, partial, and excused are legitimate states.

## Acceptance criteria

Content tests detect guilt, certainty, moral ranking, and hidden causality; severity matches real consequence; alternatives and uncertainty are visible; and rejected suggestions are treated neutrally.
