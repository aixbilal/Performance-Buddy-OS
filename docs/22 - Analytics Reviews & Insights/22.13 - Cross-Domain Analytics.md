# Cross-Domain Analytics

## Purpose

Explore interactions among domains to support better questions and planning without claiming causation or producing a single ranking of life.

## Allowed examples

Compare planned/actual time, examination mode and development maintenance, training load and protected sleep, university days and evening capacity, or language maintenance during exams. Use only compatible or clearly separate measures.

## Rules

Every analysis names included domains, permission, window, source coverage, temporal alignment, confounders, and interpretation limits. Correlation is not causation. Prayer, reflection, smoking, detailed health-adjacent data, and money are excluded by default and cannot become composite score inputs.

## Output

Observation, possible explanations, missing evidence, alternative interpretations, and user-controlled next question. `V3` may propose a narrative, but deterministic views and source links remain available.

## Acceptance criteria

No universal score exists, private domains cannot leak, aggregates are not misleading, and users can inspect or remove each source domain independently.
