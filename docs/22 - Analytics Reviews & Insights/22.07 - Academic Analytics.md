# Academic Analytics

## Purpose

Describe academic structure, outcomes, evidence, risk, and planning patterns without redefining official policy or performing unsourced calculations.

## Metrics

Verified enrollment/attempt status; credits by category; assessment and grade trajectories; SGPA/CGPA outputs with classification; topic coverage; retrieval/problem evidence; study allocation; prerequisite risk; deadline/assessment completion; and projection accuracy where scenarios existed.

## Rules

Phase 12/13 facts and formulas remain authoritative. Practice scores stay separate from official marks. Unknown policy blocks affected metrics. Course comparisons disclose credits, assessment structure, sample size, and mode. No grade is presented as a judgment of ability.

## Capability

`CORE` renders deterministic views. `V2` may explain evidence-linked patterns, but cannot change risk, grades, or records.

## Acceptance criteria

Every metric links to course/attempt and policy version, scenarios are visually distinct, corrections recompute projections, and time cannot substitute for learning evidence.
