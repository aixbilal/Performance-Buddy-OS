# Money Analytics

## Purpose

Describe money records and trends inside the Money domain without becoming financial advice or a cross-domain performance judgment.

## Metrics

Confirmed income/expenses; pending/unknown records; category distribution; recurring/variable spending; derived balances; savings contributions/withdrawals; goal progress; refunds/disputes; record completeness; and compatible period comparisons.

## Rules

Currencies are not combined without sourced rates. Transfers cannot inflate income/spending. Derived balances are not bank-authoritative. Leisure is not automatically negative. One-time and seasonal costs remain distinguishable. Sensitive totals are hidden according to permission.

## Separation

Money analytics cannot feed a universal score or be normalized against study, prayer, fitness, language, or development. Cross-domain use is limited to explicit constraints/commitments and user-requested scoped analysis.

## Acceptance criteria

Every total reconciles to Phase 21 records, advice boundaries remain visible, comparisons disclose completeness, and AI narratives cannot execute or invent transactions.
