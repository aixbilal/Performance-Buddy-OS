# Analytics Philosophy

## Purpose

Use evidence to improve understanding and decisions without turning a personal operating system into surveillance, shame, or false optimization.

## Principles

Analytics describes records, uncertainty, and change. It helps the user ask better questions, spot friction, protect recovery, and decide what matters next. It does not define personal worth or demand constant upward trends.

## Authority

Owner domains publish facts. Analytics stores versioned projections and links back to inputs. A correction invalidates or recomputes dependents. Historical snapshots may remain for audit but are not current truth.

## Interpretation

Observation, inference, hypothesis, and suggestion are separate. Small samples, missing records, changed definitions, and confounders are disclosed. Personal baselines are preferred to social comparison.

## Capability

Descriptive calculations are `CORE`. `V2` may identify patterns; `V3` may reason across domains. AI never changes a metric, diagnoses a condition, or silently commits a plan.

## Acceptance criteria

Every result is reproducible and inspectable, uncertainty is visible, users can disable sensitive views, and no analytics language manipulates behavior through guilt or fear.
