# Weekly Review

## Purpose

Review a meaningful short-term pattern across plans, evidence, workload, recovery, and domain systems to support the next week’s choices.

## Sections

Completion-state distribution; planned versus actual allocation; fixed/unplanned load; consistency and return after disruption; academic risk/evidence; development path; fitness/recovery; routine maintenance; money summary only if explicitly included; friction; and unresolved data.

## Workflow

Confirm the window and completeness; inspect facts; identify changes and possible explanations; review protected constraints; choose continue, adjust, reduce, maintain, defer, or investigate; record decisions separately from analytics.

## Rules

Week comparisons require compatible definitions and coverage. One difficult week does not establish a trend. Rest and maintenance are visible, not counted as failure. Cross-domain totals do not become a score.

## Acceptance criteria

The review exposes source coverage and prior decisions, supports user edits/corrections, and produces bounded choices without automatically modifying next week.
