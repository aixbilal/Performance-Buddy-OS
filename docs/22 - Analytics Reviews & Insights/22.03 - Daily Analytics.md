# Daily Analytics

## Purpose

Provide a compact end-of-day view of planned versus actual activity, evidence, capacity, and unresolved items without judging one day as personal success or failure.

## Sections

Plan completion-state distribution; sessions and active time by compatible domain/type; unplanned work; fixed commitments; interruptions; tracker states; evidence captured; plan changes; protected sleep/recovery status; and unknown/unreconciled records.

## Rules

The original plan and actual record remain separate. Partial, deferred, skipped, excused, rest, maintenance, not-applicable, and unknown retain their meanings. Time is not converted into progress. Private prayer, reflection, money, and health-adjacent details are excluded or reduced according to permission.

## Output

Show factual summary, notable constraints, unresolved records, and one optional next review action. Daily values are too narrow for strong causal conclusions.

## Acceptance criteria

The view is reproducible by local date/timezone, late corrections update it, every number links to source records, and calm language avoids streak or completion pressure.
