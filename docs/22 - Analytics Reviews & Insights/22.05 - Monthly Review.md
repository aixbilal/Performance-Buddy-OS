# Monthly Review

## Purpose

Examine longer patterns, system effectiveness, goal/path movement, recurring friction, and capacity while preserving important context from weeks and modes.

## Sections

Goal/system reviews; time and completion distributions; learning evidence; academic trajectory; development milestones; fitness load/recovery; routine consistency; private-domain summaries by permission; money domain review; trend confidence; and major decisions or mode changes.

## Rules

Calendar month and rolling-window views are labeled separately. Definition changes and incomplete weeks are marked. Volume growth is not automatically improvement. Seasonal, exam, travel, illness, and recovery periods remain contextual annotations, not data to normalize away.

## Output

Observations, possible explanations, evidence gaps, user decisions, and next review triggers remain separate. Historical monthly reviews are immutable snapshots with revision links.

## Acceptance criteria

All comparisons use compatible windows and definitions, private domains remain partitioned, trends show uncertainty, and the review supports sustainable decisions rather than maximizing every metric.
