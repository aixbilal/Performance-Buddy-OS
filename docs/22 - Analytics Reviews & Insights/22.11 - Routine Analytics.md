# Routine Analytics

## Purpose

Support sustainable routine reflection while protecting spiritual meaning, private context, and legitimate flexibility.

## Metrics

Completion-state distribution, rolling consistency, maintenance/rest/excused proportions, return after disruption, session mix for reading/language, language skill-practice balance, and reminder/capture friction may be shown within approved scope.

## Rules

Prayer analytics is private, factual, and incapable of measuring intention, sincerity, acceptance, or worth. Reflection text is excluded unless explicitly included in a private review. Smoking lapses do not erase prior progress. Nutrition, skincare, and hydration remain health-adjacent and non-diagnostic. Missing is unknown.

## Language

For German/Italian, distinguish progression from 10–15 minute exam maintenance and show speaking/listening/vocabulary/grammar balance rather than one streak.

## Acceptance criteria

No routine contributes to a universal score, private reasons remain hidden, flexibility states are preserved, and reviews support restart without guilt.
