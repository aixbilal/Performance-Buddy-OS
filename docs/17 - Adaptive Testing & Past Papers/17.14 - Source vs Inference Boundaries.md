# Source vs Inference Boundaries

## Purpose

Make the authority level of every past-paper and adaptive-testing claim unmistakable.

## Classification

Source facts include visible question text, printed marks, paper date/label, and verified answer material. Extracted candidates are machine transcriptions pending review. Mapped topics, difficulty, error causes, weakness patterns, likely emphasis, and revision priority are inferences with evidence and uncertainty.

## Rules

AI output never becomes source fact through repetition. A past question proves historical appearance only within its verified context. Instructor hints, student recollections, and unofficial solutions retain their source class. Conflicts are preserved. Future exam content, grades, and success are never guaranteed.

## UX

Show labels, source links, corpus scope, confidence, and “why” details near the claim. Generated questions are visibly generated even when grounded. Corrections preserve the prior classification and actor.

## Acceptance criteria

Audits can trace every claim to source or inference, no inference is visually presented as official, and missing evidence produces an honest gap rather than a confident narrative.
