# Phase 17 — Adaptive Testing & Past Papers

## Purpose

Phase 17 defines practice-test generation, attempts, deterministic scoring, mistake and weakness analysis, knowledge feedback, and safe use of past papers. It improves study evidence without confusing practice with official assessment or historical frequency with future certainty.

## Document map

| Range | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| 17.01–17.05 | Adaptive testing and grounded generation |
| 17.06–17.10 | Attempts, scoring, errors, weakness, feedback |
| 17.11–17.14 | Past-paper storage, extraction, analysis, authority |
| 17.15 | Revision-priority proposals |

## Ownership boundaries

Knowledge OS owns topics and confidence. Phase 13 owns official assessments and marks. Phase 14 owns academic risk and study allocation. Obsidian Integration owns file access/indexing. Phase 17 owns practice-test and past-paper evidence contracts.

## Invariants

1. Generated questions cite permitted sources or are labeled ungrounded drafts.
2. Attempts are preserved before answers, hints, scoring, or explanations are revealed.
3. Practice scores never enter official grades.
4. Mistakes are contextual evidence, not labels of ability.
5. Past-paper occurrence is historical evidence, not guaranteed exam scope.
6. `CORE` supports manual question banks, attempts, and deterministic scoring; AI generation is `V1` and optional.
7. Revision priorities remain proposals validated against Phase 14 constraints.
