# Difficulty Selection

## Purpose

Select question challenge appropriate to the learning objective and available evidence without treating difficulty as a fixed property or a measure of intelligence.

## Inputs

Topic confidence and uncertainty, prerequisite evidence, recent independent attempts, error patterns, question complexity, scaffolding, novelty, time pressure, and test mode.

## Strategy

Diagnostic tests span levels. Practice normally mixes secure retrieval, productive challenge, and limited stretch. Repeated errors may trigger prerequisite or simpler diagnostic items rather than endless repetition. Strong evidence may introduce transfer, integration, or reduced scaffolding.

## Rules

Sparse evidence starts with a bounded mixed sample, not an assumed low level. Difficulty changes are incremental and explainable. Assistance and timing alter observed difficulty and remain in provenance. Exact thresholds and adaptation rates are `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED` pending Phase 23 evaluation.

## Acceptance criteria

Selections show reasons and uncertainty, avoid abrupt oscillation, maintain topic coverage, and let the user override or choose a fixed level without corrupting evidence.
