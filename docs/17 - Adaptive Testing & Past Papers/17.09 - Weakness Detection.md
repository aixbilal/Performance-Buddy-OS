# Weakness Detection

## Purpose

Identify evidence-supported, time-bounded areas needing attention from patterns across attempts and mistakes.

## Inputs

Independent attempt correctness, mistake categories, topic/question coverage, difficulty, assistance, recency, repeated consistency, assessment evidence, and contradictory successful evidence.

## Output

Use `Possible`, `Emerging`, `Supported`, or `Resolved/Improving` pattern states with topic, dimension, supporting and contradicting evidence, sample size, freshness, confidence, and next validation action. These are planning observations, not ability labels.

## Rules

One mistake rarely establishes a stable weakness. Sparse or biased question coverage produces uncertainty. Missing attempts do not imply weakness. Repeated items are not independent samples. Resolution requires new evidence; it is not achieved by deleting mistakes. Exact thresholds are `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED`.

## Acceptance criteria

Patterns are reproducible, explainable, sensitive to coverage and assistance, correctable, and unable to modify Knowledge confidence or study plans without their owner rules.
