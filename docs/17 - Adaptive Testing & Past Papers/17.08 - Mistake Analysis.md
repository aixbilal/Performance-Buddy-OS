# Mistake Analysis

## Purpose

Turn incorrect, incomplete, or inefficient responses into specific correction evidence without reducing the learner to a failure label.

## Categories

Concept gap, prerequisite gap, recall failure, procedure error, calculation error, interpretation error, incomplete reasoning, careless transcription, time-pressure issue, answer-format issue, source ambiguity, flawed question, and unknown are candidate categories.

## Workflow

Compare response, answer/rubric, working, source, and context; propose one or more categories; record evidence and uncertainty; let the user/reviewer correct classification; link a correction explanation and retest target.

## Rules

A wrong answer does not prove why it was wrong. AI classification is a proposal. Several errors from one question are not automatically independent weaknesses. Correct guessing and assisted success may also warrant review. Emotional or clinical diagnoses are prohibited.

## Acceptance criteria

Every classification cites response evidence, supports multiple/unknown causes, remains editable, and produces a bounded learning action rather than shame or endless repetition.
