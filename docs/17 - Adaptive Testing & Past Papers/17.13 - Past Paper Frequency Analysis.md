# Past Paper Frequency Analysis

## Purpose

Describe historical topic and question-pattern occurrence across an explicitly bounded past-paper corpus.

## Calculation contract

Declare corpus IDs, course/version matching, exam types, date range, included/excluded papers, extraction coverage, topic-mapping version, counting unit, duplicate policy, and calculation time. Report counts, paper coverage, recency distribution, and uncertainty.

## Rules

Frequency is historical evidence, not probability unless a separately validated statistical model supports that claim. Repeated variants within one paper are distinguished from occurrence across papers. Missing years and low-quality extraction are visible. Different syllabi/curriculum versions are not combined silently.

## Presentation

Use “appeared in 4 of 7 included papers” rather than “will appear.” Open every count to source questions. Avoid certainty language, leaked-paper framing, or guaranteed-topic lists.

## Acceptance criteria

Counts are reproducible, corpus bias and missing data are disclosed, duplicate sources cannot inflate frequency, and users can inspect the supporting questions.
