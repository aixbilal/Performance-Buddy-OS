# Revision Priority Generation

## Purpose

Generate explainable revision candidates from topic evidence, mistakes, weaknesses, assessment consequence, and past-paper patterns for Phase 14 to validate and allocate.

## Inputs

Knowledge confidence/uncertainty, repeated mistake evidence, prerequisite impact, verified exam scope/date, assessment weight, topic coverage, freshness, correction status, historical paper frequency with corpus limits, available source/practice materials, and recent attention.

## Rules

Hard constraints and final allocation remain Phase 14/08 responsibilities. Past-paper frequency is one bounded signal, never the sole reason. Sparse evidence cannot imply low priority. Candidates include active method and evidence target, not “review topic” alone. Recently intensive study may trigger spacing or correction rather than more volume.

## Output

Topic/activity, priority band, dominant reasons, contradicting evidence, uncertainty, suggested duration range, practice form, success evidence, and alternatives. Exact weights remain `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED`.

## Acceptance criteria

Candidates are reproducible, source-linked, non-duplicative, constraint-aware when validated, and never silently modify plans or promise examination outcomes.
