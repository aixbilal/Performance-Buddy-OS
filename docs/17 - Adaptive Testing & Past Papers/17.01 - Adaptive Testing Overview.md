# Adaptive Testing Overview

## Purpose

Adaptive testing selects bounded practice that produces useful retrieval and application evidence, then updates future practice proposals from preserved attempts.

## Cycle

Define scope and mode; select topics and difficulty; assemble or generate questions; freeze the test version; capture an independent attempt; score through deterministic or reviewed rules; analyze mistakes; publish evidence to Knowledge OS; and propose the next test or revision action.

## Test modes

Practice, diagnostic, timed simulation, topic review, mixed retrieval, correction retest, and AI-off modes are supported. Mode determines feedback timing and assistance, not official standing.

## Capability

Question-bank selection, attempt capture, objective scoring, and rule-based sequencing are `CORE`. `V1` may generate source-grounded questions or explanations. `V2` may propose adaptations from history. AI is never required to preserve or review attempts.

## Safety and integrity

The system does not claim exam security, proctoring, or guaranteed prediction. Assistance use is recorded by context, not policed beyond the application.

## Acceptance criteria

Every attempt links to an immutable test version, feedback timing is enforced, results remain practice evidence, and adaptive changes are explainable and reversible.
