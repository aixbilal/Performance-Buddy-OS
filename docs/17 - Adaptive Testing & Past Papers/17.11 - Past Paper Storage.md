# Past Paper Storage

## Purpose

Store governed references and metadata for past examination papers while respecting copyright, privacy, source authority, and external file ownership.

## Record contract

Course/version, exam type, term/date, campus/program context, source, official/unofficial status, file reference, checksum, pages, permissions, extraction state, verification, and duplicate relationships.

## Rules

Prefer references to user-owned or authorized files over unnecessary copies. Source and authenticity are explicit; filenames alone do not prove course or year. Duplicate checksums may share one content reference without deleting originals. Papers with answers/marking schemes link them as separate artifacts.

## Security

Attachments follow Phase 16 containment: no execution, type/size validation, local default, restricted-provider access, and redacted logs. Current confidential exam material is outside legitimate past-paper scope.

## Acceptance criteria

Every paper is traceable and permissioned, duplicates are controlled, removal creates a broken reference rather than silent substitution, and metadata remains searchable without claiming authenticity.
