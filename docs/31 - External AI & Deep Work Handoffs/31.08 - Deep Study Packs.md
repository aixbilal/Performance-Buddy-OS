# 31.08 — Deep Study Packs

## Purpose

Create a bounded, portable collection for extended study in an external tool or fully manual workflow.

## Contents

Manifest and version; course/path/topic and learning objectives; permitted source inventory with authority/revisions; selected notes/lecture materials; prerequisite map; coverage/confidence evidence; known mistakes; approved past-paper/question references; glossary; study constraints; desired modes (explain, quiz, critique, role-play); output schema; citation requirements; gaps and exclusions.

## Rules

Packs are snapshots, not live vault mirrors. They exclude answers where active-test policy requires separation, graded-work misuse, inaccessible copyright material, private unrelated data, and all never-AI/local-only content for external destinations. German/Italian packs may focus practical situations without exporting recordings unless separately approved.

Time spent with a pack is not proof of learning. Imported tests/notes remain drafts; attempts and evidence use owned Phase 15/17 workflows.

## Acceptance criteria

- Pack coverage and exclusions are visible and reproducible from source revisions.
- Local/manual study remains possible with the same pack contract.
- Deletion/revocation invalidates dependent packages and imported citations.
