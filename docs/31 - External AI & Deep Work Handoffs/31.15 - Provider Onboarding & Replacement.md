# 31.15 - Provider Onboarding & Replacement
## Workflow

Onboarding records purpose, owner, terms/privacy review, region/retention, capabilities, adapter contract, credentials, allowed classifications, budgets, health checks, structured-output tests, safety tests, and user disclosure. Credentials are entered through the secret boundary and validated without logging their value.

Replacement runs compatibility and regression evaluation, maps capabilities, stages routing, preserves agent identity/memory, and provides rollback. A provider is not production-eligible until permission, failure, quality, and fallback tests pass. Retirement revokes credentials, stops routing, handles provider-side deletion obligations, and preserves non-secret audit provenance.

## Acceptance criteria

- Provider changes require no persona or agent-memory migration.
- Failed onboarding leaves no active credential or route.
- Replacement cannot weaken privacy or validation silently.
