# Release Acceptance Checklist

## Required evidence

- Approved scope, change log, application/build/schema/protocol/capability versions, known issues, and support impact are complete.
- Source, dependencies, lockfiles, licenses, SBOM, provenance, checksums, signatures, notarization, and artifacts are verified.
- Unit, integration, E2E, offline, sync, academic, rules, AI, UX/accessibility, security, migration, backup/restore, and performance regression gates pass where applicable.
- Upgrade from every supported version, interruption, low-resource, repair, uninstall, rollback/forward recovery, and export portability are proven.
- Permissions, data zones, consent, local-only behavior, logging/redaction, telemetry, AI/cloud disclosures, and retention remain compliant.
- Release channel, staged rollout, health thresholds, kill switches, support/runbooks, incident response, and approvers are ready.

## Disposition

Results are pass, fail, blocked, not applicable, or waived. Flaky/skipped tests are not passes. Critical integrity, secret, permission, approval, unsafe-advice, signing, migration, or recovery failures block release. Waivers require owner, rationale, scope, compensation, expiry, and rollback and cannot bypass product invariants.

## Acceptance criteria

- The shipped digest maps to an immutable signed checklist and evidence snapshot.
- Every non-pass item has explicit authorized disposition.
- Release closure confirms rollout health and records follow-up actions.
