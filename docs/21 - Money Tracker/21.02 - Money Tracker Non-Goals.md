# Money Tracker Non-Goals

## Purpose

Prevent a lightweight tracker from being mistaken for financial infrastructure or professional advice.

## Explicit non-goals

The baseline is not a bank or wallet; payment or transfer service; double-entry accounting system; tax return or compliance engine; credit score/debt-optimization service; investment portfolio, trading, or crypto platform; lending or insurance tool; fraud detector; currency speculation service; or regulated financial adviser.

## Consequences

Displayed balances are only as complete and current as included records. They may not equal a bank’s available balance. The product cannot guarantee affordability, solvency, investment return, tax treatment, or eligibility for a financial product.

## Future scope

Any expansion requires explicit capability promotion, jurisdiction and regulatory review, security/threat modeling, source authority, auditability, and user approval. Marketing language cannot imply guarantees.

## Acceptance criteria

UI and documentation state the boundary near consequential features, no non-goal hides inside `CORE`, and users are directed to authoritative institutions or qualified professionals when appropriate.
