# Financial Advice Boundaries

## Purpose

Define the non-negotiable separation between personal recordkeeping/education and individualized regulated financial, investment, tax, legal, credit, or debt advice.

## Boundary

Performance Buddy OS may perform deterministic arithmetic, show user records, explain its formulas, and link general educational resources. It does not recommend specific securities, crypto assets, loans, insurance, tax positions, debt remedies, or account providers; predict markets; promise returns; or determine legal obligations.

## Escalation

High-stakes decisions, suspected fraud, insolvency, tax/legal questions, or complex debt/investment choices should use appropriate official sources and qualified professionals in the user’s jurisdiction. Never act on urgency or guaranteed-return claims. [Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/protect-your-investments/fraud/protect-your-money) identifies pressure and high-return/low-risk promises as fraud warning signs.

## AI boundary

AI output is non-authoritative, may be inaccurate, and must cite current sources for time-sensitive facts. It cannot access financial records without explicit task-specific permission or execute transactions.

## Acceptance criteria

Consequential surfaces display scope, uncertainty, and source; no recommendation sounds guaranteed or personalized beyond evidence; and transaction execution is absent from baseline.
