# Recurring Expenses

## Purpose

Represent expected repeating bills and subscriptions for reminders and cash-flow planning without fabricating posted transactions.

## Definition

Payee/label, expected amount or range, currency, recurrence, due window, category, account, start/end, variability, source, autopay flag as user information, and effective version.

## Rules

Generated occurrences are expectations, not expenses until linked to a posted/manual record. Amount changes preserve historical definitions. Matching uses date/amount/payee context and requires confirmation when ambiguous. Cancellation stops future expectations but does not erase history. Annual and irregular schedules remain explicit.

## Alerts

Reminders are discreet and permission-aware. A missed expected item may mean late, cancelled, changed, unrecorded, or unmatched; it is not automatically labeled unpaid.

## Acceptance criteria

Occurrence generation is idempotent, expected and actual remain distinct, matching prevents duplicates, and variable bills use ranges/uncertainty rather than false precision.
