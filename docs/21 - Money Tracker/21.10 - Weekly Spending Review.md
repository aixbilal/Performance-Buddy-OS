# Weekly Spending Review

## Purpose

Provide a short factual review of recent cash flow, record quality, and upcoming commitments without guilt-driven messaging.

## Review sections

Income received; expenses posted; transfers; savings contributions/withdrawals; category totals; pending/uncategorized/duplicate records; recurring expenses due soon; unusual changes requiring confirmation; and one optional next action.

## Rules

Compare only compatible periods/currencies and disclose incomplete data. “Unusual” means statistically or rule-different from the user’s history, not bad. Leisure spending remains legitimate. The review does not rank the week against academics, prayer, fitness, or productivity.

## Workflow

Confirm unresolved records, inspect surprises, correct categories, reconcile where possible, then choose no change or a bounded plan adjustment. AI suggestions are optional and approval-gated.

## Acceptance criteria

The review is completable offline, every total opens to source records, missing data remains visible, and language stays calm and non-moralizing.
