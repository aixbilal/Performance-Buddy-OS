# Savings

## Purpose

Represent money deliberately set aside while distinguishing a savings transfer, a savings-account balance, and a derived change in net available cash.

## Model

Savings contributions and withdrawals reference underlying transactions, goal allocations where used, account/container, date, currency, source, and revision. A single transfer cannot be counted as both spending and new aggregate wealth.

## Rules

Savings is not assumed to be risk-free, liquid, or interest-bearing; those properties require actual account context. Expected contributions remain projections. Interest, fees, and investment gains/losses are separate sourced transaction types. Savings progress is not a moral score.

## Review

Show contributions, withdrawals, net change, and goal allocation separately. Incomplete records display uncertainty. General budget guidance may treat savings as a planned item, but the user chooses priorities.

## Acceptance criteria

Savings totals reconcile to underlying records, transfers are deduplicated, goal allocation cannot exceed available tracked amount without warning, and historical contributions survive reclassification.
