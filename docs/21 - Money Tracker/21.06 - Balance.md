# Balance

## Purpose

Calculate transparent record-based balances for configured accounts or scopes without claiming the value equals a financial institution’s authoritative balance.

## General equation

For a defined scope and status policy, closing balance equals opening balance plus included inflows minus included outflows plus/minus included adjustments. Transfers affect account-level balances but cancel in an all-owned-accounts aggregate when both sides are present.

## Calculation contract

Store currency, opening source/date, included transaction IDs, statuses, conversion policy if any, decimal precision, formula version, calculation time, and unresolved differences. Currencies are never summed without an explicit sourced exchange rate and timestamp.

## Reconciliation

Compare a derived balance with a user/imported statement snapshot. Differences produce diagnostics for missing, duplicate, pending, or mistimed records; neither side is overwritten automatically.

## Acceptance criteria

Identical inputs reproduce the balance, transfers cannot inflate totals, multi-currency uncertainty is visible, and every discrepancy can be investigated record by record.
