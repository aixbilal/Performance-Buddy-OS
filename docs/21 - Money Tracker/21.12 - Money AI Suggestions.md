# Money AI Suggestions

## Purpose

Allow optional AI to explain record patterns and propose bounded tracking or budgeting actions without becoming a transaction authority or financial adviser.

## Permitted suggestions

Clarify uncategorized records, identify possible duplicates/subscriptions, summarize user-selected periods, propose a category or reminder, highlight an assumption, or draft questions for the user to consider.

## Required contract

Every suggestion states observed records, inference, uncertainty, excluded/missing data, proposed action, expected effect, and approval choices. Deterministic `CORE` tools calculate totals. No record is created, deleted, categorized, or scheduled without validation and appropriate confirmation.

## Prohibited suggestions

No guaranteed savings/returns, securities or crypto picks, tax/legal conclusions, credit/lending decisions, manipulative urgency, essential-needs cuts, or invented transactions. Official investor guidance warns against relying solely on AI-generated information for investment decisions: [Investor.gov alert](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/artificial-intelligence-fraud).

## Acceptance criteria

Provider failure leaves data unchanged, privacy scope is explicit, advice boundaries are visible, and rejected suggestions create no write.
