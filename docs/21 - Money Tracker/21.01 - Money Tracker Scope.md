# Money Tracker Scope

## Purpose

Define the committed personal money-tracking capability and its relationship to external accounts and broader financial decisions.

## In scope

Manual income/expense entry, currencies, categories, balances derived from included records, savings and savings goals, recurring expense reminders, weekly/monthly review, CSV-style import/export where later specified, correction/reconciliation, and bounded explanatory suggestions.

## Record principles

Each transaction has stable identity, amount, currency, direction, date, status, category, source, account/container reference when configured, note, privacy class, and revision history. Decimal arithmetic is mandatory. Source records are never rewritten by projections.

## Capability

Complete manual use and calculations are `CORE` and offline. Bank connections, cloud sync, receipt OCR, and provider enrichment are optional future capabilities requiring separate security and consent contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

Users can capture, correct, categorize, review, and export records locally; every total opens to included records; and provider failure cannot block the tracker.
