# Income

## Purpose

Record money received or expected while distinguishing confirmed receipts from estimates, transfers, refunds, and other non-income movements.

## Model

Amount and currency, received/expected date, payer/source label, category, status, recurrence reference, account/container, source class, verification, note, and revision. Sensitive payer and employment details are optional.

## States

`Expected`, `Pending`, `Received`, `Cancelled`, `Refund/Reversal`, and `Unknown` remain distinct. Only states allowed by the selected balance view contribute to totals, with reasons exposed.

## Rules

Gross and net income are not interchangeable. Transfers between owned accounts do not create income. Irregular income is not silently annualized or treated as guaranteed. Imported duplicates are quarantined. Tax or benefit implications are outside product scope.

## Acceptance criteria

Income totals are reproducible by period/status/currency, expected values remain projections, transfers cannot inflate income, and corrections preserve the original record.
