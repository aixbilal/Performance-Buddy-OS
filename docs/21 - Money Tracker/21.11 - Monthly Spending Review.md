# Monthly Spending Review

## Purpose

Summarize a full month’s income, spending, savings, recurring commitments, and goal progress to support the next user-owned plan.

## Review sections

Opening/closing derived balance by scope, confirmed income, posted expenses, category distribution, recurring versus variable spending, savings net change, goal progress, refunds/disputes, record completeness, and comparison with prior compatible months or a user plan.

## Rules

Seasonal and one-time costs are identified rather than treated as recurring trend. Comparisons use the same currency/status rules. A negative cash-flow result is factual context, not personal failure. The system never recommends skipping essential food, care, education, or obligations merely to improve a graph.

## Output

Record observations, user decisions, assumptions, and next-month plan separately. Historical reviews remain snapshots and corrections trigger a revised view rather than silent rewrite.

## Acceptance criteria

Results reconcile to transactions, comparisons expose definitions, one-time events remain distinguishable, and the review supports planning without pretending to be professional financial advice.
