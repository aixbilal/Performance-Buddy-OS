# Savings Goals

## Purpose

Support explicit saving purposes and progress scenarios without promising that a target is affordable or guaranteed.

## Model

Goal name/purpose, target amount/currency, target date if chosen, starting allocation, contribution records, priority, state, privacy, assumptions, and effective version. States include `Active`, `Maintenance`, `Paused`, `Achieved`, `Retired`, and `Cancelled`.

## Progress

Actual allocated savings is evidence; planned contributions and forecast interest are scenarios. Required periodic contribution is a deterministic division/scenario with date and assumptions, not advice. Withdrawal or correction recomputes progress while preserving history.

## Rules

One amount cannot be allocated to several goals unless the UI clearly presents a shared/virtual allocation. Emergency, education, travel, leisure, or purchase goals are user choices; the app does not impose a universal hierarchy.

## Acceptance criteria

Progress is source-linked and currency-safe, scenarios are labeled, impossible or underfunded timelines show the gap neutrally, and goals never force cuts in essential needs.
