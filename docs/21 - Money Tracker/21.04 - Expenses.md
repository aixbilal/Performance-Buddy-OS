# Expenses

## Purpose

Record spending and obligations accurately while keeping purchase context neutral and user-controlled.

## Model

Amount/currency, transaction and posting dates, merchant/payee label, category, status, recurring link, account/container, payment method label where useful, source, note, split allocations, and revision.

## States

`Planned`, `Pending`, `Posted`, `Refunded`, `Cancelled`, `Disputed`, and `Unknown` are distinguished. Refunds reference the original expense when known rather than deleting it.

## Rules

Transfers are excluded from spending. Split categories must sum exactly to the source amount under currency precision. Tips, fees, discounts, and reimbursements remain explicit where recorded. Unknown pending values do not become final silently. Duplicate imports are detected through stable source IDs and contextual matching.

## Acceptance criteria

Period/category totals explain inclusion, refunds and disputes preserve history, splits reconcile exactly, and expense language never assigns moral character.
