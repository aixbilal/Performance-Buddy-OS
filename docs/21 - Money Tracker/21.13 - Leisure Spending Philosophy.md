# Leisure Spending Philosophy

## Purpose

Treat leisure as a legitimate part of life and money planning rather than an automatic waste category or evidence of poor discipline.

## Principle

Spending may support rest, relationships, hobbies, travel, entertainment, food experiences, games, or other personally valued activity. Its appropriateness depends on the user’s resources, obligations, goals, and values—not a universal productivity rule.

## Rules

Leisure is user-categorized and may be planned, spontaneous, recurring, shared, or one-time. The app can show amount, frequency, and trade-offs but cannot shame, compare socially, or label all discretionary spending unnecessary. A high month may reflect a planned event rather than a harmful trend.

## Review

Ask whether the spending was affordable within the user’s plan, aligned with what they valued, and understood in context. Offer adjustment only when requested or when a transparent configured constraint is crossed.

## Acceptance criteria

Leisure remains visible but non-moralized, user choices drive category meaning, and analytics cannot trade leisure against prayer, study, fitness, or personal worth.
