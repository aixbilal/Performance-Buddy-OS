# Categories

## Purpose

Organize money records for useful review without allowing category labels to become moral judgments or accounting authority.

## Model

Stable category ID, user label, type, parent, color/icon, active dates, privacy, aliases, and optional review/budget context. Category hierarchy is shallow enough to remain understandable.

## Rules

Renaming does not rewrite historical identity. Merging preserves old IDs and previews affected reports. Deleting an in-use category archives it or requires remapping. AI or merchant-rule categorization remains a proposal until confirmed at an approved confidence/impact level. `Uncategorized` is legitimate and visible.

## Suggested structure

Essentials, education, transport, health, family, giving, leisure, subscriptions, savings, and other labels may be offered only as editable examples. Cultural and personal priorities remain user-defined.

## Acceptance criteria

Categories are reversible, imports cannot create uncontrolled duplicates, reports remain reproducible after changes, and no category is inherently “good” or “bad.”
