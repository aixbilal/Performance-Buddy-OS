# Separation From Performance Score

## Purpose

Prevent money records from becoming a judgment about discipline, productivity, character, or overall personal performance.

## Rules

Income, balance, savings, spending, leisure, and category totals are excluded from any universal Performance Buddy OS score. Financial values are not normalized against study hours, prayer, fitness, language, sleep, or development output. Wealth, low spending, or high saving does not imply greater personal worth.

## Allowed interactions

Money may create practical constraints or planned actions: a bill due date, a weekly review, a savings goal, or a budget for a project. Cross-domain planning uses the commitment and available resources, not a moral financial rank. Analytics remains inside the Money domain unless the user explicitly requests a scoped comparison.

## Privacy

Dashboards hide sensitive totals by default according to configuration. Public sharing and social comparison are outside baseline. AI cannot infer financial health from incomplete records.

## Acceptance criteria

Schema, analytics, ranking, and recommendation tests prove no money field contributes to a universal score; cross-domain explanations remain factual; and users can disable money visibility without harming other domains.
