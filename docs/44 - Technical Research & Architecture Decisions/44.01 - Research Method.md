# Research Method

## Process

1. Define decision, owner, deadline/review trigger, scope, constraints, non-goals, and blocking impact.
2. Derive weighted criteria and mandatory gates from approved requirements.
3. Identify credible candidates including simpler/no-new-dependency options.
4. Gather dated primary sources for specifications, support, security, licensing, terms, pricing, and limits.
5. Record assumptions, uncertainty, source conflicts, versions, and applicability.
6. Build equivalent risk-focused prototypes with representative data/hardware.
7. Run functional, offline, accessibility, security, failure, performance, cost, migration, and exit tests.
8. Compare evidence, sensitivity to weights, risks, mitigations, and total ownership cost.
9. Publish research as `RESEARCH_REFERENCE`; propose an ADR separately.
10. Review, approve/reject, link Phase 00, and schedule reassessment.

Vendor marketing and AI summaries may locate leads but do not establish facts. Web/community reports may reveal risks and require verification. Benchmarks preserve environment, dataset, code, configuration, and raw results.

## Acceptance criteria

- Another reviewer can reproduce material comparisons.
- Mandatory gates cannot be compensated by weighted convenience scores.
- The method exposes what evidence would change the recommendation.
