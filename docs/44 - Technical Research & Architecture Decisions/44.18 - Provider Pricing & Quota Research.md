# Provider Pricing & Quota Research

## Purpose

Build a dated, reproducible cost and limit model for optional cloud, AI, embedding, storage, sync, network, identity, backup, observability, and support providers. This document does not record timeless prices.

## Evidence model

For each provider/service record official source, capture date, currency/tax assumptions, region, plan/tier, unit definitions, included allowances, minimums, input/output/cache/tool/media charges, storage/operations/egress, rate/concurrency/payload limits, retention, free-tier expiry, support, discounts/commitments, and change terms.

## Scenarios

Model prototype, normal personal use, exam-heavy use, local-first minimal cloud, growth, retry storm, sync resnapshot, embedding rebuild, backup restore/egress, provider outage/failover, and upper-bound abuse. Use sensitivity ranges rather than one average. Quality/safety/privacy gates precede price.

Automated calculators retain formulas and dated inputs; billing is reconciled against estimates during prototypes. Quotas have safe degradation and budget alerts/kill switches.

## Research status

`RESEARCH REQUIRED`: all prices and limits must be refreshed before an ADR or purchase.

## Acceptance criteria

- Another reviewer can reproduce every scenario total.
- Hidden egress, retries, taxes, minima, and support costs are included or marked unknown.
- Price/term changes trigger review without changing CORE behavior.
