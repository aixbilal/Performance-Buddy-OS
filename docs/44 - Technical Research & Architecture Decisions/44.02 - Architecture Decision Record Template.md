# Architecture Decision Record Template

## Required fields

```text
ADR ID / title
Status: PROPOSED | APPROVED | REJECTED | SUPERSEDED
Date / owner / reviewers
Decision scope and capability
Context and problem
Approved requirements and constraints
Assumptions and open questions
Options considered, including status quo
Evidence and dated sources
Prototype/benchmark method and results
Security, privacy, accessibility, offline and recovery impact
Data/schema, compatibility and migration impact
Performance, cost, licensing and operational impact
Decision and rationale
Consequences and accepted risks
Implementation/validation plan
Rollback/exit strategy
Review triggers/date
Affected documents
Supersedes / superseded by
```

## Rules

Facts, measurements, inference, and preference are labeled. An ADR records the decision at the time; approved rationale is never rewritten to fit later outcomes. A change creates a superseding ADR and preserves the chain.

`PROPOSED` has no implementation authority. Approval requires the authorized owner, closed mandatory gates, traceable evidence, and Phase 00 Decision Log reference. Rejected ADRs retain reasoning.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every option receives comparable treatment and known limitations.
- Decision consequences include learning and maintenance costs.
- Approval, supersession, and review status are unambiguous.
