# Cloud Model Provider Research

## Decision

Evaluate remote model providers and model families per V1/V2/V3 application, not as a universal preferred model.

## Criteria

Compare structured outputs, tools, modalities, context, grounding, safety, latency, reliability, cancellation, rate limits, version stability, region, retention/training/data-use controls, deletion, enterprise/privacy terms, incident history, pricing, quotas, support, and provider portability.

## Evaluation

Run the frozen Phase 40 suite across providers using equivalent prompts/schemas/tools and representative network profiles. Record exact provider/model/revision, adapter, parameters, region, date, finish reasons, usage, cost, quality, and critical failures. Do not send production-sensitive data.

Provider capability claims and policies require dated primary sources; unknown retention/region may block sensitive use. Switching providers rechecks consent and regression.

## Research status

`RESEARCH REQUIRED`: no provider/model/failover policy is approved.

## Acceptance criteria

- Quality/safety/privacy gates precede latency and price ranking.
- Provider failure cannot fabricate completion or weaken approval.
- The ADR defines fallback, portability, monitoring, and review triggers.
