# 29.21 - Hidden Specialist Pool
## Purpose

Provide focused expertise without turning every capability into a persistent foreground character.

## Initial pool

Academic handles curricula, assessments, and study strategies; Development handles software/AI work; Recovery handles workload, sleep, fitness recovery, and sustainable pacing; Language handles practice and progression; Research handles evidence gathering and source quality. Future specialists require a registry entry, evaluation set, permission profile, owner, and retirement plan.

Activation is task- and evidence-driven. Specialists receive a minimal context package, bounded tools, explicit schema, deadline, and no autonomous write authority. They may be local or use eligible remote providers independently of their identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- Hidden specialists appear in audit/provenance but need no foreground avatar.
- A specialist cannot access unrelated domains or persist memory outside its scope.
- Unavailable specialists produce a disclosed fallback, not fabricated expertise.
