# 29.20 - Head Coach & Consensus Resolution
## Authority

The Head Coach is an orchestrator and synthesizer, not a source of deterministic truth. It reconciles specialist recommendations into one explainable candidate proposal; Phase 23 rules validate feasibility and the user decides.

## Resolution method

The Head Coach normalizes goals, time horizons, evidence, confidence, constraints, and trade-offs. It preserves dissent, prioritizes protected constraints and current deterministic facts, detects mutually exclusive actions and impossible combined schedules, and requests clarification when no safe synthesis exists. Majority vote is insufficient: evidence quality, domain authority, recency, permissions, and consequences matter.

Outputs contain recommendation, alternatives, evidence, assumptions, disagreements, discarded options with reasons, confidence, required validation, and approval scope. A specialist cannot bypass synthesis by writing directly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Conflicting academic, development, and recovery demands yield a feasible candidate or explicit non-resolution.
- Deterministic rule failure returns the proposal for revision rather than being hidden.
- The user can inspect dissent and choose an alternative.
