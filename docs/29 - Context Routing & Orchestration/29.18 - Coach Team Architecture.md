# 29.18 - Coach Team Architecture
## Team model

The three visible companions form the persistent interaction layer. Hidden specialists supply bounded expertise. The Performance Guide / Head Coach owns task framing, delegation, evidence collection, conflict synthesis, feasibility checks, and the single proposal presented for deterministic validation and user review.

The Culture & Knowledge Guide and Growth & Communication Guide may answer within scope or consult registered specialists. Academic, Development, Recovery, Language, Research, and future specialists return structured findings, evidence, assumptions, constraints, confidence, and unresolved conflicts; they do not independently commit plans or records.

## Flow

`user/eligible trigger → intent and permission check → Head Coach plan → minimal specialist context → parallel or sequential consultation → synthesis → deterministic rules → user decision → audited write`.

## Acceptance criteria

- One request produces one accountable synthesized proposal.
- Impossible combined schedules are rejected or revised before presentation.
- Specialist failure degrades transparently and never removes human control.
