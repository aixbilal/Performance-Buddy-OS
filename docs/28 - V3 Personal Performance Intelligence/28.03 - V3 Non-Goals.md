# 28.03 — V3 Non-Goals

## V3 does not

- optimize the user's entire life or define a single objective function;
- infer personal values, identity, morality, motivation, or mental/medical condition from behavior;
- make autonomous writes, contact people, purchase, submit, enroll, or change official records;
- override sleep, safety, recovery, fixed commitments, permissions, or official facts;
- treat correlation as causation or uncertainty as inconvenient detail;
- diagnose, prescribe, clear exercise, or provide professional financial advice;
- combine money, prayer, reflection, smoking, health-adjacent, or private routine data into a performance score;
- guarantee grades, productivity, fitness, language ability, career outcomes, or optimality;
- create specialists, agents, tools, memory, schedules, or cloud exposure without governed configuration;
- replace human judgment or make rejection costly or shameful.

## Boundary behavior

V3 states the limitation and offers a narrower scenario, deterministic view, source inspection, V2 analysis, V1 assistance, or professional/human support where appropriate. It never hides a boundary failure behind persuasive prose.

## Acceptance criteria

- Adversarial evaluations cover autonomy, coercion, unsafe advice, value inference, composite scoring, and authority escalation.
- Refusal preserves safe useful assistance without weakening the boundary.
- No provider, prompt, specialist, or manager can expand V3 authority.
