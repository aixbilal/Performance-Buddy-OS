# 28.08 — Recovery Reallocation

## Purpose

Make recovery a legitimate high-value allocation when evidence and safety boundaries indicate reduced load, without diagnosing or treating rest as failure.

## Inputs

Protected sleep, recent training and cognitive load, recovery sessions/rest days, soreness/fatigue/readiness self-report, illness context, pain/discomfort, current mode, schedule capacity, and permitted device estimates. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## Rules

No single signal provides medical clearance or diagnosis. V3 uses Phase 19 cues such as `Normal Context`, `Caution`, `Recovery Favored`, or `Unknown`, and respects urgent-symptom guidance. Recovery may reduce exercise intensity, cognitive demand, optional work, or reschedule blocks—but not erase fixed obligations invisibly.

Recommendations are conservative, reversible, non-punitive, and show contradictory signals. Concerning symptoms are outside optimization and direct the user toward appropriate professional/emergency support.

## Acceptance criteria

- Safety scenarios favor conservative action and never claim medical certainty.
- Protected sleep is not traded as free capacity.
- Recovery proposals name affected loads, duration, alternative, and reassessment trigger.
