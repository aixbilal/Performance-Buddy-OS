# 28.06 — Academic Reallocation

## Purpose

Propose moving bounded capacity toward, within, or away from academics based on verified consequence and marginal return while protecting sustainable performance.

## Inputs

Official deadlines/policies, enrollment and prerequisites, assessment weights, current marks and CORE projections, topic evidence, study effectiveness, remaining work, risk signals, fixed university commitments, user goals, modes, and competing domain demand.

## Rules

Phase 13 performs all calculations. V3 compares feasible academic actions using deadline leverage, prerequisite consequence, evidence gap, readiness, diminishing returns, and opportunity to correct mistakes. It cannot invent policy, assume more hours ensure results, or sacrifice sleep/recovery.

Imminent academic risk may temporarily place development or language into documented maintenance. When academic trajectory is healthy, V3 may protect development, fitness, routines, or recovery. Reallocation states what changes, why, how long, and when normal allocation resumes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Calculation fixtures match CORE exactly and sources are authoritative/fresh.
- Academic urgency cannot silently erase other long-term systems.
- Every proposal includes displaced work, alternative, uncertainty, and review trigger.
