# 28.09 — Long-Horizon Reasoning

## Purpose

Reason across weeks, semesters, and selected goal horizons while recognizing that forecasts weaken with distance and plans must adapt.

## Horizon model

Use nested horizons: immediate action, current week, assessment/milestone interval, semester/path segment, and longer goal direction. Each horizon has its own evidence freshness, uncertainty, commitment level, and review cadence. Near-term actions are concrete; distant allocations are ranges and scenarios.

## Method

Anchor official/fixed events; model capacities and dependencies; segment known modes; represent uncertainty and scenario branches; check continuity and maintenance needs; identify decision points; and roll the plan forward as evidence changes. Do not extrapolate a short streak or disruption indefinitely.

Long-horizon reasoning cannot create guaranteed forecasts, hard-code personal values, or let a distant optimization violate current safety. Future commitments remain proposals until accepted through owned workflows.

## Acceptance criteria

- Scenario assumptions, branches, ranges, and decision points are inspectable.
- Backtests assess forecast calibration and harmful overcommitment, not only fit.
- Rolling updates explain material recommendation changes without rewriting history.
