# 28.14 — V3 Recommendation Contract

## Required fields

A V3 recommendation contains stable ID/version; objective and horizon; capability/mode; included domains and consent; current goals/preferences; source manifest and freshness; facts, derived measures, assumptions, and hypotheses; proposed action/allocation; target evidence; alternatives considered; expected benefit range; trade-offs/displaced work; protected constraints; uncertainty/confidence; deterministic validation result/reason codes; expiration; review trigger; and prior-guidance change explanation.

## Lifecycle

Create as an uncommitted proposal; validate schema, sources, permissions, domain facts, calculations, capacity, constraints, reversibility, and staleness; present a concise impact preview; allow accept, edit, reject, or defer; revalidate edits and current state; then commit each owner command separately with idempotency and audit.

Validation means feasible under known rules—not optimal, medically safe, financially advisable, or guaranteed. Recommendations cannot contain hidden actions. A batch approval names every affected owner and item.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every material claim maps to evidence or is labeled assumption/hypothesis.
- Stale, rejected, blocked, expired, or revoked recommendations cause no write.
- Approval cannot be replayed after a recommendation, source, or target changes.
