# Academic References

## Purpose

Separate official CUI sources, general academic policies, and sensitive personal academic records so each retains the correct authority, applicability, and permission boundary.

## Subfolders

- `CUI Curriculum/` holds candidate official curriculum publications. Record program, campus, cohort, effective dates, URL or governed origin, capture date, and checksum. A file is not official merely because its name contains CUI.
- `Academic Policies/` holds candidate official regulations, grading rules, notices, and amendments. Preserve version and supersession relationships; do not infer a rule from common practice.
- `Personal Academic Records/` holds user-supplied transcripts, registrations, schedules, and similar evidence. These records describe the user and never establish institution-wide policy.

## Safety and authority

The applicable CUI curriculum and grading-policy versions remain `RESEARCH REQUIRED` until verified under `12.15 - Official CUI Source Handling.md`. Personal records follow `12.16 - Personal Academic Record Import.md`: preserve the original, minimize identifiers, record permission and checksum, extract into a candidate workspace, validate, preview, and obtain confirmation before committing data.

Do not send personal records to cloud AI without explicit, source-specific permission. OCR and AI extraction produce candidates only. Conflicts remain visible until resolved by the appropriate authority.

## Acceptance criteria

- Official, policy, and personal sources remain physically and semantically separated.
- Each used source has provenance, applicability, classification, and permission.
- No imported record silently becomes a general academic rule.
- Missing or ambiguous authority blocks consequential academic calculation.
