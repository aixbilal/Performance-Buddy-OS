# Reference Governance

## Scope and ownership

This document governs intake, storage, classification, transformation, citation, review, retention, and promotion of Phase 46 material. It does not decide product behavior, academic policy, architecture, or visual standards; those decisions belong to their approved owners.

## Intake record

For every material reference, record when applicable:

- source class from Phase 00, creator or issuer, title, and stable location;
- capture date, publication/effective date, version, and applicable cohort or environment;
- checksum and original filename;
- permission, license, confidentiality, and allowed processing zones;
- transformation history, extraction method, reviewer, and known limitations;
- related requirement, research question, decision, or imported entity.

Missing provenance lowers the source to `INFERENCE` or `PROVISIONAL_ASSUMPTION` and blocks consequential automation.

## Folder rules

Official CUI material is stored separately from personal records. Personal academic records are sensitive and must not be copied into general research or visual folders. Provider reports and web research remain `RESEARCH_REFERENCE`; generated concepts remain `AI_INTERPRETATION`. UX and visual references guide analysis but cannot be copied blindly or treated as licensed assets.

Do not store secrets, credentials, unnecessary student identifiers, unlawful copies, executable downloads, or live external links that can mutate the project. Scan and quarantine untrusted files before parsing. Preserve the original read-only; derived text, OCR, thumbnails, mappings, and annotations are separate artifacts with provenance.

## Conflict and promotion

Preserve conflicting sources and compare authority, applicability, dates, and supersession. AI may summarize or extract but cannot authenticate or promote a source. Official facts require official verification; personal facts require user confirmation or validated import. Research becomes architecture only through Phase 44 and an approved ADR. Product or documentation changes follow Phase 00 revision rules.

## Lifecycle

Review references when their authority, license, sensitivity, version, or relevance changes. Retention and deletion follow Phase 32, Phase 38, and Phase 39. Removing a derived artifact must not erase required decision provenance; deleting a sensitive original requires explicit authority and an auditable recovery decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every used reference has sufficient provenance to reproduce its role.
- Source class, decision authority, and interpretation are visibly separate.
- Sensitive and untrusted inputs cannot bypass permission or validation controls.
- Promotions, conflicts, transformations, and deletions are reviewable and auditable.
