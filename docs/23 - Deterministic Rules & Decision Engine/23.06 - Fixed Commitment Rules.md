# Fixed Commitment Rules

## Purpose

Preserve commitments whose time or obligation cannot ordinarily move, while distinguishing them from important but flexible actions.

## Rules

Fixed commitments reserve their full interval plus configured travel/transition buffers after protected constraints. They cannot overlap each other unless the source explicitly allows it. The engine never moves, splits, shortens, or cancels one as an optimization. Correcting source time is different from overriding its fixed nature.

## Conflict outcomes

A fixed/fixed conflict returns `BLOCK/REQUIRE_SOURCE_CORRECTION`. Fixed demand exceeding capacity returns `INFEASIBLE`. Flexible actions may be moved, reduced, or deferred according to their owner rules, with original plan retained.

## Classification changes

Converting fixed to windowed/flexible is consequential configuration with reason, actor, effective time, and impact preview. AI may propose only.

## Acceptance criteria

Overlap, buffers, recurrence, timezones, cancellations, and source corrections are tested; fixed status remains separate from priority and deadline.
