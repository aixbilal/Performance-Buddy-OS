# Recommendation Validation

## Purpose

Validate candidate recommendations from rules, analytics, users, or AI before they are shown as feasible or offered for approval.

## Candidate contract

Source/capability, intended action, target entities, proposed changes, evidence, assumptions, confidence, expected effect, alternative, expiration/freshness, and permissions requested.

## Checks

Schema and allowed action; source authorization; protected/fixed constraints; domain ownership; current state/version; capacity and dependencies; privacy; numerical recalculation through `CORE`; reversibility; duplicate/replay; and approval level.

## Outcomes

`VALID`, `VALID_WITH_WARNINGS`, `NEEDS_CLARIFICATION`, `STALE`, `BLOCKED`, or `UNSUPPORTED`. Validation does not mean optimal, medically safe, financially advisable, or guaranteed; domain limits remain visible.

## Acceptance criteria

AI cannot bypass tools, stale proposals cannot commit, explanations name rejected conditions, alternatives are also validated, and rejected/revoked recommendations cause no write.
