# Sleep Protection Rules

## Purpose

Prevent plans and recommendations from silently reducing configured sleep opportunity to fit work, study, exercise, or routines.

## Inputs

User-approved sleep window/target, wake obligations, timezone, transition buffer, existing fixed commitments, current plan, mode, and explicit temporary exceptions. Device-estimated sleep is contextual evidence, not the schedule boundary unless configured.

## Rules

Reserve the protected window before flexible scheduling. Reject overlaps from flexible work. A fixed conflict is surfaced as infeasible and requires source correction or an explicitly allowed human decision; it is never hidden. Exams and deadlines may raise priority but do not auto-remove sleep. Catch-up after a short night cannot be prescribed medically by the engine.

## Overrides

Only an approved user-configurable exception path may change the window; it records reason, duration, impact, and reversion. AI cannot initiate or approve the exception.

## Acceptance criteria

Boundary, midnight, timezone, recurrence, and fixed-conflict tests pass; no candidate plan gains feasibility by silently shortening sleep.
