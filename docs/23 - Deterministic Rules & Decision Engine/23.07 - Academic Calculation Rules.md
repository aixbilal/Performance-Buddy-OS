# Academic Calculation Rules

## Purpose

Enforce deterministic, source-versioned academic mathematics and prevent unverified policy from producing official-looking results.

## Registered rules

Assessment ratios/weights, category aggregation, grade mapping, pass conditions, credit-point calculations, repeat/replacement inclusion, SGPA, CGPA, projections, precision/rounding, and exceptional statuses are registered only from verified Phase 12/13 policy definitions.

## Gates

Validate policy applicability, source verification, complete required inputs, decimal precision, interval boundaries, weight totals, duplicate attempts, credit units, zero denominators, repeat equivalence, and classification (`DERIVED VERIFIED`, `SCENARIO`, or `UNRESOLVED`).

## Rules

No common CUI weights, thresholds, or replacement behavior are assumed. AI never performs the arithmetic. Historical calculation snapshots retain original rule versions. Corrections recompute dependents without overwriting attempt history.

## Acceptance criteria

Phase 13 fixtures and boundary/property tests reproduce exactly, missing policy blocks official classification, and every value exposes formula substitutions and source versions.
