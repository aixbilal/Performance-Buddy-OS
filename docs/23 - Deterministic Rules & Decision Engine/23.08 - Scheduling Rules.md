# Scheduling Rules

## Purpose

Validate candidate schedules against temporal, capacity, dependency, mode, and flexibility contracts.

## Sequence

Resolve timezone/day boundary; reserve protected constraints; place fixed commitments; enforce availability, travel, buffers, and dependencies; preserve active/completed/pinned items; evaluate windowed and flexible candidates; check capacity and fragmentation; report conflicts and minimal repairs.

## Rules

Intervals cannot have negative duration or unauthorized overlap. Unknown duration is not invented. Splitting requires action support and minimum useful segment. Maintenance cannot be represented as full progression. Recalculation preserves original plan and returns candidates, not committed mutations.

## Results

`VALID`, `VALID_WITH_WARNINGS`, `INFEASIBLE`, or `UNRESOLVED`, plus reason codes, displaced items, remaining capacity, alternatives, and rule versions.

## Acceptance criteria

DST/timezone, midnight, recurrence, overlap, dependency, buffer, partial-day, and mode tests pass; infeasible plans remain visibly infeasible.
