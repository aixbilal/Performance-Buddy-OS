# Protected Constraints

## Purpose

Define constraints that ordinary planning, scoring, gamification, AI, and convenience cannot bypass.

## Classes

Permission/security boundaries; data-integrity requirements; protected sleep/recovery; fitness health/safety limits; urgent welfare concerns; professional/medical restrictions recorded by the user; fixed official examination/appointment constraints; and other explicitly approved protected boundaries.

## Contract

Each constraint records owner, source, strength, scope, effective dates, timezone where relevant, validation rule, allowed exception authority, privacy, and reason code. Unknown applicability may block high-consequence actions.

## Behavior

Protected violations return `BLOCK` or `UNRESOLVED` with safe alternatives. The engine cannot “optimize around” them by shrinking buffers, changing evidence, or hiding the conflict. Immediate stop/reduce controls remain available for fitness.

## Acceptance criteria

All entry points—UI, import, background work, API, and AI tools—enforce the same constraints; bypass attempts are logged safely; and alternatives never weaken permissions or safety.
