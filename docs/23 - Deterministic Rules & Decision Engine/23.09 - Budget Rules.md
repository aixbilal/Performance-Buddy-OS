# Budget Rules

## Purpose

Provide deterministic validation for lightweight money records, balances, savings scenarios, and recurring expectations without becoming financial advice.

## Rules

Use decimal currency arithmetic and declared minor-unit precision. Validate nonnegative source amounts with explicit direction, currency compatibility, split totals, transaction state, duplicate source IDs, transfer pairing, refund/reversal links, recurrence, and inclusion policy. Multi-currency aggregation requires a sourced rate and timestamp.

## Calculations

Derived balance follows the Phase 21 scoped equation. Expected income/expenses and savings projections remain scenarios. Transfers cancel only where both owned-account sides are in scope. Unknown/pending records follow the selected view policy and are disclosed.

## Boundary

Rules cannot recommend securities, loans, tax positions, or essential-needs cuts. AI may explain deterministic outputs only.

## Acceptance criteria

Fixtures cover splits, refunds, pending transactions, transfers, duplicates, currencies, zero values, and reconciliation; every total opens to included records.
