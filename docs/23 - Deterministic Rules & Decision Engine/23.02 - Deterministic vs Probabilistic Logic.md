# Deterministic vs Probabilistic Logic

## Purpose

Keep authoritative validation and calculation separate from uncertain predictions, classifications, and model suggestions.

## Deterministic logic

Schema/range checks, permissions, time overlap, recurrence, credit/grade arithmetic under verified policy, balance arithmetic, state transitions, evidence requirements, priority bands under approved rules, and reason-code production belong to `CORE`.

## Probabilistic logic

AI intent parsing, semantic classification, difficulty/risk estimation from incomplete evidence, pattern interpretation, generated text/questions, and forecasting may produce proposals with confidence, provenance, and missing inputs. Capability labels are `LOCAL`, `V1`, `V2`, or `V3` as appropriate.

## Boundary rules

Probabilistic output never becomes an authoritative fact merely by confidence. It is schema-validated, mapped to allowed candidate actions, and passed through deterministic rules. Uncertain classification affecting consequence requires review. Deterministic tools—not an LLM—perform official academic and money arithmetic.

## Acceptance criteria

Every feature identifies its logic class, disabling models preserves `CORE`, model failure cannot weaken constraints, and interfaces distinguish facts, estimates, and proposals.
