# Duration Trackers

## Purpose

A Duration tracker records time spent on a defined activity or condition. It supports manual entry, linked session intervals, and authorized device imports while preserving source differences.

## Data contract

Duration is stored in a canonical unit with original input retained. The record includes start/end when known, active duration, source, timezone, confidence, and linked occurrence. A duration without reliable boundaries may be recorded as a stated total.

## Rules

- Duration must be non-negative and respect configured plausibility limits.
- Overlapping intervals are flagged before aggregation.
- Paused or unknown time is excluded unless explicitly classified.
- Manual, session-derived, and device-derived values retain provenance.
- Time reaching a target does not prove learning, output quality, spiritual intention, or safe exercise.

## Reconciliation

When a Phase 09 session supplies time, the tracker references that session rather than copying an unaudited total. Corrections propagate through versioned projections and avoid double counting.

## Acceptance criteria

The same records reproduce the same total under the same rule version; clock uncertainty is visible; offline entry works; and completion remains domain-aware.
