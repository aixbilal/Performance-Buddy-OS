# Reflection Trackers

## Purpose

A Reflection tracker captures short qualitative observations on a recurring or event-triggered basis. It supports awareness and review without pretending subjective text is an objective performance score.

## Prompt model

A definition contains one or more prompts, response types, optionality, privacy class, and effective version. Responses may be Markdown text, a bounded self-rating, selected tags, or a combination. Users may skip any prompt the owner marks optional.

## Rules

- Empty, skipped, and not scheduled remain distinct.
- Self-ratings are contextual observations, not diagnoses.
- Prompt changes create a new version so responses stay interpretable.
- Private text is not required merely to earn completion.
- Search and analytics respect privacy and consent boundaries.

## AI boundary

`LOCAL` or later intelligence may summarize themes or propose prompts. It must separate observation from inference, show supporting records, avoid medical or moral diagnosis, and require approval before saving generated interpretation. Cloud processing requires explicit scoped consent.

## Acceptance criteria

The tracker works offline, preserves prompt versions, supports private and minimal responses, and never ranks personal or spiritual worth from reflection content.
