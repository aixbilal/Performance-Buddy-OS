# Count Trackers

## Purpose

A Count tracker records whole-number repetitions or discrete items within an occurrence, such as glasses of water, practice sets, pages processed, or completed exercises where counting is meaningful.

## Data contract

Definitions specify a non-negative integer unit, optional target, optional safe maximum, aggregation rule, and whether multiple entries accumulate. Records retain individual contributions so totals can be explained and corrected.

## Validation rules

- Reject fractional, negative, incompatible-unit, or out-of-range values unless the owner explicitly defines otherwise.
- Prevent duplicated imported events through stable source identifiers.
- Distinguish `0` recorded from `Unknown`.
- A target reached may propose `Complete`; owner rules determine whether confirmation or evidence is required.
- Counts across different units or definition versions are not combined without an explicit conversion.

## Corrections and aggregation

Edits preserve the original entry and reason. Daily or occurrence totals are derived projections, not replacement facts. When late entries arrive, affected totals and consistency metrics recompute deterministically.

## Boundary

Count quantity does not establish quality, safety, learning, or intention. Domain owners may impose limits and warnings that the generic tracker cannot override.
