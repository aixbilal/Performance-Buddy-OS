# Consistency Metrics

## Purpose

Consistency metrics describe sustainable repetition and return patterns. They support reflection; they are not a universal performance score or judgment of character.

## Baseline metrics

- eligible completion rate by rolling window;
- completion-state distribution;
- planned versus actual occurrence coverage;
- return time after disruption;
- frequency and volume trends where units are comparable;
- maintenance, rest, and excused proportions;
- evidence coverage and unknown-record rate.

## Calculation contract

Every metric declares numerator, denominator, included states, time window, timezone, definition versions, late-entry policy, and calculation version. Results link to source records. Unknown is not silently treated as incomplete. Rest and excused handling is visible.

## Comparison rules

Do not combine different units, incompatible tracker definitions, or private domains into one moralized score. Personal baselines are preferred to social comparison. Small samples and missing data display uncertainty rather than strong conclusions.

## Capability boundary

`CORE` calculates descriptive metrics. `V2` may propose pattern interpretations with evidence and confidence; `V3` may reason across domains without overriding source owners.

## Acceptance criteria

Metrics are reproducible, explainable, privacy-aware, resistant to missing-data distortion, and emphasize trajectory and recovery over perfection.
