# Tracker Customization

## Purpose

Customization lets users adapt tracker labels, types, units, targets, schedules, evidence prompts, display preferences, reminders, and privacy settings without breaking canonical semantics or history.

## Safe workflow

1. Choose a canonical tracker type and owner domain.
2. Define a clear action or measurement and unit.
3. Configure valid states, target, recurrence, and evidence proportionality.
4. Select privacy and reminder behavior.
5. Preview generated occurrences and metric effects.
6. Validate, confirm, and activate an effective-dated version.

## Change rules

Cosmetic changes may update the active definition without semantic migration. Type, unit, component set, target meaning, timezone policy, or aggregation changes create a new version. The system offers archive-and-replace when conversion would be misleading. Bulk edits require preview, confirmation, and recoverability.

## Templates and AI

Templates are starting configurations, not domain authority. `LOCAL` or `V1` may draft a tracker from natural language, but the preview identifies assumptions and requires user approval. Unsafe, ambiguous, or privacy-sensitive proposals are flagged.

## Acceptance criteria

Customization works offline, cannot silently corrupt historical comparison, exposes ownership and privacy, preserves stable IDs and revisions, and supports export without proprietary lock-in.
