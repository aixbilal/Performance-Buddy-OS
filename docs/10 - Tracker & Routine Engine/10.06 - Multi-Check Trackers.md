# Multi-Check Trackers

## Purpose

A Multi-Check tracker represents one occurrence containing several named components. It is suitable when each component matters independently and a single Boolean would hide useful truth.

## Definition and records

The definition contains stable component IDs, display order, required/optional status, and owner-specific rules. Each component receives its own state, timestamp, and optional evidence. The parent outcome is derived by a versioned rule and may be confirmed by the user.

## Rules

- Component labels may change without changing stable IDs.
- Adding or removing components creates a new effective definition version.
- Partial completion remains visible; unchecked is `Unknown` unless explicitly marked otherwise.
- One component cannot satisfy another unless the owner defines an equivalence.
- Parent completion cannot erase component-level details.

## Sensitive domains

Prayer and other spiritual routines may use this structure, but Phase 20 owns their meaning, flexibility, privacy, and non-judgmental presentation. The generic engine must not infer intention, quality, or moral value.

## Acceptance criteria

Historical occurrences render against their original component version, parent derivation is explainable, partial truth is preserved, and private components inherit the strictest applicable privacy classification.
