# Streak Policy

## Position

Streaks are optional, secondary context. They must never become the primary success measure or create pressure to sacrifice sleep, recovery, safety, honesty, worship context, study quality, or higher priorities.

## Definition

A streak is a sequence of eligible logical periods under a named policy version. The policy declares qualifying states, treatment of planned rest and excused periods, timezone, grace behavior, late-entry cutoff, and definition-change handling.

## Rules

- Missing records remain `Unknown`; displays must not accuse the user of failure.
- Backfilling requires truthful evidence and preserves entry time.
- Rest or excused may preserve continuity but cannot add completed volume.
- Partial or maintenance eligibility is explicit and owner-specific.
- Streak repair purchases, shame language, alarming loss animation, and manipulative notifications are prohibited.
- Pausing or hiding streaks is always available.

## Recovery presentation

When continuity ends, the product records the sequence and focuses on the next useful return. Longest-streak comparison is subordinate to rolling consistency, honest state distribution, and recovery time.

## Acceptance criteria

Users can understand the exact policy, disable the feature, correct records audibly, and safely choose rest or higher priorities without coercive UX.
