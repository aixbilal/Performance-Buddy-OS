# Recurrence & Scheduling

## Purpose

Recurrence rules generate expected tracker occurrences without rewriting factual records. Scheduling is deterministic, timezone-aware, and explainable.

## Supported baseline

`CORE` supports one-time, daily, selected weekdays, weekly, monthly, interval-based, and bounded custom patterns; start/end dates; local timezone; completion windows; planned rest days; exceptions; pause periods; and effective-dated rule revisions.

## Generation rules

Occurrences use stable identifiers derived from tracker, rule version, and logical period. Regeneration is idempotent. A timezone change preserves the original logical date of existing records and applies a clear policy to future occurrences. Daylight-saving gaps or overlaps are surfaced when relevant.

## Changes

Editing recurrence creates an effective-dated version. Past occurrences and completed records remain attached to the old rule. The user chooses how unresolved future occurrences are migrated; no bulk historical rewrite occurs silently.

## Exceptions and conflicts

Rest, travel, exams, illness, recovery, and owner-defined exceptions may suppress, excuse, or reschedule occurrences according to explicit policy. Fixed commitments and protected constraints from Phase 08 remain authoritative.

## Acceptance criteria

The same rule and timezone reproduce the same occurrences, duplicates are impossible under retry, exceptions are visible, and missed records are never fabricated from schedule alone.
