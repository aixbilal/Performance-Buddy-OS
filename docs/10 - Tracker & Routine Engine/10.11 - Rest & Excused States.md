# Rest & Excused States

## Purpose

`Rest` and `Excused` protect truthful records when performance was deliberately not required. They prevent unsafe or misleading pressure to complete routines in every circumstance.

## Rest

`Rest` is a planned or consciously chosen recovery outcome recognized by the owner domain. It can be a successful adherence outcome for fitness, sleep protection, workload recovery, or another approved routine policy. It is not a concealed completion and does not claim the action occurred.

## Excused

`Excused` means an occurrence was waived because a configured rule or confirmed context applies, such as illness, travel, examination mode, schedule conflict, or an owner-defined spiritual/personal exception. Sensitive reasons may be stored as a private category or omitted.

## Calculation policy

Metrics show rest and excused counts separately. Their denominator treatment is declared by each metric and owner policy. They may preserve eligibility for an optional streak, but must never inflate completed volume.

## Rules

AI can propose an exception but cannot diagnose, invent, or apply it silently. Bulk exception changes require preview and confirmation. Retroactive corrections preserve provenance.

## Acceptance criteria

Rest and excused states remain visible, do not masquerade as completed work, respect privacy, and never encourage overriding safety or recovery to protect a metric.
