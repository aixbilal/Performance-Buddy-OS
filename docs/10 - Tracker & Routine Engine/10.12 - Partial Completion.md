# Partial Completion

## Purpose

`Partial` records useful work without falsely claiming the full requirement. It supports consistency over perfection and improves future estimation.

## Record model

A partial record contains the achieved amount or components when measurable, the original target snapshot, optional reason, evidence, and next disposition. The remaining portion may be deferred, reduced, cancelled, or left unresolved through Phase 08 planning rules.

## Rules

- Partial is not automatically a fixed percentage.
- The owner domain defines whether quantity, components, quality, or evidence determines progress.
- Multiple partial records may accumulate only when the definition permits and duplicate evidence is prevented.
- Reaching a numeric target may propose completion but cannot override a qualitative evidence requirement.
- Minimum maintenance can be recorded as `Maintenance` rather than inflating partial progression.

## Metrics and language

Analytics reports partial volume and frequency separately from complete outcomes. UI language describes what happened and offers a next step without calling the record failure.

## Acceptance criteria

Users can save honest partial work quickly, remaining work stays visible, aggregation is explainable, and no domain’s completion semantics are replaced by a universal percentage.
