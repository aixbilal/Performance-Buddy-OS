# 25.01 — Local AI Overview

## Definition

`LOCAL` means inference is performed on hardware controlled by the user, without sending task content to a remote inference provider. It may support bounded language and embedding workloads when the installed runtime, model, and device meet declared gates.

## Architectural position

Local AI is an adapter under Phase 24, not a separate authority. It consumes purpose-bound, permission-filtered context and returns proposals or derived evidence through versioned schemas. Domain services resolve identifiers and Phase 23 validates consequences.

## Intended strengths

- offline availability after approved model/runtime installation;
- reduced remote disclosure and predictable marginal inference cost;
- low-latency bounded parsing, classification, extraction, and retrieval assistance;
- user control over model files, activation, resource limits, and removal.

## Limits

Local execution does not guarantee correctness, secrecy, speed, low energy use, or suitability for every device. Model packages and runtimes introduce supply-chain risk. Other local users, malware, logs, swap, backups, and crash reports may still expose data. Large or high-quality workloads may require an explicitly approved `CLOUD` path.

## Acceptance criteria

- UI and documentation distinguish `CORE`, `LOCAL`, and `CLOUD` behavior.
- Every local feature has an off state and deterministic/manual fallback.
- No local result is presented as authoritative merely because it stayed on-device.
