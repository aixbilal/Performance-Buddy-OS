# Offline Edit Reconciliation

## Reconnection sequence

After connectivity returns, the client:

1. validates account, device, permission, consent, protocol, and schema state;
2. uploads eligible local operations idempotently from the durable queue;
3. obtains durable acknowledgements without advancing past gaps;
4. pulls remote changes after the last verified cursor;
5. verifies integrity and causal dependencies;
6. applies causally ordered, valid changes transactionally;
7. detects and preserves conflicts and tombstones;
8. reports item-level outcomes and advances safe checkpoints.

Upload and pull may be interleaved by the selected protocol, but the observable guarantees remain the same. Local work stays usable during reconciliation.

## Long-offline devices

A device outside retention or schema compatibility windows must perform a safe resnapshot or migration. It may not upload stale state as a replacement for newer revisions. Revoked devices cannot resume merely by reconnecting.

## Failure behavior

Partial acknowledgement, interruption, or application restart resumes from durable operations and cursors. Reconciliation never silently drops failed items or labels them synchronized.

## Acceptance criteria

- Repeated reconnection converges without duplicate effects.
- Deletions cannot be undone by a stale device without an explicit restore.
- Long-offline and incompatible clients receive a safe recovery path.
