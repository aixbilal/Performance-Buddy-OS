# Sync Performance

## Measures

Track change-to-queue, queue wait, batch build, encryption, upload, durable acknowledgement, pull, verification, apply, conflict detection, cursor advance, convergence time, retry delay, throughput, bytes, battery, and local storage growth.

## Scenarios

Benchmark first sync, incremental sync, typical/large history, many small and large attachment changes, multiple devices, long offline, network flapping, high latency/loss, throttling, quota, provider outage, partial acknowledgement, replay/reorder, conflicts, resnapshot, and revoked device.

Performance cannot weaken immediate local durability, authentication, encryption, causal ordering, tombstones, conflict preservation, or item-level status. Batching and compaction retain idempotency and checkpoint safety. Interactive CORE and device battery/resource policy take priority over convergence speed.

Diagnostics use opaque operation/device IDs, size/count buckets, states, reason codes, and stage durations; payload content remains excluded.

`RESEARCH REQUIRED`: approve batch, bandwidth, convergence, retention, and device-tier budgets after protocol/provider selection.

## Acceptance criteria

- Load tests converge without duplicate effects or lost revisions.
- Queue growth, retry storms, and storage use remain bounded.
- No item is marked synced before verified durable acknowledgement.
