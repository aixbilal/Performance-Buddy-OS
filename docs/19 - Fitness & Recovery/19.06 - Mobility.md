# Mobility

## Purpose

Track controlled movement practice intended to explore or maintain usable range, coordination, and comfort without diagnosing restrictions.

## Record model

Body area or movement, drill, active/passive emphasis, repetitions or duration, range description, control, discomfort, goal context, and optional comparison evidence. Measurements retain method and device/source.

## Rules

Mobility and flexibility are related but not interchangeable. A larger measured range is not always a better or safer outcome. Comparisons require consistent setup. Pain, numbness, tingling, instability, or worsening symptoms are not targets to push through and should follow the safety boundary.

## Use

Mobility may be a standalone session, warm-up component, cooldown, recovery activity, or movement-quality practice. It cannot be assumed to prevent injury or correct a medical problem.

## Acceptance criteria

Records preserve method and context, progress includes control and comfort, the app avoids diagnostic claims, and drills can be reduced or stopped immediately.
