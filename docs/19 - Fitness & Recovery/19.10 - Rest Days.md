# Rest Days

## Purpose

Treat deliberate rest as a legitimate training and recovery state rather than an empty or failed day.

## Types

Planned rest, reactive rest, medical/professional-advice rest, travel/logistics rest, exam-load rest, and user-chosen rest may be configured. Sensitive reasons are optional and privacy-protected.

## Rules

Rest records no training completion and does not inflate volume. It may preserve consistency eligibility under Phase 10 policy without creating completed work. A rest day does not require compensatory training later. AI may propose rest but cannot diagnose the reason, determine readiness, or override professional advice.

## Planning

Rest protects capacity after load, during illness/injury concern, or when sleep/recovery constraints require it. Phase 08 treats it as a protected or chosen commitment according to configuration.

## Acceptance criteria

Rest is visible in history and analytics, streaks cannot pressure unsafe activity, private reasons are optional, and backlog logic does not automatically double the next session.
