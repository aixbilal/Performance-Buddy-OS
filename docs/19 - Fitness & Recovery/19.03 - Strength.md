# Strength

## Purpose

Track resistance-training exercises, sets, repetitions, load, effort, technique notes, and progression while avoiding unsafe universal prescriptions.

## Record model

Exercise/variation, equipment, set sequence, repetitions, external load and unit, bodyweight context where relevant, perceived effort or repetitions-in-reserve if configured, rest, range/tempo notes, assistance, discomfort, and verification source.

## Rules

Load plus bodyweight conventions are explicit. Warm-up and work sets remain distinct. Failed, assisted, partial-range, and stopped sets are recorded honestly. Estimated maximums are derived scenarios, not direct measurements or required tests. Form quality cannot be inferred from load or repetition count alone.

## Progression and safety

Progress may involve technique, control, repetitions, load, range, consistency, or reduced discomfort—not only heavier weight. The app never encourages a maximal attempt, training through pain, unsafe spotting, or overriding equipment limitations merely to maintain progress.

## Acceptance criteria

Units and exercise variants cannot mix silently, revisions are auditable, estimated values are labeled, and a stopped or reduced session remains a valid safe outcome.
