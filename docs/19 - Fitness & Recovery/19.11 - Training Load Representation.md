# Training Load Representation

## Purpose

Represent training demand across modalities without pretending unlike activities can be reduced safely to one perfect score.

## Source measures

Running may use duration, distance, intervals, pace, and perceived effort. Strength may use sets, repetitions, load, and effort. Explosive work may use contacts/sprints and quality. Mobility/recovery use distinct measures. Device-derived cardiovascular metrics retain device provenance.

## Derived views

Session load, modality-specific weekly volume, intensity distribution, monotony/change indicators, and user-configured summaries may be derived with named formulas and versions. Cross-modality totals are labeled approximations and never medical readiness scores.

## Rules

Unknown data is not zero. Duplicate device/manual records are reconciled. Sudden change flags are contextual prompts, not injury predictions. Exact thresholds and load formulas are `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED` until evaluated for the individual use case.

## Acceptance criteria

Every load value exposes inputs and units, incompatible measures are not silently added, corrections recompute history, and no single score authorizes or prohibits training.
