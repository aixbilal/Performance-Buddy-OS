# Stretching

## Purpose

Track static, dynamic, and other user-configured stretching activities with clear context, duration, intensity perception, and safety limits.

## Record model

Target area/movement, stretch type, side, sets, duration or repetitions, perceived intensity, session placement, discomfort, and source. Left/right records remain distinct where useful.

## Rules

Stretching is not automatically required for every session and is not presented as guaranteed injury prevention or treatment. The appropriate type and timing depend on activity and individual context. Bouncing, extreme range, external force, or painful stretching is never encouraged by the product.

## Evidence

Consistency, comfortable range, controlled movement, and user-reported response are tracked separately. Duration alone does not prove effectiveness. Flexibility measurements use consistent procedures and remain estimates.

## Acceptance criteria

Stretch types and sides cannot mix silently, no universal prescription is implied, pain prompts stopping rather than completion pressure, and user/coach-defined protocols retain provenance.
