# Wrist Forearm & Hand Training

## Purpose

Track grip, finger, wrist, and forearm strengthening or mobility work with special attention to repetitive load, technique, and symptoms.

## Record model

Exercise, side, grip/position, resistance and unit, repetitions or hold, sets, tempo, device/tool, perceived effort, discomfort, occupational/sport context, and evidence source.

## Rules

Left and right sides remain separate. Grip-device readings require device and setup provenance. High repetition, heavy loading, awkward positions, and simultaneous climbing/calisthenics/development-use load may matter but cannot be diagnosed by the app. Numbness, tingling, weakness, sharp pain, swelling, or persistent worsening is not treated as normal training data to overcome.

## Progression

Increase only through user/qualified guidance and evidence of tolerance. Strength, endurance, control, and symptom-free function are distinct dimensions.

## Acceptance criteria

Side and device context are preserved, aggregate load avoids duplicate sessions, symptom capture triggers conservative guidance, and no rehabilitation claim is made.
