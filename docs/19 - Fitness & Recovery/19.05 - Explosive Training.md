# Explosive Training

## Purpose

Represent jumps, sprints, throws, and other high-rate force work with conservative volume, quality, readiness, and safety controls.

## Record model

Exercise, contacts/repetitions, sets, distance or height where relevant, rest, surface/equipment, perceived quality, effort, readiness note, technique stop reason, and source. Explosive volume remains distinct from ordinary repetition counts.

## Rules

Quality degradation, pain, unstable landing, unsafe surface, inadequate space, or excessive fatigue support stopping or changing the session. More contacts or maximal effort is not automatically better. Progression parameters are individualized and not generated from a generic formula.

## Boundary

Explosive work carries greater consequence than routine low-impact activity and should not be casually inserted by streak, gamification, or AI. Users with injury concerns, relevant conditions, or uncertainty should seek qualified guidance.

## Acceptance criteria

High-impact sessions have explicit readiness and environment checks, reduced/stopped outcomes remain valid, load is not merged with strength volume, and no automated recommendation claims safety clearance.
