# Recovery Signals

## Purpose

Collect contextual signals that may inform a conservative training decision while avoiding medical diagnosis or false readiness certainty.

## Candidate signals

Sleep, soreness, fatigue, motivation/readiness self-report, pain/discomfort, recent load, illness context, resting/device measures where permitted, appetite/general wellbeing, and performance trend. Each retains source, time, baseline context, and uncertainty.

## Rules

No single signal decides readiness. Consumer-device values are estimates. Missing data remains unknown. Concerning pain, acute illness, neurological symptoms, chest symptoms, fainting, severe breathing difficulty, or other urgent concern is outside readiness scoring and follows safety/emergency guidance.

## Output

Use `Normal Context`, `Caution`, `Recovery Favored`, or `Unknown` as non-medical planning cues, with contributing and contradictory signals. Exact thresholds remain provisional.

## Acceptance criteria

Cues are explainable and reversible, cannot claim clearance or diagnosis, prioritize safety, and offer rest/reduction without punitive language.
