# Calisthenics

## Purpose

Track bodyweight strength, holds, movement progressions, assistance, and technique evidence for exercises such as push-ups, pull-ups, dips, squats, and skill-based variations.

## Model

Exercise progression, variation, repetitions or hold duration, set count, range of motion, tempo, assistance/band, added load, technique cues, confidence, discomfort, and evidence. Bodyweight is contextual and not automatically treated as exact external load.

## Rules

Progression levels are user- or coach-configured, not universal. Advancing requires adequate evidence under defined form criteria; completing repetitions alone does not prove control. Skills with inversion, height, unstable support, or high joint demand require appropriate environment and supervision decisions outside the app.

## Progress evidence

Useful evidence includes consistent clean repetitions, controlled range, longer quality holds, reduced assistance, safe harder variations, and video/manual review where explicitly chosen. Video is optional sensitive data.

## Acceptance criteria

Variations remain distinct, assistance is preserved, progress is technique-aware, and the product never auto-unlocks or prescribes a riskier skill from counts alone.
