# Sleep Integration

## Purpose

Use sleep as a protected planning and recovery input without diagnosing sleep disorders or claiming exact readiness from consumer data.

## Inputs

User-defined sleep window/target, manual duration and quality, schedule constraint, wearable estimates where permitted, recent pattern, interruptions, and user-reported fatigue. Manual and device values remain distinct.

## Rules

Protected sleep cannot be shortened automatically to fit training. Poor or uncertain sleep may trigger conservative options: reduce intensity/volume, switch modality, recover, or keep the plan with an explicit user choice. One night does not prove a medical condition or exact performance capacity.

## Privacy

Sleep data is sensitive health-adjacent information. Provider access requires explicit scope and purpose. Raw device data is minimized.

## Boundary

Persistent sleep difficulty, concerning symptoms, or medical questions require appropriate professional support; the app provides no diagnosis or treatment.

## Acceptance criteria

Sleep constraints are enforced before scheduling, uncertainty is visible, wearable estimates are not treated as clinical measures, and training never silently displaces protected sleep.
