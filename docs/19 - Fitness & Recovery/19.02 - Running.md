# Running

## Purpose

Represent walking/running sessions, intervals, distance, pace, effort, route context, and recovery without turning generic data into an individualized training prescription.

## Record model

Session type, planned/actual intervals, duration, distance, pace or speed, perceived effort, terrain, elevation where available, surface, footwear note, weather context, heart-rate/device data when permitted, discomfort, and evidence source.

## Rules

Distance, pace, and heart rate retain units and device/manual provenance. Stops, pauses, and unknown GPS segments remain explicit. Personal records require comparable conditions and do not automatically raise future targets. Running is higher-impact than many moderate activities; increases should be gradual and responsive to individual context.

## Safety

The user can stop for pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty beyond expected exertion, unsafe traffic/weather, or other concern. The app provides no emergency assessment or return-to-run clearance. Prior injury and health conditions warrant appropriate professional guidance.

## Acceptance criteria

Records are unit-safe and explainable, GPS anomalies are flagged, progress does not auto-escalate, and safety/recovery always outrank plan completion.
