# Health & Safety Boundaries

## Purpose

Define non-negotiable limits so Fitness & Recovery supports safe recordkeeping without posing as medical, rehabilitation, or emergency guidance.

## Product boundary

Performance Buddy OS does not diagnose conditions, evaluate injuries, prescribe rehabilitation, clear return to sport, interpret clinical tests, or replace a qualified clinician or coach. General public-health guidance is not an individualized program.

## Immediate controls

The user can stop, reduce, pause, or replace any session without friction or streak penalty. Pain, injury concern, acute illness, fainting, chest symptoms, severe/unusual breathing difficulty, neurological symptoms, unsafe environment/equipment, or professional advice overrides plans. For urgent or severe symptoms, seek local emergency help; the app cannot assess urgency.

## Conservative design rules

No automatic maximal tests, forced progression, unsafe catch-up, diagnosis from wearables, or AI-generated clearance. Recommendations state evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, and missing context. High-impact/explosive work receives stronger warnings than ordinary low-impact activity.

## Sources

General baseline: [WHO Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240014886), [CDC Adult Activity Guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html), and the [Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity/media/pdfs/Physical_Activity_Guidelines_2nd_edition.pdf).

## Acceptance criteria

Safety overrides cannot be bypassed by AI or gamification, emergency limitations are clear, sensitive data is protected, and no feature makes a medical claim without appropriate regulated scope and evidence.
