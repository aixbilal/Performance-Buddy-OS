# Fitness Evidence

## Purpose

Define what records support claims about completed training and physical progress while preserving uncertainty and modality context.

## Evidence types

User attestation, structured sets/intervals, device import, route/distance record, timed result, technique review, standardized test, coach/qualified feedback, and repeated comparable performance. Each has different strength and limitations.

## Rules

Time alone does not prove workout quality. Device data is not automatically more authoritative than a corrected manual record. Personal records require comparable method and conditions. Video/photo evidence is optional sensitive data. One maximal effort does not establish sustainable capacity or safety. Duplicate sources reference one event.

## Progress claims

Claims specify dimension, baseline/comparison, conditions, source, uncertainty, and calculation version. Revisions preserve prior evidence. Analytics cannot turn prayer, money, body image, or private health-adjacent data into a moral performance score.

## Acceptance criteria

Every completion/progress claim is traceable, unit-safe, source-aware, correction-friendly, and unable to overstate physiological meaning.
