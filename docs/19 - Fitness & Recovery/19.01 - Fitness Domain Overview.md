# Fitness Domain Overview

## Purpose

Fitness & Recovery coordinates physical training and recovery records while protecting safety, sleep, sustainable progression, and human judgment.

## Core entities

Activity definition, planned workout, exercise, set/interval, fitness session, load record, recovery signal, rest day, progress evidence, safety event, and revision. Domain specializations share these contracts but retain distinct metrics.

## Ownership

Phase 19 owns fitness meaning, load, recovery, and safety. Phase 09 owns session lifecycle; Phase 10 supplies tracker mechanics; Phase 08 schedules within protected constraints; Analytics observes trends without prescribing care.

## Capability

Manual plans/records, local calculations, source-aware imports, validation, and user-configured boundaries are `CORE`. `V2` may identify patterns and propose adjustments only after evaluation. The application is not a medical device, coach, physiotherapist, or emergency service.

## Principles

Any movement can be useful; progression is gradual and individual; training and recovery are both legitimate work; and missed data is unknown rather than failed. Public-health targets may be shown as general references, never personalized prescriptions.

## Acceptance criteria

Every recommendation exposes basis and uncertainty, safety overrides are immediate, rest is first-class, imported data retains provenance, and offline recording remains fully usable.
