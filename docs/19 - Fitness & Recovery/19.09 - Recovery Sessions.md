# Recovery Sessions

## Purpose

Represent intentional low-demand activities that support readiness or comfort without pretending they erase fatigue or injury.

## Examples

Easy walking/cycling, gentle mobility, light technique work, breathing/relaxation, or another user-approved recovery activity may be recorded. The product does not prescribe a universal recovery method.

## Record model

Reason/context, activity, duration, intensity, body response, before/after self-report, discomfort, sleep context, and source. A recovery session is distinct from complete rest.

## Rules

Recovery activity must remain genuinely low-demand for the individual. If it worsens symptoms or fatigue, stopping is valid. Completion does not prove physiological recovery. Recovery sessions cannot compensate mathematically for excessive training or inadequate sleep.

## Planning

The system may propose recovery as an option after high load or poor signals, with uncertainty and alternatives including rest. The user decides; medical or rehabilitation needs route outside the product.

## Acceptance criteria

Recovery sessions remain separate from training volume, before/after reports are not diagnoses, and rest is never penalized relative to active recovery.
