# Phase 19 — Fitness & Recovery

## Purpose

Phase 19 defines safe tracking, planning, evidence, and recovery contracts for running, strength, calisthenics, explosive work, mobility, stretching, wrist/forearm/hand training, rest, sleep, and training load. It does not diagnose, treat, rehabilitate, or replace qualified care.

## Evidence basis

The baseline follows current public-health principles that some activity is better than none, adults should build activity gradually, and individual ability and health context matter. Sources: [WHO physical activity guidelines](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240014886) and [CDC adult activity guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html).

## Invariants

1. Pain, injury concern, acute illness, unsafe conditions, and medical advice override timers, streaks, goals, and AI suggestions.
2. The user can stop or reduce a session immediately without punitive messaging.
3. Training volume is not universal evidence that more is better.
4. Load, readiness, recovery, and progress remain estimates with source and uncertainty.
5. `CORE` records and validates; it does not prescribe individualized medical or rehabilitation plans.
6. AI may propose conservative adjustments but cannot diagnose or clear someone for exercise.
