# Progressive Improvement

## Purpose

Support gradual, evidence-based development of endurance, strength, skill, mobility, and consistency without enforcing constant linear increase.

## Progress dimensions

Technique, control, tolerance, repetitions, load, duration, distance, pace, range, reduced assistance, movement quality, consistency, recovery response, and transferable performance are separate dimensions.

## Rules

Progression can mean increase, hold, deload, simplify, vary, or rest. Change one or a small number of material variables at a time where practical so response can be understood. Plateaus and regressions are context for review, not moral failure. No universal percentage increase is built into `CORE`.

## Evidence and proposals

Comparable sessions and validated performance evidence support progression. AI may suggest a conservative option with reasons and uncertainty, but cannot prescribe, guarantee safety, or bypass user/coach/medical constraints.

## Acceptance criteria

Progress decisions identify evidence and affected variables, unsafe metric chasing is discouraged, deload/rest remains valid, and historical targets are never rewritten.
