# V3 — Personal Performance Intelligence

## Purpose

Define the long-horizon cross-domain intelligence vision and strict safety conditions for recommending the highest-value next action.

## Central question

> Given everything currently known and permitted, what deserves attention next?

V3 considers academic urgency and return, development trajectory, knowledge gaps, language consistency, fitness load, recovery, routines, fixed commitments, goals, and longer horizons. It coordinates context; it does not erase domain ownership.

## Output contract

A V3 recommendation includes proposed next action or allocation, alternatives considered, evidence and provenance, current goals, constraints, uncertainty, domain trade-offs, deterministic validation, and why the recommendation changed from prior guidance.

## Invariants

Protected constraints remain hard boundaries. Academic formulas and policy remain deterministic. Private data access remains permissioned. The human retains final control. V3 cannot claim a complete model of the user or reduce life to a single objective function.

## Architecture discipline

Start with native orchestration and the simplest reasoning structure that meets evaluation needs. Specialist or multi-agent designs require evidence that a simpler call or sequential pipeline is insufficient.

## Promotion prerequisites

Mature CORE, reliable provenance, stable domain contracts, V1/V2 outcome history, cross-domain safety tests, explainability UX, cost/latency controls, and rollback/deactivation paths.

## Status

V3 is a planned long-term capability, not an initial-release claim. Until promotion gates pass, examples remain design targets.
