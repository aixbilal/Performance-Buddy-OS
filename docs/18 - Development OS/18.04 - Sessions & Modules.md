# Sessions & Modules

## Purpose

Connect Phase 09 development sessions to learning modules and projects without confusing activity time with module completion.

## Session purposes

Learn, guided practice, independent exercise, build, debug, test, refactor, review, architecture, documentation, and maintenance are distinct purposes. A session may support a module and project while its time is counted once.

## Start contract

Select module/project context, intended outcome, assistance mode, evidence target, and bounded scope. “Code for one hour” is insufficient when a testable or explainable outcome can be defined.

## Close contract

Record actual outcome, implementation status, verification status, evidence references, blockers, concepts used, assistance, and next action. `Implemented but unverified` remains distinct from complete.

## Rules

Long builds and background processes do not inflate active time. Unplanned debugging is valid and may be linked later. A session can produce partial evidence without advancing the whole module.

## Acceptance criteria

Every module-linked session is traceable, honest about verification and assistance, recoverable after interruption, and unable to claim skill from time alone.
