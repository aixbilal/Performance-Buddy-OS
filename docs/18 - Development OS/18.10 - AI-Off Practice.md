# AI-Off Practice

## Purpose

Create deliberate intervals for independent recall, implementation, debugging, and architecture reasoning before optional AI review.

## Modes

From-scratch implementation, code reading/explanation, debugging without hints, algorithm/problem practice, architecture sketch, test design, and refactoring can be configured as AI-off checkpoints.

## Behavior

Phase 09 disables generative hints, solutions, rewrites, and proactive coaching for the declared interval while preserving local tools, documentation explicitly allowed by the exercise, timer, notes, tests, and save/recovery. Questions may be parked for later.

## Evidence

Record scope, allowed resources, original attempt, tests/results, blockers, exit reason, and later review separately. AI-off status is contextual evidence, not proof of mastery or integrity, and the product cannot police outside tools.

## Rules

Difficulty should be productive rather than artificial. Exiting early is allowed without shame. Post-practice assistance cannot modify the preserved original attempt.

## Acceptance criteria

The mode is visible and reversible, increases no data collection, preserves the attempt, and supports evidence-based comparison without punitive ranking.
