# 27.08 — Workload Analysis

## Purpose

Assess recorded demand and capacity across approved windows so overload, imbalance, and maintenance needs can be reviewed before schedule proposals are made.

## Inputs

Fixed commitments, travel, planned and actual sessions, deadlines, estimated remaining work, flexible actions, study modes, development paths, fitness load, recovery/rest, protected sleep, routines, unknown time, and user-configured capacity. Money is not workload or performance input.

## Rules

Reconcile overlaps and avoid double-counting multi-domain links. Separate wall-clock, active effort, cognitive demand, physical load, and uncertainty. Missing time remains unknown. Exam/recovery/university modes change comparison context. Protected sleep and safety boundaries are constraints, not spare capacity.

Output shows demand, available capacity, variance, bottlenecks, displaced work, data gaps, and scenarios. V2 may propose reducing, moving, splitting, deferring, or entering maintenance where domain rules allow; it cannot silently sacrifice one domain.

## Acceptance criteria

- Totals reconcile to source intervals and display unknown/overlap explicitly.
- Stress tests preserve fixed commitments, sleep, recovery, and domain safety limits.
- Every workload recommendation names trade-offs and validates alternatives through Phase 23.
