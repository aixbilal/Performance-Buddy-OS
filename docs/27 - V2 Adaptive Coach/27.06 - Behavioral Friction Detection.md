# 27.06 — Behavioral Friction Detection

## Purpose

Identify repeated obstacles in systems, context, plans, sessions, or product workflows so the user can test improvements without blame.

## Candidate signals

Repeated deferral, underestimation, start delay, interruption clusters, context switching, unresolved blockers, reminder dismissal, failed capture/save, prerequisite mismatch, unverified completion, or recurring recovery need may qualify when evidence thresholds are met.

## Interpretation rules

Separate technical/product friction, schedule design, capacity, external constraints, and unknown causes. Missing records do not imply avoidance. Friction is not laziness, lack of discipline, a mental-health condition, or a moral failure. Private reasons are optional.

Output includes window, occurrences and counterexamples, affected workflow, coverage, possible causes, uncertainty, and one or more reversible experiments such as smaller scope, different timing, buffer, tooling change, or seeking human help. Experiments are proposals with review dates, not silent configuration changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every friction claim traces to inspectable events and a versioned method.
- False positives can be dismissed without shame or repeated notification pressure.
- Suggestions are bounded, reversible, approved, and evaluated without blaming non-adherence.
