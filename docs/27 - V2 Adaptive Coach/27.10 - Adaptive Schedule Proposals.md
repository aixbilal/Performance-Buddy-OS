# 27.10 — Adaptive Schedule Proposals

## Purpose

Convert validated V2 findings into bounded schedule-change proposals that respect Phase 08 planning and Phase 23 constraints.

## Proposal contract

Include triggering findings, cited window/evidence, current schedule versions, proposed before/after blocks, expected benefit, uncertainty, protected constraints, fixed/flexible status, displaced work, trade-offs, alternatives, reason codes, validation result, expiration, and review date.

## Rules

Prefer the smallest reversible change supported by evidence. Preserve fixed commitments, protected sleep, travel, recovery, deadlines, and domain priority rules. A proposal to increase one domain states what loses capacity. Maintenance modes—such as language learning during exams—follow owned rules, not ad hoc deletion.

The user may accept, edit, reject, or defer through Phase 26 approval. Editing or changed schedule state triggers revalidation. No scheduled analysis may commit its own proposal.

## Acceptance criteria

- Alternatives are independently validated and all displaced work is visible.
- Stale, rejected, deferred, blocked, and expired proposals cause no mutation.
- Outcome review compares the accepted change with a defined baseline without claiming causality from one trial.
