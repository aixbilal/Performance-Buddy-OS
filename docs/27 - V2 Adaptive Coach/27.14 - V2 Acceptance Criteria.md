# 27.14 — V2 Acceptance Criteria

## Release gate

V2 requires all Phase 24.17 and V1 control gates plus evidence that historical analysis provides useful signal beyond deterministic analytics without unacceptable false positives, privacy risk, or pressure.

## Evidence-quality gates

- Analysis manifests are reproducible from cited source revisions.
- Window, coverage, missingness, confounders, alternatives, and uncertainty are visible.
- Correlation language never implies unsupported causation.
- Pattern/risk thresholds are predefined and evaluated on temporally valid holdouts.

## Safety and control gates

- Private categories are excluded by default and independently consented.
- No universal life/performance score, diagnosis, moral judgment, or hidden cross-domain trade exists.
- Every meaningful adaptation passes Phase 23 and fresh Phase 26 approval.
- Scheduled runs cannot write, self-schedule indefinitely, or overwhelm the user.

## Outcome and reliability gates

- Precision, recall, calibration, false-alert burden, usefulness, dismissal, and adverse outcomes meet declared thresholds.
- Provider/runtime failure, stale inputs, deletion, cancellation, and permission revocation fail safely.
- Recommendation memory is inspectable, correctable, forgettable, scoped, and reversible.

## Promotion evidence

Store methods, prompts, schemas, models/providers, datasets, hardware where relevant, thresholds, results, limitations, human review, and rollback version. Mandatory failures block promotion under 02.14.

## Acceptance criteria

- A signed report maps every V2 claim to reproducible evaluation evidence.
- Zero unresolved critical permission, approval, data-integrity, causal-language, or protected-constraint defects remain.
- V2 can be disabled or rolled back without affecting trusted CORE records or V1 availability.
