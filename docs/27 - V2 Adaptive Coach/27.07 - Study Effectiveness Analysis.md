# 27.07 — Study Effectiveness Analysis

## Purpose

Compare study activity with learning evidence and assessment evidence to suggest better allocation or methods without equating hours with learning.

## Evidence

Eligible evidence includes topic coverage, class exposure, focus sessions, retrieval practice, test attempts, mistake categories, knowledge confidence evidence, assignments/quizzes, assessment results, source freshness, and course context. Official/personal academic authority rules remain unchanged.

## Analysis

Align study activity to topics and outcomes; account for difficulty, assessment timing, prior exposure, assistance mode, missing attempts, and small samples; compare compatible methods/windows; then state observed associations and alternatives. AI-off practice and independent attempts remain distinguishable from assisted work.

V2 may propose a bounded experiment—such as more retrieval practice on a topic—with target evidence and review date. It cannot claim a method caused a grade, invent mastery, calculate GPA itself, or guarantee improvement.

## Acceptance criteria

- Time, completion, confidence, and demonstrated knowledge remain separate measures.
- Academic calculations exactly use Phase 13 CORE outputs.
- Sparse, confounded, or mismatched topic evidence causes qualification or abstention.
