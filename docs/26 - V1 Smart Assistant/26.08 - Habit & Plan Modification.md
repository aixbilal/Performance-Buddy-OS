# 26.08 — Habit and Plan Modification

## Scope

V1 can propose creating, editing, pausing, resuming, or rescheduling trackers, routines, goals, systems, actions, milestones, and daily-plan items in response to a user request.

## Domain rules

Tracker definitions and completion semantics remain owned by Phase 10; goals and paths by Phase 11; daily plans and modes by Phase 08; routines by Phase 20. V1 invokes those contracts rather than inventing new states. It never marks evidence-based work complete from conversation alone.

## Proposal checks

Resolve recurrence, date/time/timezone, duration, target, priority, fixed/flexible status, dependencies, and affected future occurrences. Preview whether a change modifies one instance or the series. Validate capacity, protected sleep, fixed commitments, conflicts, routine flexibility, and domain permissions. Private routine categories receive category-specific access and disclosure.

V1 may explain tradeoffs but does not detect long-term patterns or autonomously rebalance domains; that belongs to V2.

## Acceptance criteria

- Series edits, exceptions, pause/resume, timezone, and completion-evidence cases are tested.
- A proposal identifies all affected entities and future occurrences before approval.
- Rejection, invalidation, or failed commit leaves the prior habit/plan intact.
