# 26.09 — Academic Assistance

## Scope

V1 may explain courses, assessments, academic status, prerequisite paths, study requirements, and scenarios using permitted official sources, personal academic records, knowledge evidence, and deterministic calculators. It may draft study actions or schedule proposals for approval.

## Authority

Official institutional sources govern curriculum and policy; personal records govern the user's entered results; Phase 13 CORE engines calculate weighted scores, GPA, CGPA, and projections. The model may explain calculator inputs and outputs but never invent policy, marks, grading rules, or arithmetic.

## Assistance patterns

- answer “what is due/current?” with source and freshness;
- invoke CORE grade or projection scenarios and explain assumptions;
- retrieve notes and identify covered sources without claiming mastery;
- propose a study block tied to topics and evidence;
- state uncertainty when official and personal sources conflict.

Generated academic advice is not a guarantee and does not alter enrollment, marks, attempts, or official records without the normal import/correction workflow.

## Acceptance criteria

- Known calculation fixtures exactly match Phase 13 CORE outputs.
- Missing policy/source data triggers `RESEARCH REQUIRED` or clarification, not invention.
- Private records, instructor material, and vault sources obey their permissions and citation rules.
