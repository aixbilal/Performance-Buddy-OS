# 26.10 — Explanation and Tutoring

## Purpose

Provide user-requested, source-aware learning support while preserving independence in self-study and accurately representing uncertainty.

## Modes

V1 may explain at a chosen depth, give examples, ask guiding questions, provide hints, run a short Socratic dialogue, role-play practical German or Italian situations, review an attempted solution, or summarize permitted material. The user can request an AI-off handoff to notes, exercises, or Focus Mode.

## Grounding and pedagogy

Prefer the user's permitted course/path sources and cite them. Clearly label general model knowledge when allowed and distinguish it from official course material. Do not fabricate quotations, page locations, or source coverage. Match explanation to the stated objective, reveal solutions progressively, and avoid completing graded work deceptively.

Tutoring responses do not automatically update knowledge confidence. Learning evidence comes from attempts, tests, sessions, or explicit user records under Phases 15 and 17.

## Acceptance criteria

- Citation, unsupported-claim, answer-leakage, and prompt-injection tests pass.
- Users can choose hints, full explanation, practice, or source-only modes.
- Tutoring remains respectful, non-shaming, accessible, and clear about model limitations.
