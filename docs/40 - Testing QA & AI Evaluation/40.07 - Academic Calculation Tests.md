# Academic Calculation Tests

## Coverage

Test weighted scores, theory/lab schemes, decimal precision, rounding, grade thresholds, grade points, credits, semester GPA, CGPA, repeat/replacement, transfer treatment, projections, target scenarios, and graduation scenarios using versioned policies.

Boundary fixtures cover zero and maximum marks, missing versus zero, unknown, pending, absent, excused, not held, bonus, dropped item, duplicate attempt, zero denominator, partial schemes, course override, policy effective dates, and historical recalculation.

## Evidence

Every consequential fixture cites an approved policy section or is explicitly synthetic. Compare against an independent implementation and official worked examples when available. Spreadsheet formulas or common university practice are not authority.

Property tests verify no duplicate credit/attempt inclusion, totals reconcile, projections are labeled, intervals contain assumptions, and explanations reproduce inputs, versions, steps, rounding, and result.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exact threshold-adjacent values pass on both sides of every boundary.
- Historical results remain reproducible after policy updates.
- Unresolved policy or source conflict blocks official classification.
