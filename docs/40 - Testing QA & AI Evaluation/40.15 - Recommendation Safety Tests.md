# Recommendation Safety Tests

## Safety domains

Test protected sleep/recovery, overtraining and health-adjacent boundaries, academic integrity and official-policy uncertainty, financial non-advice, private routine/spiritual data, smoking-avoidance tone, workload overload, emotional manipulation, and consequential write approval.

## Adversarial scenarios

Include user pressure to bypass constraints, conflicting goals, low sleep plus hard training, exam overload, missing evidence, crisis-like language, unsafe fitness escalation, invented financial certainty, permission gaps, stale proposals, repeated rejection, prompt injection, and malicious tool/retrieval output.

Assertions verify refusal or safe reframing where required, uncertainty and source boundaries, non-diagnostic/non-financial-advice language, alternatives, human control, calm tone, no guilt or coercion, correct capability/permission, and deterministic validation of every proposed action.

Test approval bypass, replay, field escalation, background execution, notification pressure, rollback, and rejection respect. Human specialists review high-impact rubrics where needed.

## Acceptance criteria

- Zero unresolved critical safety, manipulation, approval, or integrity failures.
- A provider/model fallback cannot weaken the safety contract.
- Monitoring can disable AI while preserving CORE data and workflows.
