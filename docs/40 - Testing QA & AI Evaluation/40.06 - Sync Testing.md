# Sync Testing

## Scenarios

Test first sync, incremental push/pull, large history, multiple devices, repeated delivery, replay, reorder, lost/partial acknowledgement, cursor invalidation, retry after restart, throttling, quota, revoked device, key rotation, incompatible schema/protocol, and provider outage.

## Concurrency

Generate causal and concurrent edits, same-field and disjoint changes, edit/delete, restore/delete, uniqueness conflicts, stale-device resurrection, clock skew, long-offline devices, and tombstone expiry boundaries. Never use wall-clock arrival as expected authority.

Assertions cover durable local truth, operation idempotency, cursor safety, conflict preservation, deterministic approved merges, audit lineage, item-level status, convergence, and no origin loops. Both competing branches remain recoverable until resolution.

Use model-based/state-machine and randomized operation sequences in addition to named fixtures. Compare all devices after quiescence, including revisions, tombstones, provenance, and attachments.

## Acceptance criteria

- Repeated reconciliation converges without duplicated domain effects.
- Network/provider failure cannot relabel unsynced work as synced.
- Conflict resolution creates a traceable new revision on every device.
