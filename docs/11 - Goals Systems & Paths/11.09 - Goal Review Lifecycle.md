# Goal Review Lifecycle

## Purpose

Goal review verifies that a goal remains relevant, feasible, correctly defined, and supported by useful systems and evidence.

## Lifecycle states

`Draft`, `Active`, `Maintenance`, `Paused`, `Achieved`, `Retired`, and `Cancelled` are goal lifecycle states. They are distinct from occurrence completion states. A goal may return from Paused or Maintenance to Active through an auditable decision.

## Review cadence

Each goal declares a review interval or trigger. Reviews may also occur after milestone completion, deadline change, sustained conflict, major evidence correction, mode change, or user request. Missing a review creates a pending review, not a failed goal.

## Review inputs

Current success definition, evidence trajectory, system adherence, milestones, capacity, conflicts, changed assumptions, owner-domain risk, and user intent are considered. Private evidence is shown only within its permission scope.

## Outcomes

Continue, revise scope, change system, reprioritize, enter maintenance, pause, mark achieved with evidence, retire as no longer relevant, or cancel. Semantic changes create a new effective version and retain history.

## AI boundary

`V2` may prepare an evidence-grounded review and proposals. It cannot mark achievement, diagnose motivation, or rewrite goals without approval.

## Acceptance criteria

Every review records inputs, decision, actor, date, next review, and affected plans; valid adaptation is not framed as failure.
