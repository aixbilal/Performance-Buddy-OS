# Goal Progress Evidence

## Principle

Goal progress is a derived claim supported by traceable evidence. Activity, time, streaks, and task counts may be useful inputs but are not universally equivalent to outcome progress.

## Evidence sources

Evidence may include owner-domain results, milestone decisions, accepted artifacts, assessment outcomes, tracker records, session outcomes, external-source references, and user attestations where permitted. Each item retains source, time, actor, confidence or verification state, and definition version.

## Calculation

Every progress projection declares included evidence, weighting or aggregation rule, calculation version, and timestamp. Unknown or incompatible data is excluded visibly rather than converted to zero. Multiple references to one fact are deduplicated through stable provenance.

## Corrections

Source corrections invalidate or recompute dependent projections. Historical projections may be retained for audit but are not presented as current truth. Goal completion requires the goal’s explicit success contract and owner validation.

## AI boundary

AI may summarize evidence or propose a progress interpretation. It must separate fact, inference, and recommendation; cite the records used; show missing context; and cannot certify its own output as user achievement.

## Acceptance criteria

Every progress display is explainable, reproducible, domain-aware, privacy-respecting, and resistant to double counting or elapsed-time inflation.
