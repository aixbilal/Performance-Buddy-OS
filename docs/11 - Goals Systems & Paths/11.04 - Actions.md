# Actions

## Purpose

An action is the smallest independently executable planning unit. It tells the user what can be done, under which context, and how completion will be recognized.

## Required contract

An action has a title, owner domain, lifecycle state, source, optional parent links, completion definition, and evidence requirement. It may include effort estimate, duration estimate, deadline, earliest start, location, energy need, prerequisites, and fixed/flexible classification.

## Lifecycle

`Draft → Ready → Planned/In Progress → Complete`, with legitimate alternatives `Partial`, `Deferred`, `Skipped`, `Excused`, `Cancelled`, and `Unknown`. Phase 08 owns schedule placement; Phase 09 owns session execution; the owner domain validates completion.

## Rules

- Actions must be specific enough to start and finish.
- Duration elapsed is not proof of completion.
- Splitting preserves the parent reference and prevents double counting.
- Repeating behavior should normally originate from a system rather than copied actions.
- Dependency cycles and impossible date constraints are rejected.

## AI boundary

AI may draft, split, or reword actions and propose metadata. The preview shows assumptions; meaningful creation, movement, or completion requires approval and deterministic validation.

## Acceptance criteria

Ready actions are executable, traceable, feasible under known constraints, and recoverable after edits or failed saves.
