# Goal System Action Model

## Purpose

The model separates long-term intent from repeated practice and immediate execution so plans remain meaningful and progress claims remain honest.

## Canonical entities

- `Goal`: a desired outcome or sustained direction with scope and success evidence.
- `System`: a repeatable process intended to influence one or more goals.
- `Action`: a concrete executable commitment with a clear stopping condition.
- `Milestone`: an evidence-bearing checkpoint inside a goal.
- `LearningPath`: an ordered or branching structure for developing a capability.

## Relationships

A goal may be supported by several systems and milestones. A system may support multiple goals without duplicating its records. An action may originate from a system, milestone, learning-path unit, or direct commitment. Links use stable IDs and effective dates. Circular dependencies are rejected.

## State and provenance

Each entity has an owner domain, lifecycle state, definition version, privacy class, creation source, and revision history. An action completing does not automatically complete its parent goal. Derived progress records inputs, calculation rule, and time.

## Capability boundary

Creation, linking, lifecycle validation, and deterministic projections are `CORE`. `V1` may draft structures; `V2` may detect drift; `V3` may propose cross-domain allocation. Meaningful writes require preview and human approval.

## Acceptance criteria

Every action traces to its context when one exists, every progress claim is explainable, owner boundaries are preserved, and deleting or archiving a parent never silently destroys evidence.
