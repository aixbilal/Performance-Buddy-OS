# Goal Priority

## Purpose

Goal priority determines relative attention across longer horizons while respecting the contextual hierarchy owned by the Personal Operating Model. It is an input to planning, not a command that bypasses feasibility or human choice.

## Ordering model

Hard filters apply first: safety, protected sleep and recovery, fixed commitments, prerequisites, and unavailable capacity. Feasible goals may then be compared using consequence, deadline, current risk, user importance, strategic relevance, trajectory, neglect, opportunity window, expected value, and resource cost.

## Priority representation

`CORE` supports user-set bands such as `Critical`, `High`, `Normal`, `Low`, and `Paused`, plus dominant reasons and effective dates. Exact scoring weights remain governed by Phase 08 and must not be invented here. A domain is not permanently privileged regardless of context.

## Rules

Secondary learning paths may rise when core areas are on trajectory or a deadline makes them strategically relevant. Money, prayer, reflection, and private data cannot be collapsed into a moral performance score. Missing data does not lower priority automatically.

## AI boundary

`V2` or `V3` may recommend reordering with evidence, alternatives, uncertainty, and trade-offs. The user approves meaningful changes.

## Acceptance criteria

Priority is explainable, constraint-safe, stable against irrelevant data, contextual rather than rigid, and separable from today’s schedule.
