# Portfolio Presentation

## Objective

Present Performance Buddy OS as evidence of disciplined product thinking, engineering, learning, and validation—not as a misleading claim of commercial readiness or autonomous AI.

## Portfolio narrative

The story should explain the real personal problem, research and requirements, domain boundaries, offline-first design, deterministic rules, data and migration strategy, privacy, human-approved AI, testing, recovery, accessibility, and evidence-driven trade-offs. The owner should be able to explain consequential code and decisions independently.

Useful evidence includes approved ADRs, architecture diagrams, concise domain flows, representative screenshots, synthetic demonstrations, test and evaluation summaries, migration/restore exercises, performance measurements, accessibility checks, and a frank limitations section. Feature count, generated-code volume, and model novelty are weak evidence.

## Safety and honesty

Use synthetic, anonymized, or specifically consented data. Never expose personal academic records, reflections, finances, spiritual routines, credentials, private notes, provider keys, or identifiable diagnostic logs. Clearly label prototypes, mockups, deferred capabilities, simulated AI output, and incomplete platform support.

Do not claim users, uptime, security certification, compliance, accuracy, supported institutions, or production readiness without evidence. Attribution and licenses apply to references, libraries, visual assets, and AI assistance.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every public claim is demonstrable from a versioned artifact or evidence record.
- Public materials contain no unauthorized personal or sensitive data.
- Current, prototype, and `FUTURE` capabilities are visibly distinguished.
- The owner can explain the system, trade-offs, failures, and lessons without relying on generated marketing text.
