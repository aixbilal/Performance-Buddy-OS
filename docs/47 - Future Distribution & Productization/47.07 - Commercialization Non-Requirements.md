# Commercialization Non-Requirements

## Purpose

Prevent imagined commercial needs from distorting the personal product or being presented as approved scope.

## Not required for CORE

CORE does not require subscriptions, billing, trials, entitlements, licensing servers, advertisements, growth analytics, referrals, marketplaces, organization plans, seat management, sales administration, customer support consoles, public status pages, service-level agreements, legal-policy automation, tax handling, regional hosting, anti-fraud systems, or marketing websites.

It also does not require remote feature flags, compulsory telemetry, cloud accounts, data harvesting, engagement manipulation, or reduced exportability. Commercial convenience cannot weaken local ownership, user consent, privacy zones, AI approval, deterministic correctness, accessibility, backup, deletion, or portability.

## Proposal rules

A future commercial proposal must state the user benefit, buyer and user relationship, business model, data incentives, costs, legal and support obligations, accessibility impact, failure and exit behavior, and evidence that a simpler distribution form is insufficient. Pricing, taxes, consumer rules, app-store terms, privacy obligations, and vendor costs are time-sensitive `RESEARCH REQUIRED` items.

Revenue potential alone does not approve multi-user or hosted architecture. Dark patterns, punitive retention, misleading AI claims, sale of sensitive performance data, and employer or institution surveillance remain outside product principles.

## Acceptance criteria

- Commercial assumptions create no current dependencies or acceptance criteria.
- Future proposals expose incentives, obligations, and data impact.
- Users retain understandable export, cancellation, and deletion paths.
- Product principles constrain commercialization rather than being rewritten for it.
