# Course Attempts

## Purpose

A Course Attempt records one student-specific enrollment in a course offering and preserves its history independently from the course definition.

## Fields

Stable attempt ID, student/program relationship, course and offering IDs, term, registration status, attempt sequence, credit basis, outcome status, official grade reference when available, source, verification status, and revisions.

## Rules

- Each enrollment is a separate attempt; later attempts do not overwrite earlier ones.
- Attempt sequence is derived within a verified course/equivalence context.
- Marks and grade calculations are referenced from Phase 13.
- Withdrawn, incomplete, transferred, audited, and other statuses require official definitions before consequential use.
- Imported duplicates are quarantined until matched.

## Corrections

Corrections retain original input, replacement value, actor, reason, time, and source. A newly issued transcript may supersede a personal capture but does not erase provenance.

## Privacy

Attempts and grades are sensitive personal records. Cloud or AI access requires explicit scoped permission.

## Acceptance criteria

The complete attempt history is reconstructable, duplicates are detectable, unknown status remains unresolved, and consumers cannot mutate attempts directly.
