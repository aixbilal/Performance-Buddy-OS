# Current Enrollment

## Purpose

Current Enrollment is the verified personal snapshot of courses and offerings in which the user is presently registered or otherwise participating.

## Model

Each entry links student term, course, offering, course attempt, registration status, section when needed, schedule references, instructor only when useful, source, captured time, and verification state.

## Sources

An official portal or registration document is stronger evidence than a manually entered plan. User confirmation can establish personal working context but cannot establish general policy. Schedule screenshots or AI extraction remain drafts until reviewed.

## Reconciliation

Imports compare stable identifiers and preserve additions, drops, section changes, and conflicts. Removed enrollment is not deleted; its status and effective date change. Current views exclude unresolved duplicates while surfacing them for review.

## Interactions

Phase 08 receives fixed university commitments and deadlines. Phase 13 receives active assessment containers. Phase 14 receives current course demand. These consumers cannot modify enrollment directly.

## Acceptance criteria

The snapshot has an as-of time, source, and verification state; changes are auditable; offline use is supported; and sensitive identifiers are minimized.
