# Prerequisite Risk Analysis

## Purpose

Prerequisite risk analysis identifies how current or projected course outcomes may affect access to later courses. It is decision support, not an official eligibility determination.

## Inputs

Verified prerequisite graph, current enrollment, completed attempts, unresolved results, planned offerings where sourced, curriculum placement, and scenario outcomes from Phase 13. Missing inputs remain explicit.

## Risk classes

`Blocked`, `At Risk`, `Clear`, and `Unknown` are descriptive analysis states. Each result names the affected downstream course, dependency path, evidence, assumptions, time horizon, and rule version.

## Rules

Hard verified failures or missing prerequisites may support `Blocked`. Scenario grades support only projected risk. Unknown equivalence or policy cannot produce false clearance. Risk severity considers downstream breadth and timing but does not calculate study allocation; Phase 14 owns that decision.

## AI boundary

`CORE` traverses verified graphs. `V2` may explain patterns and propose review actions, but cannot change academic records or claim official registration eligibility.

## Acceptance criteria

Risk is reproducible, source-linked, scenario-labeled, uncertainty-aware, and never presented as a university ruling.
