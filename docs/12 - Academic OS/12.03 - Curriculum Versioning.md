# Curriculum Versioning

## Purpose

Curriculum versioning preserves the exact course and prerequisite structure applicable to a cohort while allowing later official revisions without rewriting history.

## Version identity

A curriculum version records program, official title or identifier, publication/effective dates, applicable cohort/campus conditions, source references, verification status, supersession relationship, and content checksum or snapshot reference.

## Rules

- Published revisions create new versions unless the authority explicitly labels a clerical correction.
- Courses, semester placement, credit hours, prerequisites, and requirement groups are effective-dated members.
- Personal attempts retain the curriculum context applicable when taken.
- A newer curriculum does not automatically migrate a student.
- Migration requires an official mapping or explicitly provisional user-approved scenario.

## Conflict handling

Conflicting official documents are preserved with capture dates and applicability claims. Dependent enrollment or calculation remains unresolved until the authoritative version is confirmed.

## Acceptance criteria

Any historical degree view can be reconstructed, source changes are auditable, migration cannot occur silently, and unresolved applicability blocks false certainty.
