# Personal Academic Record Import

## Purpose

Import transcripts, score sheets, registrations, schedules, and user records as sensitive evidence about the user’s history without treating them as institution-wide policy.

## Pipeline

Capture source class, owner, date, document type, checksum, and permission; preserve the original securely; extract candidate identity, term, course, credits, marks, grade, and status; match against versioned academic entities; validate; preview differences; and require confirmation before publishing.

## Matching and conflicts

Course code, title, term, attempt, and curriculum context participate in matching. Ambiguous matches remain quarantined. A transcript may supersede a manual personal entry for current display while both sources and correction history remain preserved.

## Privacy

Minimize student identifiers, redact exports where possible, encrypt according to platform policy, and require explicit consent before cloud or external AI processing. Logs must not expose raw marks or identifiers unnecessarily.

## Failure and recovery

Import is transactional and idempotent by source checksum plus importer version. Validation failure leaves approved records unchanged. The user can resume review or discard only the candidate import.

## Acceptance criteria

No record is committed without preview, provenance, and validation; duplicates and ambiguity are visible; and imported evidence never establishes general CUI policy.
