# Eight-Semester Degree Model

## Purpose

Represent the approved eight-semester planning view for the user’s degree while keeping official duration and course placement source-dependent.

## Model

The view contains eight ordinal semester slots linked to one curriculum version. Each slot may contain required courses, electives or requirement groups, recommended sequence, and curriculum credit totals. Slots describe curriculum placement, not the user’s actual attempt history.

## Rules

- “Eight-semester” is a view configuration, not proof that every student completes in eight terms.
- Summer, repeated, withdrawn, transferred, or delayed courses remain outside or alongside the canonical slots as personal attempts.
- A course moved between curriculum versions retains separate placements.
- Elective choices reference requirement groups and do not replace official options with guesses.

## Progress view

The system can overlay passed, current, planned, blocked, and remaining status using personal evidence. Overlay status never changes the curriculum structure. Credit totals identify whether they are curriculum requirements, attempted credits, earned credits, or projected credits.

## Acceptance criteria

The eight slots reproduce the verified curriculum, personal history remains distinct, exceptional terms are representable, and no duration guarantee is implied.
