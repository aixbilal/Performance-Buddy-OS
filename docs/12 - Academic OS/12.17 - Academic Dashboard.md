# Academic Dashboard

## Purpose

The Academic Dashboard provides a calm, source-aware view of current enrollment, degree structure, attempts, prerequisites, and academic risks without becoming a competing source of truth.

## Required sections

- current term and verified enrollment with as-of time;
- curriculum progress by requirement and credit category;
- course status and attempt history;
- prerequisite blockers, risks, and unknowns;
- upcoming assessments and deadlines from owning records;
- recent academic evidence and unresolved imports;
- links to Phase 13 grade calculations and Phase 14 study planning.

## Presentation rules

Official facts, personal records, derived values, scenarios, and unresolved data use distinct labels. Every total or risk opens to inputs, source, rule version, and timestamp. Unknown values display gaps, not zeros. Grades and risks use neutral language rather than shame or false reassurance.

## Privacy and accessibility

Sensitive marks and identifiers can be concealed. The dashboard supports keyboard navigation, screen-reader summaries, non-color state cues, and reduced motion. Export respects permission and redaction choices.

## Capability boundary

`CORE` renders deterministic local projections. `V2` may add evidence-grounded explanations; it cannot edit records or claim official standing.

## Acceptance criteria

The dashboard is offline-capable, traceable, current as of a visible time, responsive to corrected data, and honest about unresolved CUI policy.
