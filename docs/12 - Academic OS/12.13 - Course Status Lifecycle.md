# Course Status Lifecycle

## Purpose

Define course-level status across curriculum requirement, enrollment, attempts, and final satisfaction without confusing these distinct concerns.

## Canonical states

`Not Eligible`, `Eligible`, `Planned`, `Enrolled`, `In Progress`, `Completed`, `Passed`, `Failed`, `Withdrawn`, `Repeated`, `Transferred/Exempt`, and `Unresolved` may appear where the applicable owner and official policy support them.

## State rules

Eligibility derives from prerequisites. Planned is not enrolled. Completed activity is not necessarily passed. Passed/failed requires a verified outcome rule. Repeated describes a relationship and does not erase the prior outcome. Transferred or exempt requires official recognition.

## Transitions

Transitions store actor, source event, effective time, rule version, and prior state. Invalid transitions are blocked or quarantined for review. Late official corrections recompute current projections while preserving the historical decision trail.

## Presentation

Views identify which layer a status belongs to and use neutral language. `Unresolved` is preferred to a guessed outcome.

## Acceptance criteria

Every state is traceable, contradictory layers are detected, corrections are auditable, and no schedule or AI inference can manufacture an official result.
