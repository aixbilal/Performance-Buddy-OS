# Course Model

## Purpose

Define version-aware academic course identity and separate official catalog facts from offerings, attempts, and knowledge topics.

## Course definition

Fields include stable ID, curriculum version, official code, title, credit structure, course type, requirement group, description where sourced, semester placement, prerequisite references, effective dates, and provenance.

## Identity rules

Course code alone is not globally unique. Identity includes institution/program or catalog context and version. Renamed or recoded courses remain separate unless an official equivalence mapping exists. Theory and lab components may be one course or linked courses according to the official source.

## Boundaries

A `CourseOffering` is a term-specific delivery. A `CourseAttempt` is the user’s enrollment outcome. Knowledge topics and notes belong to Phase 15. Assessment composition and grades belong to Phase 13.

## Validation

Credit values, prerequisites, and requirement classification require official provenance. Duplicate imports are matched through codes, titles, context, and source rather than silently merged.

## Acceptance criteria

Courses remain reconstructable by curriculum version, aliases and equivalences are explicit, and consumer phases reference stable IDs instead of copied mutable names.
