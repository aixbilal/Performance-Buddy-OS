# Semester Model

## Purpose

Distinguish curriculum semester placement from an actual academic term in the user’s history.

## Entities

`CurriculumSemester` is an ordinal slot within a curriculum version. `AcademicTerm` is a dated institutional period such as a named semester or summer term. `StudentTerm` links the user to an AcademicTerm with enrollment status and personal records.

## Fields

Term fields include stable ID, official label, type, start/end dates, exam windows when sourced, campus, status, and source. Student-term fields include load, standing when officially recorded, notes, and provenance.

## Rules

- Term labels are not parsed into policy without confirmation.
- Date changes create revisions and update future plans, not historical events.
- A student term may contain courses from several curriculum-semester slots.
- GPA belongs to Phase 13 and is not stored here as an unexplained value.

## Acceptance criteria

Historical and current terms are distinguishable, atypical terms work, source dates are auditable, and curriculum sequence is not confused with enrollment chronology.
