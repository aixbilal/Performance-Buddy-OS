# Official CUI Source Handling

## Purpose

Define how CUI curriculum and policy material becomes authoritative academic data while acknowledging that the applicable versions have not yet been verified for this project.

## Accepted authority

Prefer official CUI websites, portals, published handbooks, catalogs, regulations, notices, and authenticated records. Capture issuer, title, URL or governed location, publication/effective date, retrieval date, campus/program/cohort applicability, page or section, and checksum when possible.

## Current status

The exact CUI curriculum and grading-policy versions applicable to the user are `RESEARCH REQUIRED` under OQ-003. Therefore this baseline contains no assumed course list, grading threshold, repeat policy, prerequisite rule, or GPA formula.

## Conflict protocol

Preserve each source. Compare authority, effective date, applicability, amendments, and supersession. Do not resolve a conflict from filename, recency, or AI judgment alone. Consequential calculations remain blocked until an authorized source or explicit official clarification resolves it.

## AI and web boundary

Search, OCR, and AI may locate or extract candidates but cannot promote them to official fact. A reviewer confirms source authenticity, context, and transcription.

## Acceptance criteria

Every CUI-specific claim links to an applicable official source, stale or ambiguous material is labeled, and policy gaps cannot silently fall back to common university practice.
