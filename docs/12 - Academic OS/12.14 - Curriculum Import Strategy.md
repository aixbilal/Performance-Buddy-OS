# Curriculum Import Strategy

## Purpose

Define a safe, repeatable pipeline for importing curriculum structure from official documents or reviewed structured data.

## Pipeline

1. Acquire the source with URL/location, publication date, capture date, checksum, and applicability context.
2. Preserve an immutable raw snapshot where permission allows.
3. Extract candidate program, version, semester, course, credits, groups, and prerequisites.
4. Normalize formats without changing meaning.
5. Validate totals, identities, references, graph integrity, and duplicates.
6. Present differences and uncertainty for human review.
7. Approve and publish a new curriculum version.

## Extraction authority

OCR, parsers, and AI produce `IMPORTED DRAFT` data, never official truth by themselves. Low-confidence cells, ambiguous footnotes, and unreadable pages remain unresolved. Manual correction retains the original extraction and reviewer.

## Idempotency and rollback

The same source checksum and importer version produce the same candidate set. Publication is transactional; failure leaves the approved version unchanged. Rollback restores the previous active version without deleting the failed import.

## Acceptance criteria

Imports are reproducible, source-complete, reviewable field by field, resistant to duplication, and incapable of silently replacing approved curriculum data.
