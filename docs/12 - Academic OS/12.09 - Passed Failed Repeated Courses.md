# Passed Failed Repeated Courses

## Purpose

Define neutral status representation for passed, failed, and repeated courses while reserving policy-specific interpretation for verified official rules.

## Status layers

`AttemptOutcome` describes one attempt. `CourseSatisfactionStatus` describes whether a curriculum requirement is currently satisfied. `RepeatRelationship` links attempts believed or verified to represent repeats or equivalents. These layers must not be collapsed.

## Rules

- Pass/fail thresholds come only from the applicable official grading policy.
- A repeated course creates a new attempt and preserves every earlier result.
- Grade replacement, averaging, exclusion, credit earning, and repeat limits belong to verified Phase 13 policy.
- A passed attempt does not prove prerequisite applicability unless the course identity/equivalence is confirmed.
- Unknown or conflicting policy yields `UNRESOLVED`, not a guessed status.

## Presentation

Use factual, non-shaming language. Show attempt chronology, source, verification, and how the current satisfaction status was derived.

## Acceptance criteria

History is never overwritten, repeat relationships are sourced, status derivations are explainable, and no institution rule is hard-coded without authority.
