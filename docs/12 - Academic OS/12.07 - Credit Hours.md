# Credit Hours

## Purpose

Represent official course credit values and clearly distinguish curriculum, attempted, earned, enrolled, and projected credits.

## Data model

A credit specification records total credits and, when officially available, theory/lab or contact-hour components, unit system, source, curriculum version, and effective dates. Values use exact numeric precision supported by the official policy.

## Credit categories

- curriculum required credits;
- credits enrolled in a term;
- credits attempted;
- credits earned through passed outcomes;
- transfer or exemption credits where officially recognized;
- projected credits in a labeled scenario.

## Rules

Credit hours are not marks, grade points, workload hours, or study-time estimates. Phase 13 determines how credits participate in GPA/CGPA using verified policy. Failed, withdrawn, repeated, transferred, or excluded attempts must not be counted without an applicable rule.

## Validation

Totals expose included course IDs, attempt IDs, rule version, and exclusions. Unknown credit values block affected official totals rather than becoming zero.

## Acceptance criteria

Every credit total is reproducible, categorized, source-linked, version-aware, and resistant to duplicate attempts or implicit conversions.
