# Prerequisite Graph

## Purpose

Represent official prerequisite and co-requisite relationships as a versioned directed graph suitable for validation, planning, and risk analysis.

## Graph model

Nodes are versioned courses or requirement groups. Edges contain relationship type, minimum condition where officially defined, source, curriculum version, effective dates, and verification status. Alternative requirements use explicit groups rather than ambiguous free text.

## Validation

`CORE` checks missing nodes, duplicate edges, impossible self-dependencies, cycles, version mismatch, and unresolved references. A detected cycle is reported for source review; it is not automatically “fixed.”

## Evaluation

Eligibility is evaluated against verified attempt outcomes and applicable policy. Unknown course equivalence, incomplete results, or missing thresholds produce `UNRESOLVED`. Co-requisites remain distinguishable from prerequisites.

## Change handling

Graph revisions are effective-dated. Historical enrollment decisions retain the graph version used at the time.

## Acceptance criteria

Every edge is traceable to an official source, alternative logic is deterministic, uncertainty is visible, and consumers receive both result and explanation path.
