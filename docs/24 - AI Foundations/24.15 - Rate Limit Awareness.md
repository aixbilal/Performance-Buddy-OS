# 24.15 — Rate Limit Awareness

## Model

Rate limits are provider- and account-specific resource constraints, not generic failures. Adapters normalize scope, remaining capacity when supplied, retry-after information, and reset time without assuming values the provider did not return.

## Scheduling policy

Interactive requests receive priority over optional background work. Queues are bounded, fair, cancellable, and separated by workload class. Retry-after is honored; otherwise bounded exponential backoff with jitter applies. Circuit breakers and concurrency caps prevent retry storms. Expired or no-longer-relevant work is discarded before execution.

Degraded modes include delayed execution with consent, smaller approved workloads, local capability, deterministic/manual workflow, or clear unavailability. The system does not silently change providers when that changes data exposure, quality guarantees, or cost.

Tool and commit idempotency survives queue retries. UI status distinguishes queued, running, throttled, failed, and completed states.

## Acceptance criteria

- Simulated 429 and quota exhaustion cannot create duplicate actions.
- Queue growth, wait time, drops, retries, and recovery are observable.
- Background runs cannot starve user-initiated work.
