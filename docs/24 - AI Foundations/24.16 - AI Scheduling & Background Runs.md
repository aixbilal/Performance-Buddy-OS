# 24.16 — AI Scheduling and Background Runs

## Eligibility

Background AI is optional and explicitly enabled per application and schedule. Each schedule declares owner, purpose, capability, data scope, provider policy, frequency, quiet hours, expiry, budgets, notification behavior, and cancellation route.

## Execution rules

At start time, reacquire authorization and current consent; a historical permission snapshot is audit evidence, not enduring authority. Recheck source revisions, network and device constraints, cost limits, and rate capacity. Use locks and idempotency keys to prevent overlapping or duplicate runs. Missed runs follow a declared skip, catch-up, or merge policy.

Background runs may refresh indexes, prepare summaries, or create proposals. They may not make meaningful plan changes, contact external parties, or write sensitive records without the approval policy defined for that exact action. No run may create an unbounded recurring schedule or wake loop.

Results expire, cite evidence, and state when they were generated. Notifications respect Phase 06 interruption principles.

## Acceptance criteria

- Users can see, pause, run now, edit, and cancel every schedule.
- Revoked consent or expired schedules stop before data access.
- Offline, sleep, duplicate-trigger, timezone, and daylight-change cases are tested.
