# Definition of Ready

## Ready checklist

A work item is ready only when it has:

- stable ID, user problem/outcome, owner, priority, capability/status, and bounded scope;
- authoritative source documents, domain meaning, terminology, and non-goals;
- dependencies, assumptions, open questions, risks, and required ADRs resolved enough to start;
- data/entities/revisions/provenance, permission zones, privacy/security, offline/degraded behavior, and migration impact;
- UI/interaction/accessibility expectations where surfaced;
- deterministic versus AI boundary, approval behavior, failure/recovery, compatibility, and fallback;
- test/evaluation approach, acceptance criteria, performance expectations, and evidence owner;
- estimate/range and explicit deferral of adjacent scope.

Unresolved external facts are marked `RESEARCH REQUIRED` with owner and blocking impact. A mockup, request, or AI-generated design alone does not make an item ready.

## Disposition

Items are ready, needs refinement, blocked, deferred, or rejected. Readiness can be revoked after material requirement, dependency, threat, or architecture change.

## Acceptance criteria

- Implementation does not begin with unknown domain authority or acceptance behavior.
- Ready items can be explained and tested independently.
- Readiness review prevents architecture drift and hidden optional dependencies.
