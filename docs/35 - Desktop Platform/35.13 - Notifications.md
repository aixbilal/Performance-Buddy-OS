# Notifications

## Delivery role

Desktop notifications deliver approved reminders, timer events, actionable failures, and high-value review prompts. They report product state; they do not create schedule truth or mark work complete merely because delivery occurred.

## Rules

Notifications respect user permission, per-category controls, quiet hours, focus and recovery modes, urgency, batching, cooldowns, and operating-system settings. Sensitive content is hidden on lock screens and previews by default. Wording is calm, specific, non-manipulative, and never uses guilt or false urgency.

Actions route through authorized commands and revalidate current state because a notification may be stale. Duplicate delivery is suppressed by stable event IDs. Missed OS delivery is recorded where knowable but does not trigger uncontrolled repetition.

Local reminders do not require cloud. Remote push, if added, is separately labeled `CLOUD`/`MOBILE` and consent-scoped.

## Accessibility and recovery

Important information also exists inside the application; notifications are never the only recovery path. Actions have descriptive labels and keyboard-accessible equivalents.

## Acceptance criteria

- Quiet hours, stale actions, duplicates, denied permission, and sleep/resume are tested.
- Private content is not exposed by default.
- Notification failure cannot alter completion or evidence.
