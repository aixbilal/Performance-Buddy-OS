# Money Domain

## Purpose

Define lightweight personal money records and reviews while preventing financial data from becoming a performance judgment.

## Responsibilities

Money owns income, expenses, categories, balance, savings, savings goals, recurring expenses, weekly/monthly spending review, bounded AI suggestions, leisure-spending philosophy, and financial-advice boundaries.

## Scope

This is lightweight personal tracking, not accounting, banking, investment management, tax preparation, credit scoring, or regulated advice. Imported or manually entered transactions retain provenance and can be corrected without rewriting audit history invisibly.

## Interactions

Analytics produces domain-specific totals and trends. Performance may schedule a review but does not rank spending against study, prayer, fitness, or productivity. Intelligence may explain patterns or propose a budget action only with permission and clear uncertainty.

## Invariants

- Money is excluded from an overall performance score.
- Leisure spending is not automatically bad.
- Deterministic arithmetic calculates balances and totals.
- AI does not fabricate transactions or provide authoritative financial advice.
- Sensitive financial data is minimized and protected.

## Failure behavior

Unknown, duplicate, or conflicting transactions remain flagged. Calculation errors are test failures, not matters for LLM judgment. External service absence cannot prevent local manual use.

## Acceptance criteria

Balances are reproducible from records, reviews are calm and contextual, privacy permissions are explicit, and cross-domain features cannot moralize finances.
