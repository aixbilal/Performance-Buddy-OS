# Academic Domain

## Purpose

Define ownership of university structure, assessments, official policy, academic evidence, calculations, and study demand.

## Responsibilities

Academic owns university/program/curriculum versions, semesters, courses, attempts, credit hours, prerequisites, enrollment, assessments, marks, grade rules, SGPA/CGPA, projections, course risk, topic coverage, and study requirements.

Phases 12–14 and 17 refine these responsibilities. Phase 13 owns deterministic academic mathematics; Phase 23 validates rules.

## Source authority

Official CUI sources define applicable policy. User-confirmed information defines personal configuration. Imported records describe the user’s history. AI reports and inference never equal official policy. Every calculation retains inputs, rule/source version, and derivation.

## Interactions

- Performance receives deadlines, fixed university commitments, study demand, and validated risk.
- Knowledge receives course/topic relationships and returns learning evidence.
- Analytics derives academic trends without modifying records.
- Intelligence may tutor, retrieve, generate grounded tests, or propose allocation; deterministic tools calculate results.

## Invariants

Attempts and historical grades are not overwritten by changed policy. Unknown weights or thresholds remain unresolved. Projections are labeled scenarios, not promises. Course and prerequisite identity is version-aware.

## Privacy and failure

Marks and personal records are sensitive. Provider access requires explicit permission. Conflicting official sources block consequential calculation until resolved.

## Non-goals

Academic does not own general daily planning, knowledge storage, or the user’s complete performance score.
