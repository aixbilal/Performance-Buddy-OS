# Fitness & Recovery Domain

## Purpose

Define exercise, training load, rest, recovery signals, progress, and safety ownership.

## Responsibilities

Fitness & Recovery owns running, strength, calisthenics, explosive training, mobility, stretching, wrist/forearm/hand work, recovery sessions, rest days, load representation, progressive improvement, sleep integration, recovery signals, evidence, and safety boundaries.

## Core concepts

Activity type, session, planned/actual load, milestone, rest/recovery state, user-reported signal, progression, and safety flag. These are personal fitness records, not clinical diagnoses.

## Interactions

Performance reserves time and uses validated load/recovery context. Analytics derives personal trends. Intelligence may propose lower or higher load with reasons. Sleep configuration contributes protected constraints. No consumer may reinterpret a warning as permission to train.

## Invariants

Rest is valid. Progression is gradual and contextual. Missing recovery data does not imply readiness. Minutes/repetitions alone do not prove improvement. User stop/reduce decisions are always respected.

## Safety boundary

The product does not diagnose injury, prescribe treatment, or present AI advice as medical authority. Serious or uncertain symptoms require appropriate qualified help outside the product.

## Privacy and acceptance

Fitness/recovery data is permission-scoped. The domain succeeds when training history is honest, safety constraints survive cross-domain planning, and recommendations remain explainable and optional.
