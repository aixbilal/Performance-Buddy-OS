# Routine & Spiritual Domain

## Purpose

Define prayer, personal routines, reading, language learning, reflection, and private-data boundaries.

## Responsibilities

Routine & Spiritual owns prayer tracking, multi-check prayer representation, hydration, nutrition habits, skincare, smoking avoidance, reading, practical language learning, daily reflection, routine priority/flexibility, and private routine-data handling.

Tracker mechanics are shared from Phase 10; language path mechanics are shared from Phase 11. Phase 20 owns domain semantics, including `20.09 - Language Learning.md`.

## Core concepts

Routine definition, schedule, check/state, priority, flexibility, maintenance mode, reflection, privacy category, primary language, skill area, and practice evidence.

## Interactions

Performance schedules fixed/flexible routines. Analytics may summarize consistency without judging character. Intelligence may propose adjustments or language practice with explicit permissions. Examination mode may reduce language to maintenance rather than failure.

## Invariants

Prayer completion is not a productivity score or proof of intention. Private reflections and spiritual records receive strict permission boundaries. Routines support partial, excused, rest, maintenance, and not-applicable states where meaningful. Calm language is mandatory.

## Language scope

German/Italian practical communication may use 30–40 minutes normally and reduced maintenance during examination pressure, across speaking, listening, vocabulary, reading/pronunciation, and basic grammar.

## Acceptance criteria

Capture is low-friction, privacy is preserved, maintenance is honest, and no cross-domain score moralizes routine data.
