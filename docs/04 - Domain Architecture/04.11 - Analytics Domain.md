# Analytics Domain

## Purpose

Define trustworthy derived metrics, reviews, trends, friction, and risk signals without creating a competing source of truth.

## Responsibilities

Analytics owns metric definitions, aggregation windows, deterministic daily/weekly/monthly/semester reviews, domain analytics, cross-domain views, trend/friction analysis, risk signals, time allocation, and review language.

## Data contract

Every metric identifies owner, source records, inclusion/exclusion rules, time zone/window, formula/version, missing-data treatment, and interpretation limits. Derived values are reproducible and never replace source records.

## Interactions

Domains publish permissioned facts. Analytics returns views and signals. Intelligence may consume these with provenance, but an AI narrative cannot modify the metric or its inputs. Performance may use validated signals as priority context.

## Invariants

Correlation is not causation. Missing records are unknown. Time spent is not equivalent to progress. Aggregation must not reveal private spiritual, reflection, health-adjacent, academic, or financial data beyond permission. Cross-domain views do not become a universal score.

## Risk signals

A signal is an explainable indication for review, not a diagnosis or automatic plan change. It includes window, threshold/rule, evidence coverage, uncertainty, and safe next step.

## Acceptance criteria

Metrics are testable, provenance-linked, stable across versioned formulas, and communicated calmly. Users can inspect why a result exists and correct source-data issues.
