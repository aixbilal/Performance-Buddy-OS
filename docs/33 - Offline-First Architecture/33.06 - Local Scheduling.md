# 33.06 — Local Scheduling

## Scope

The local scheduler owns recurrence expansion, reminders, plan maintenance jobs, index refresh, retention cleanup, backup triggers, queued-operation retries, and other registered deterministic work. OS delivery limitations are separate from schedule truth.

## Contract

Each job has ID/version, owner, purpose, next/last run, timezone, recurrence, constraints, misfire policy (`skip`, `catch-up`, `merge`), priority, quiet hours, lease, idempotency key, attempts, and result. Jobs persist locally and are inspectable, pausable, and cancellable.

## Rules

Clock/timezone changes recalculate future triggers without rewriting history. Sleep/resume and missed runs use declared policy. Concurrent app instances use leases. Background AI follows Phase 24/27 and cannot commit proposals. Network-required jobs queue or fail visibly; they do not block local jobs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Restart, sleep, clock drift, timezone, duplicate trigger, and long-offline tests pass.
- Jobs never create wake/retry storms or duplicate side effects.
- Reminder delivery failure does not alter occurrence/completion state.
