# Authentication Future

## Status

Remote account authentication is `FUTURE` until cloud or multi-device capability is promoted. Local-only CORE must not require an online account. Local application locking, OS account protection, and remote identity are separate concerns.

## Requirements

A future design must define account identifiers, verification, session and refresh-token handling, device enrollment, multi-factor or passkey strategy, recovery, credential changes, reauthentication for consequential operations, lockout and abuse defenses, revocation, deletion, support boundaries, and privacy-preserving audit.

Use established protocols and vetted libraries; do not design custom authentication or store passwords without an approved specialist design. Device names and biometrics are not remote authorization by themselves. Recovery must resist takeover while honestly addressing irreversible loss where applicable.

Authentication proves an identity or session; domain authorization still checks purpose, resource, operation, data zone, and approval. Offline cached sessions require explicit expiry and degraded behavior.

`RESEARCH REQUIRED`: identity provider/build decision, passkeys, MFA, account recovery, token lifetimes, email/phone use, age/legal requirements, and support model.

## Acceptance criteria

- Threat tests cover enumeration, replay, fixation, theft, recovery abuse, and revoked devices.
- Signing out does not silently delete valid local records.
- CORE operates without enrollment in the future account system.
