# Security Review Checklist

## Review gate

Every release and material architecture change confirms:

- threat model, trust boundaries, assets, assumptions, and residual-risk owners are current;
- data inventory, zones, purposes, retention, deletion, export, backups, and derivatives are mapped;
- authentication, authorization, consent, device, and approval paths fail safely;
- database/file encryption and key/secret lifecycle are tested;
- UI bridges, files, imports, APIs, callbacks, jobs, queues, AI tools, and provider payloads validate untrusted input;
- logs, telemetry, crashes, notifications, support bundles, and redaction exclude prohibited content;
- dependencies, licenses, vulnerabilities, SBOM, builds, signing, updater, and artifacts are verified;
- offline, outage, retry, replay, conflict, revocation, migration, restore, and incident recovery tests pass;
- accessibility and recovery do not require weakening security;
- cloud/AI disclosures match network behavior and provider terms.

## Disposition

Findings record severity, evidence, affected versions, owner, remediation, deadline, verification, and residual risk. Critical/high findings block release unless an authorized time-bounded exception documents compensating controls and rollback.

## Acceptance criteria

- The completed checklist links to evidence, not unchecked assertions.
- Exceptions expire and cannot be copied forward automatically.
- Review repeats after material code, provider, permission, threat, or platform change.
