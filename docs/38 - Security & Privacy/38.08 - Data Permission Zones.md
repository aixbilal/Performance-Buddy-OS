# Data Permission Zones

## Zones

Every protected source and derivative carries a data zone:

| Zone | Boundary |
|---|---|
| `NEVER_AI` | Excluded from all model and embedding processing, local or remote. |
| `LOCAL_ONLY` | May stay and be processed only on the authorized device. |
| `ASK_EVERY_TIME` | Requires fresh contextual consent for each bounded AI use. |
| `APPROVED_CLOUD` | May reach named remote purposes/providers within active scope. |
| `PUBLIC_OR_EXPORTABLE` | May be exported for approved purpose; still subject to ownership and integrity. |

These zones do not replace domain permissions, privacy class, source authority, or legal constraints. The effective policy is the strictest applicable combination.

## Propagation

Summaries, embeddings, indexes, prompts, filenames, metadata, aggregates, citations, backups, sync payloads, exports, logs, and caches inherit the strictest contributing zone unless an explicit authorized declassification policy permits otherwise. Cross-domain processing uses permission intersection, not union.

Zone changes require authorization, impact analysis, invalidation/purge of prohibited derivatives, queued-work cancellation, and an audit record. Mandatory secret classifications cannot be weakened.

## Acceptance criteria

- Egress tests cover direct content, metadata, aggregates, caches, and derivatives.
- Revocation reaches queues, indexes, memories, exports, and provider connections.
- Missing classification defaults conservatively and never broadens access.
