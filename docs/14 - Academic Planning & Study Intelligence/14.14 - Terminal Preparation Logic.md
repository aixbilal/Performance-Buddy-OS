# Terminal Preparation Logic

## Purpose

Specialize preparation for terminal/final examinations, where cumulative scope and consequence may require stronger prioritization without weakening safety.

## Allocation direction

Rank confirmed exams by date, consequence, prerequisite risk, current evidence, remaining scope, and feasible improvement. Use larger coherent blocks for integration and representative practice, plus short retrieval and error-review blocks where appropriate.

## Rules

- Preserve sleep, meals, hydration, prayer, movement, and recovery.
- Never solve overload by removing all buffers.
- Prioritize active recall, problem solving, mock/past-paper practice, and correction over passive time accumulation.
- Label past-paper trends as inference and exam projections as scenarios.
- Pause or maintain secondary paths explicitly when necessary.

## Infeasibility and exit

When demand exceeds safe capacity, show uncovered scope and bounded options rather than a fictional schedule. After the final exam, trigger transition and recovery review before restoring backlog.

## Acceptance criteria

Terminal preparation is evidence-driven, source-aware, capacity-honest, and cannot imply guaranteed outcomes or official topic predictions.
