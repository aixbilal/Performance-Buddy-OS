# Academic Risk Model

## Purpose

Estimate academic consequence and urgency so attention can be directed before a problem becomes irreversible.

## Risk dimensions

Deadline proximity, assessment weight, current verified marks, target-grade scenario gap, prerequisite impact, remaining coverage, weakness evidence, missed required work, attendance/exposure gaps where relevant, workload feasibility, and source uncertainty.

## Risk states

`Critical`, `High`, `Moderate`, `Low`, and `Unknown` are planning bands, not official academic standing. Each result includes horizon, affected course/outcome, triggers, protective factors, assumptions, missing inputs, and calculation version.

## Rules

Verified hard deadlines and prerequisite blocks are distinguished from projected risk. A grade scenario cannot become a factual outcome. Sparse data does not imply low risk. Risk cannot be increased merely to motivate the user, and no single metric decides the band.

## AI boundary

`CORE` evaluates approved rules. `V2` may identify patterns and draft explanations; it cannot diagnose ability or guarantee failure/success.

## Acceptance criteria

Risk is reproducible, calibrated against evidence, source-aware, non-shaming, and linked to specific feasible responses rather than vague alarm.
