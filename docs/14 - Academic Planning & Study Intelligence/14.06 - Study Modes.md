# Study Modes

## Purpose

Study modes adjust the mix and intensity of academic activities without changing official records, grade policy, or historical evidence.

## Canonical modes

- `Normal`: balanced class follow-up, coverage, practice, and review.
- `Midterm`: temporary exam-focused preparation for confirmed midterms.
- `Terminal`: higher-focus final-exam preparation under protected constraints.
- `Weekend`: longer flexible blocks with recovery and catch-up options.
- `Recovery`: reduced demand and smallest useful academic restart.
- `University`: on-campus capture, exposure, and fixed-commitment reconciliation.

## Rules

Activation has source, scope, start/end, affected courses, and actor. Unverified exam dates cannot silently activate a mode. Mode modifies prioritization parameters and suggested methods, not factual risk or completion. Exiting a mode triggers review of deferred work rather than automatic backlog loading.

## Acceptance criteria

Modes are visible, reversible, effective-dated, compatible with Phase 08, and unable to bypass sleep, safety, prayer, nutrition, recovery, or other protected requirements.
