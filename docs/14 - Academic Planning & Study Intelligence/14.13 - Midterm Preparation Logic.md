# Midterm Preparation Logic

## Purpose

Specialize academic demand and methods for a confirmed midterm period consistently with Phase 08 Midterm Mode.

## Activation and scope

Activation requires user action or confirmed dates, with affected courses and end date. Course order considers exam timing, verified weight, current risk, prerequisite impact, coverage, and evidence gaps.

## Method policy

Favor retrieval, worked and unworked problems, error correction, compact concept review, and source-grounded practice. Class exposure reduces duplicated review only when the intended evidence was achieved. Optional work is reduced before protected sleep and recovery.

## Reconciliation

After each study session or assessment, recalculate remaining gaps and present minimal allocation changes. Maintenance for development or language remains honest and does not count as full progression.

## Exit

Mode ends on its configured date or user action. Deferred work receives a recovery-aware review instead of immediate overload.

## Acceptance criteria

Plans are source-grounded, feasible, explainable by course, responsive to evidence, and unable to sacrifice essential routines or safety for a projected mark.
