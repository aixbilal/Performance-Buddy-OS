# University Mode

## Purpose

University Mode supports the on-campus day through fixed commitments, low-friction capture, contextual actions, and later reconciliation.

## Behavior

Show today’s verified classes, labs, assessments, travel buffers, deadlines, and compact course actions. Capture attendance where appropriate, topics covered, new work, marks, questions, and completed practice with minimum interruption. Offline capture is mandatory.

## Rules

Schedules retain source and as-of time. A changed room or cancelled class is a personal event until authoritative context is confirmed. Exposure does not equal mastery. New assignments create demand but do not silently overwrite the day or protected sleep. Duplicate time across class and study evidence is prevented.

## Reconciliation

At a suitable transition, match captures to courses/topics, route marks and deadlines to owners, identify satisfied or new requirements, account for travel/fatigue, and send candidate evening changes to Phase 08.

## Capability

The baseline is `CORE`; `MOBILE` may later provide companion capture. AI extraction remains optional and reviewable.

## Acceptance criteria

University Mode is calm, quick, privacy-aware, offline-capable, source-conscious, and produces an explainable feasible remainder rather than automatic schedule churn.
