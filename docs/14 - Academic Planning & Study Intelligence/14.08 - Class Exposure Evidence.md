# Class Exposure Evidence

## Purpose

Represent what the user encountered or practiced during lectures, labs, tutorials, and other university contact so later planning can avoid duplication and identify follow-up needs.

## Evidence model

An exposure record links course offering, date, class type, topics, activities, source, capture method, notes, and verification state. Attendance and exposure are separate: attendance may be known while topic coverage remains unknown.

## Rules

- Exposure establishes opportunity, not mastery.
- In-class problem solving may satisfy part of planned practice only when evidence criteria match.
- One class can support several topic links but its time is counted once.
- AI extraction from notes, slides, or images creates a reviewable draft.
- Official attendance policy remains source-dependent and outside this evidence inference.

## Reconciliation

New assignments, deadlines, or marks route to their owning records. Phase 08 uses validated exposure to update the remaining day while preserving original plans.

## Acceptance criteria

Capture is fast, offline-capable, provenance-aware, non-duplicative, and never promotes exposure to knowledge confidence without stronger evidence.
