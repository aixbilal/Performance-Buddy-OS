# Exam Ramp-Up Strategy

## Purpose

Increase exam preparation progressively as a confirmed examination approaches while avoiding panic-driven overload and unsafe last-minute compression.

## Inputs

Confirmed exam date and scope, assessment consequence, course risk, topic coverage, retrieval/problem evidence, error patterns, available capacity, other examinations, and protected constraints. Unverified scope is labeled and cannot be presented as official.

## Stages

Early ramp emphasizes coverage and prerequisite repair. Middle ramp emphasizes active recall, problem solving, spaced review, and weakness correction. Final ramp emphasizes representative practice, error review, concise retrieval, logistics, and sleep protection.

## Rules

Ramp intensity is bounded by feasible capacity. Passive rereading does not automatically satisfy active-practice demand. Past-paper frequency is evidence/inference, not guaranteed exam content. Secondary skills may enter deliberate maintenance rather than false failure.

## Acceptance criteria

The strategy begins from confirmed dates, adapts to evidence, preserves buffers and recovery, reports infeasibility, and never guarantees a grade.
