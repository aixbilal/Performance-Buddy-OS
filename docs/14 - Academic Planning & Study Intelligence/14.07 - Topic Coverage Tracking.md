# Topic Coverage Tracking

## Purpose

Track which course topics have been introduced, studied, practiced, assessed, or demonstrated without confusing exposure with mastery.

## Coverage states

`Unknown`, `Listed`, `Introduced`, `Studied`, `Practiced`, `Assessed`, and `Demonstrated` describe evidence stages. They are not a forced linear lifecycle; one evidence item may support several relationships without double counting.

## Record model

Each record links course, versioned topic, source, activity/evidence, date, depth or scope, verification state, and optional confidence. Topic identity and knowledge confidence belong to Phase 15; Phase 14 uses their references for planning.

## Rules

Lecture mention does not prove understanding. Reading or timer completion does not prove practice. Demonstrated status requires owner-defined evidence such as retrieval, checked problems, or assessment performance. Missing topic lists remain unresolved.

## Acceptance criteria

Coverage is source-linked, granular enough for planning, honest about evidence strength, resilient to topic-version changes, and never converted into mastery from time alone.
