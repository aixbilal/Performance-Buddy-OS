# Study Allocation Model

## Purpose

Distribute available academic study capacity among courses and topics using constraint-safe, explainable rules.

## Allocation sequence

Reserve fixed commitments and protected sleep/recovery; establish available academic windows; remove ineligible or blocked activities; satisfy imminent high-consequence minimums; allocate remaining capacity using risk, deadline, prerequisite impact, evidence gap, expected return, and continuity; preserve buffers; then present alternatives.

## Output

Allocations are ranges or bounded blocks with course, activity, evidence target, reason codes, and trade-offs. They are sent to Phase 08 as candidates, not silently committed plans.

## Fairness and stability

Do not reward courses merely for richer data or permanently starve a lower-risk course. Apply continuity floors where feasible. Avoid tiny fragmented blocks that cannot produce useful evidence. User pins and overrides are respected and logged.

## Status

The factor set is approved; exact weights, normalization, thresholds, and tie-breakers are `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED` until representative validation.

## Acceptance criteria

Allocations fit real capacity, protect constraints, state infeasibility honestly, respond predictably to material evidence changes, and expose why one candidate displaced another.
