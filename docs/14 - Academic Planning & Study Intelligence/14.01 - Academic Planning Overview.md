# Academic Planning Overview

## Purpose

Academic planning determines what study demand exists, why it matters, and which feasible study candidates should be offered to the general planning engine.

## Pipeline

1. Snapshot current enrollment, deadlines, assessments, topics, evidence, prerequisites, mode, and capacity.
2. Validate source and freshness.
3. Estimate course/topic risk and remaining requirement.
4. Generate candidate activities with evidence targets.
5. Allocate within the academic budget and protected constraints.
6. Send candidates and explanations to Phase 08 for user-confirmed scheduling.

## Output contract

Each candidate includes course/topic, study method, intended evidence, suggested duration range, deadline/window, priority band, dominant reasons, assumptions, and alternatives. The output is not a committed calendar entry.

## Capability

Rule-based demand and allocation are `CORE`. Exact weights remain `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED` until validated. `V2` may propose historical adaptations; `V3` may compare cross-domain value, but deterministic constraints and human approval remain authoritative.

## Acceptance criteria

Given identical inputs and rule versions, the engine produces the same candidate set; missing evidence lowers certainty rather than fabricating demand; and every suggestion traces to academic facts.
