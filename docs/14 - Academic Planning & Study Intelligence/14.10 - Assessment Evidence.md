# Assessment Evidence

## Purpose

Use quizzes, assignments, projects, midterms, finals, and practice assessments as high-value evidence for planning while respecting Phase 13 authority over official marks and grades.

## Evidence model

References include assessment identity, course attempt, covered topics where known, official or practice status, earned/max marks, category weight, date, source, verification, error classifications, and policy version.

## Rules

Official results remain Phase 13 records. Practice scores are never mixed with official marks. One assessment may indicate performance across several topics, but inference strength depends on question coverage and sample size. A low score is evidence of a gap under those conditions, not a diagnosis of ability.

## Planning use

Assessment evidence may raise or lower topic requirement, reveal error patterns, influence risk, and suggest retrieval or correction work. Unverified weight or grade policy limits consequence estimates.

## Acceptance criteria

Official and practice evidence are visibly separate, provenance is retained, topic inferences expose their basis and uncertainty, and planning cannot alter marks.
