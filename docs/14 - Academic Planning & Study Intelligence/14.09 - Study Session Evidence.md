# Study Session Evidence

## Purpose

Translate Phase 09 university study sessions into academic planning evidence while preserving the distinction between activity, output, and learning.

## Evidence levels

Session duration and method are activity evidence. Attempted problems, retrieval responses, error logs, summaries in the user’s own words, completed assignment segments, and checked outputs provide stronger outcome evidence. Correctness and mastery require the owning assessment or knowledge rule.

## Rules

- A completed timer cannot mark a topic mastered.
- Planned and unplanned sessions both remain valid and distinguishable.
- Partial outcomes update only the evidenced portion.
- AI-assisted and AI-off attempts retain provenance.
- Reusing one artifact across links does not duplicate time or completion.

## Planning use

Validated session evidence updates recent attention, remaining requirement, method effectiveness, and next-action candidates. Notes may be promoted to Phase 15 explicitly, never automatically.

## Acceptance criteria

Every planning update traces to a session and evidence type, uncertain correctness remains visible, and source corrections trigger deterministic recalculation.
