# 06.16 - Proactive Interruption & Break Interaction
## Purpose

Specify opt-in proactive companion interactions, especially five-minute break experiences, without arbitrary focus interruption.

## Scheduling and interruption policy

Focus/Today scheduling is authoritative for when a prompt may surface. During an active focus block, companions do not open conversational overlays, play attention-grabbing motion, or demand replies. A prompt may be queued for the next declared break, session boundary, or user-configured interruption window. Safety-critical deterministic alerts remain governed by their own policies.

At an eligible break, the system may offer one optional **five-minute interaction**: a quick culture fact and question, language practice, communication drill, reflection, recovery check, or plan review. The card shows the companion, expected duration, reason for appearing, and `Start`, `Later`, and `Dismiss` controls. Silence, dismissal, and reduced-frequency preferences are respected without guilt language.

## Guardrails

Eligibility is computed from local schedule state, focus status, quiet hours, interruption budget, recent dismissals, accessibility settings, and user preferences. AI may propose content but cannot override eligibility. Sensitive memory is not exposed on lock screens or shared displays. Cloud generation requires permission; a local or deterministic card is the fallback.

## Failure and acceptance criteria

Provider delay or outage never extends a break or blocks returning to work. Structured-output failure produces a safe static fallback. Tests prove that active focus is not arbitrarily interrupted, five-minute limits are honored, repeated dismissal reduces frequency, and every proactive interaction has an explainable trigger.
