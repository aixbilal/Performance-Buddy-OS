# Calm Intelligence Principle

## Purpose

Define language and feedback that support reflection and action without shame, manipulation, or overstated certainty.

## Principle

Calm intelligence presents relevant facts, uncertainty, trade-offs, and next options in a neutral supportive tone. It does not perform motivation through anxiety or moral judgment.

## Language rules

- Describe the record: “2 sessions completed; 1 deferred,” not “You failed again.”
- Explain the condition: “The plan conflicts with the configured sleep boundary.”
- Offer bounded options: reduce, move, defer, or keep with an acknowledged trade-off.
- State uncertainty: “Recovery data is missing,” not “You are fully recovered.”
- Avoid anthropomorphic certainty such as “I know what is best.”
- Do not treat streak loss, leisure spending, prayer records, marks, or workout load as character judgments.

## Escalation

Urgency is reserved for genuine deadline, safety, data-loss, security, or permission risk. Severity uses plain consequences and a safe response. Repeated alerts should consolidate rather than intensify emotionally.

## AI responses

AI recommendations separate observation, inference, suggestion, and confidence. The explanation names relevant evidence and missing context. A rejected suggestion is not framed as disobedience.

## Review language

Reviews focus on trajectory, friction, system design, and recovery. “Maintenance,” “rest,” “excused,” and “partial” remain legitimate states. Missing records are unknown rather than automatically incomplete.

## Acceptance criteria

Content reviews find no guilt/shame/dark-pattern language; warnings match actual severity; and users can understand a problem and next step without being pressured into one choice.
