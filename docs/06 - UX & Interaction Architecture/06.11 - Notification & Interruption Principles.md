# Notification & Interruption Principles

## Purpose

Control interruption frequency, urgency, privacy, and actionability across local, mobile, and optional service notifications.

## Principle

Notifications earn attention by being timely, actionable, and proportionate. The system is not successful because it sends more reminders.

## Priority levels

- Critical: credible security, data-loss, or immediate safety risk.
- Time-sensitive: fixed commitment, imminent deadline, active timer/session.
- Advisory: review prompt, risk signal, optional recommendation.
- Ambient: background completion, sync/index status available in-app.

## Rules

- Consolidate related items and avoid repeated escalation without new evidence.
- Respect quiet hours, focus sessions, sleep boundaries, and user channel preferences.
- Do not expose marks, finances, prayer/reflection, health-adjacent, or private text on lock screens by default.
- Every actionable notification deep-links to canonical context.
- Dismissal, snooze, defer, and disable behavior is predictable.
- Missing data does not generate accusatory reminders.

## AI notifications

V2/V3 recommendations are advisory and labeled. They do not interrupt by default unless user-configured and validated for relevance. Recommendation delivery does not approve the action.

## Offline and mobile

Local schedules work offline where committed. MOBILE notification behavior is future scope and must respect device permissions and sync freshness.

## Acceptance criteria

Notification audits show a clear owner, reason, severity, privacy policy, expiry, and next action; focus and sleep protections are not bypassed.
