# Evidence Weighting

## Purpose

Define how evidence type, quality, independence, coverage, recency, and consistency influence confidence without reducing learning to arbitrary points.

## Weighting dimensions

Directness of demonstration, verified correctness, independence from hints, task difficulty, topic coverage, source authority, recency, repeated consistency, transfer to new contexts, and contradiction all matter.

## Rules

- Weights and thresholds are versioned, testable rules.
- Time, word count, attendance, and file creation cannot dominate direct evidence.
- Multiple records from one underlying event are deduplicated.
- Official assessments may be strong but still limited by coverage and conditions.
- AI-assisted success is retained with assistance provenance rather than discarded or treated as independent.
- Missing evidence does not contribute a negative weight.

## Status

The dimensions are approved; exact numeric values, normalization, freshness curves, and band thresholds remain `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED` pending Phase 23 evaluation.

## Acceptance criteria

Changing one evidence item has a traceable effect, no hidden factor controls confidence, contradictory evidence is preserved, and prior rule versions remain reproducible.
