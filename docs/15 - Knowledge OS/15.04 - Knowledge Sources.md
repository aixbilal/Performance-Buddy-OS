# Knowledge Sources

## Purpose

Represent where knowledge claims, materials, and evidence originate so retrieval and confidence never detach content from provenance.

## Source types

Official course material, textbook, paper, lecture, personal note, assignment, quiz/test, documentation, website, video, repository, imported file, and AI-generated content are distinct source types. Source type does not by itself prove correctness.

## Record contract

Store stable reference, title, author/issuer when known, location, captured and publication dates, version, checksum where possible, access permissions, source class, verification state, and affected topics.

## Rules

Source text is distinct from summaries and interpretations. Broken, moved, changed, or inaccessible sources retain their reference state. Conflicting sources remain visible until resolved. Copyrighted content is referenced or minimally indexed according to permission rather than copied without need.

## Acceptance criteria

Every retrieved claim can show its source, changed files trigger revalidation, restricted material cannot leak through snippets, and missing provenance lowers authority visibly.
