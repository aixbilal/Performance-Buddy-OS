# Knowledge Confidence

## Purpose

Represent the system’s evidence-based estimate of how reliably the user can understand, retrieve, or apply a knowledge entity under relevant conditions.

## Representation

Use bands such as `Unknown`, `Emerging`, `Developing`, `Reliable`, and `Strong`, accompanied by uncertainty, evidence coverage, freshness, and capability dimensions. Bands are estimates, not permanent labels or judgments of intelligence.

## Rules

- No evidence yields `Unknown`, not the lowest band.
- Recent independent retrieval/application generally supports stronger claims than passive exposure.
- Contradictory evidence widens uncertainty and remains visible.
- Confidence can decay only through an approved transparent freshness rule; absence alone does not prove forgetting.
- Topic confidence does not automatically equal course grade or readiness.

## Capability

`CORE` applies approved deterministic evidence rules. Exact thresholds and decay parameters are `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH REQUIRED` until evaluated. `V2` may explain patterns but cannot silently set mastery.

## Acceptance criteria

Assessments are reproducible, dimension-aware, correctable, and explainable from source evidence without false precision.
