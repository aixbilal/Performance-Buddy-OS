# Knowledge OS Overview

## Purpose

Knowledge OS provides a local, source-grounded layer for understanding what the user is learning, where information came from, what evidence exists, and what can be retrieved safely.

## Core entities

`KnowledgeEntity`, `Topic`, `SourceReference`, `NoteReference`, `MaterialReference`, `LearningEvidence`, `ConfidenceAssessment`, `IndexEntry`, `RetrievalResult`, and `KnowledgeRelation`.

## Lifecycle

Entities are created or imported as drafts, matched to existing identities, reviewed, activated, revised, merged through an auditable mapping, archived, or left broken when an external source disappears. Source deletion never causes silent content substitution.

## Capability

Manual structure, provenance, lexical search, filters, evidence rules, and deterministic confidence projections are `CORE`. `LOCAL` may classify or retrieve semantically; `V1` may explain and tutor from sources; `V2` may detect gaps; graph inference beyond explicit links remains non-authoritative.

## Acceptance criteria

Knowledge remains usable offline, every evidence claim traces to a source, permission changes propagate to search/retrieval, and disabling AI leaves canonical records intact.
