# Learning Evidence Model

## Purpose

Provide one governed contract for observations that may support or challenge a knowledge-confidence assessment.

## Evidence fields

Stable ID, knowledge entity, evidence type, source reference, observed outcome, correctness/quality where verified, independence or assistance mode, context, captured time, freshness, verification state, privacy class, and revision history.

## Evidence types

Exposure, study activity, self-explanation, retrieval attempt, worked problem, application/project, assignment, practice test, official assessment, peer/instructor feedback, and correction are distinct. Exposure and activity have lower direct claim strength than demonstrated performance.

## Rules

Evidence may support, contradict, or remain neutral. Duplicate source events are deduplicated. AI-generated material is not evidence that the user learned it. Corrections invalidate dependent projections. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## Acceptance criteria

Every confidence input is typed, sourced, permissioned, and auditable; evidence strength is not inferred from duration alone; and owner-domain facts are referenced rather than copied.
