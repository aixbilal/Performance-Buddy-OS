# Knowledge Graph Possibilities

## Purpose

Define safe possibilities for connecting topics, prerequisites, courses, evidence, sources, projects, and mistakes without prematurely committing to a graph database or treating inferred edges as truth.

## Candidate relations

`parent-of`, `part-of`, `prerequisite-for`, `related-to`, `applied-in`, `assessed-by`, `evidenced-by`, `sourced-from`, `commonly-confused-with`, and `corrects` may be represented with direction, source, confidence, version, and owner.

## Authority tiers

Explicit official relations, user-confirmed links, imported links, and model-inferred links remain distinct. Inferred edges are proposals and never satisfy prerequisites or change confidence until reviewed under an approved rule.

## Architecture boundary

`CORE` can store explicit relations in the normal local data model and traverse bounded links. Graph database selection, embedding-driven discovery, and automated ontology construction are `FUTURE` or `LOCAL` research, not baseline dependencies.

## Safety

Cycles are valid for some relations but rejected for strict hierarchies. Permission is evaluated at every node and edge; graph traversal cannot leak restricted neighbors.

## Acceptance criteria

Every edge exposes type and provenance, projections are rebuildable, inferred links are visibly non-authoritative, and the application remains functional without graph-specific infrastructure.
