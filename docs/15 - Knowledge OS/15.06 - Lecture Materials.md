# Lecture Materials

## Purpose

Connect slides, handouts, recordings, lab sheets, examples, and instructor-provided resources to courses and topics with explicit provenance.

## Model

Each material reference records course offering, material type, title, source/issuer, date, version, file/location, checksum, permissions, linked topics, extraction state, and verification status.

## Rules

Possessing a material does not establish class attendance, topic exposure, or learning. OCR and AI extraction produce candidate metadata and topic links for review. Updated slides create a new content version; prior links remain reproducible. Restricted materials are not sent to external providers without permission.

## Interactions

Phase 14 may use verified material/topic scope as planning context. Phase 16 indexes permitted local files. Phase 17 may ground test generation only in accessible, cited material.

## Acceptance criteria

Materials are searchable by metadata offline, version changes are detected, topic links are reviewable, source permissions are enforced, and content is never mistaken for evidence of mastery.
