# Knowledge Confidence Explainability

## Purpose

Show why a confidence band was assigned, what uncertainty remains, and which evidence could meaningfully change it.

## Explanation contract

Display knowledge entity and dimension; current band and uncertainty; calculation/rule version; supporting, contradicting, and excluded evidence; source and date; independence/assistance; freshness; coverage gaps; and prior changes.

## Layered UX

A summary states the band and strongest reason. An expanded view lists evidence contributions and limitations. A technical view exposes deterministic trace data. Users can open canonical sources and correct mislinked evidence.

## Language

Say “evidence is limited” rather than “you do not know this.” Avoid guarantees, ability labels, or shame. Explain that a high band is not an official grade and a low-confidence estimate may reflect sparse data.

## AI boundary

AI may turn the trace into plain language, but the trace remains authoritative and available offline.

## Acceptance criteria

A user can reproduce the reasoning, identify missing evidence, distinguish fact from inference, and challenge a result without losing history.
