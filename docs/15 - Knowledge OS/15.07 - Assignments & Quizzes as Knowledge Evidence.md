# Assignments & Quizzes as Knowledge Evidence

## Purpose

Use assignment and quiz performance as evidence about specific knowledge while preserving official academic records and avoiding overgeneralization.

## Evidence mapping

Reference the Phase 13 assessment item or approved practice attempt, mapped topics/questions, response or artifact where permitted, score/correctness, assistance mode, feedback, date, and source verification.

## Rules

- Official marks remain owned by Phase 13.
- A total score is weak topic evidence when question coverage is unknown.
- Correctness with substantial assistance differs from independent retrieval.
- One attempt cannot be duplicated as multiple independent evidence events.
- Small samples and ambiguous marking widen confidence uncertainty.
- A poor result indicates a gap under observed conditions, not fixed ability.

## Acceptance criteria

Evidence retains question/topic coverage and assistance provenance, official and practice work remain distinct, corrections propagate, and confidence changes expose their basis and limitations.
