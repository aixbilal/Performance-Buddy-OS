# Knowledge Entity Model

## Purpose

Define the shared identity and lifecycle contract for concepts, skills, procedures, formulas, questions, and other learnable units.

## Fields

Stable ID, entity type, canonical label, aliases, description, owner context, source references, topic links, privacy class, lifecycle state, effective version, creation source, verification state, and revision history.

## Rules

- Identity is not determined by label alone.
- Aliases support discovery but do not merge entities automatically.
- Merge preserves old IDs as redirects and records reviewer, reason, and evidence.
- Split creates new identities and maps historical evidence explicitly.
- External notes or files are referenced; their content ownership remains external.
- AI-created entities are drafts until confirmed.

## Cross-domain links

Courses, development modules, assessments, sessions, and goals reference stable knowledge IDs. Knowledge OS may summarize their context but cannot modify those owners.

## Acceptance criteria

Identity survives renaming, merges/splits are reversible or auditable, privacy is inherited correctly, and every consumer can resolve a canonical entity without copying mutable meaning.
