# Lab Course Assessments

## Purpose

Define the assessment-scheme contract for lab courses or separately assessed lab components while preserving their official relationship to theory courses.

## Model

The scheme may contain lab performance, assignments, practical examinations, projects, viva, attendance only where officially assessed, or other sourced categories. It records whether the lab is an independent course, linked component, or combined assessment.

## Rules

- Lab structure comes from the curriculum and official assessment source.
- Theory and lab marks are never combined without an explicit formula and applicability rule.
- Practical caps, mandatory components, or separate passing conditions require verified policy.
- Missed, excused, make-up, and zero marks remain distinct.
- Evidence artifacts may support review but do not replace an official mark record.

## Boundary

Phase 13 performs calculations; Phase 12 owns course/component identity; Phase 15 may reference lab work as knowledge evidence without altering marks.

## Acceptance criteria

Independent and combined lab models are representable, every contribution is explainable, and no theory default is silently reused for lab assessment.
