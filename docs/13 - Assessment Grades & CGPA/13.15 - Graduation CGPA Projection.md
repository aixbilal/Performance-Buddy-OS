# Graduation CGPA Projection

## Purpose

Explore possible graduation CGPA trajectories from verified academic history and explicit future-grade scenarios.

## Inputs

Current derived or imported CGPA context, all eligible historical attempts, remaining curriculum credits, planned future attempts, repeat-policy assumptions, target CGPA, grade-point assumptions or ranges, and applicable policy versions.

## Rules

The engine uses attempt-level credit points rather than rounded CGPA where available. Remaining credits must come from a verified curriculum or be labeled assumed. Repeat/replacement effects are applied only through verified policy or visibly provisional scenarios.

## Outputs

Scenario end CGPA, required average grade points over remaining eligible credits, sensitivity bands, optimistic/pessimistic bounds, and infeasibility explanation. The user’s aspiration toward approximately 3.5+ is represented as a target scenario, never a guaranteed outcome.

## Boundary

These are `SCENARIO — NOT OFFICIAL` projections. Academic planning may consume them as one risk input but cannot overrule health, recovery, or factual constraints.

## Acceptance criteria

Every result exposes assumptions, remaining-credit source, formula, rounding, and uncertainty; corrected history recomputes the scenario without altering source records.
