# Assessment Categories

## Purpose

Assessment categories group related items such as assignments, quizzes, laboratories, projects, midterms, or finals according to an applicable official or course-specific scheme.

## Model

A category has stable ID, label, course scheme, weight or contribution rule, item aggregation rule, drop/replace rule if officially supported, required-item policy, maximum, precision, order, and source. Labels alone do not determine semantics.

## Rules

- Category weights must satisfy the verified scheme’s total and precision rules.
- Empty, missing, excused, not-yet-held, and zero-mark items remain distinct.
- Best-N, drop-lowest, make-up, bonus, or cap behavior requires an explicit sourced rule.
- Category revisions are effective-dated and do not rewrite prior calculation runs.
- Theory and lab categories remain separate when the official structure requires it.

## Validation

`CORE` detects duplicate IDs, invalid weight totals, negative limits, impossible earned marks, unsupported extra credit, and unresolved required items.

## Acceptance criteria

Every category contribution is reproducible from its items and rule; missing evidence is not converted to zero silently; and imported labels cannot create policy.
