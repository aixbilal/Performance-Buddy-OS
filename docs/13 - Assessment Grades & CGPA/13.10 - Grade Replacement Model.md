# Grade Replacement Model

## Purpose

Model how repeated attempts affect GPA, CGPA, earned credits, and displayed academic history under an applicable official policy.

## Current status

CUI replacement, exclusion, averaging, repeat-limit, and transcript-display rules applicable to the user are `RESEARCH REQUIRED`. No behavior is assumed.

## Policy contract

The model records attempt matching/equivalence, eligible statuses, which attempt contributions are included, credit treatment, effective dates, scope, limits, source, and verification state. It distinguishes transcript history from calculation inclusion.

## Rules

- Every attempt remains immutable and visible according to permissions.
- Replacement changes derived inclusion, never the historical grade.
- Course equivalence must be officially established.
- Policy applicability may depend on attempt date or cohort.
- Unknown policy blocks affected CGPA rather than selecting latest, highest, or average by convenience.

## Acceptance criteria

Inclusion decisions explain every attempt and source rule, historical results survive recalculation, and fixtures cover multiple repeats and policy-version changes.
