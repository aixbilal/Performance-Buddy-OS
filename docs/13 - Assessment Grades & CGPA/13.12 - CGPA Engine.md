# CGPA Engine

## Purpose

Calculate cumulative GPA from eligible attempts across the applicable academic history using verified grade, credit, and replacement policies.

## General formula

Where policy uses cumulative credit weighting, `CGPA = Σ(eligible credits × grade points) / Σ(eligible credits)`. Eligibility and repeat treatment are policy-controlled and cannot be inferred from the formula alone.

## Inputs and rules

The engine consumes immutable attempts, mapped grade points, credits, equivalence links, repeat inclusion decisions, transfer/exemption treatment, and applicable policy versions. It must not average displayed semester GPAs unless the official policy explicitly uses that method.

## Reconciliation

Derived CGPA is compared with an imported official value using configured tolerance and source context. A mismatch produces a diagnostic showing terms, attempts, credits, grades, replacement decisions, and rounding—not an automatic overwrite.

## Output

Store numerator, denominator, precision, rounding, included/excluded attempts, input hash, policy sources, time, and classification.

## Acceptance criteria

Results are reproducible, repeat-safe, explainable, decimal-accurate, and blocked when a material policy or input is unresolved.
