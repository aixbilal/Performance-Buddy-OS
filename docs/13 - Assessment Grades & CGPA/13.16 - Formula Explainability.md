# Formula Explainability

## Purpose

Make every academic calculation understandable and auditable by the user, developers, and validators.

## Explanation contract

Each result shows result type and classification; formula name/version; policy and source; applicable dates; input rows with provenance; transformations; included and excluded records with reasons; unrounded intermediate values; rounding point and mode; final value; warnings; and calculation timestamp/hash.

## Layered presentation

The summary states the result and status. An expanded view shows substitutions and totals. A technical view exposes machine-readable trace data. Scenario explanations visually separate verified facts, user assumptions, and derived outputs.

## Language rules

Use “calculated from these records” rather than “the university confirms.” Explain mismatches and missing data neutrally. Do not hide exclusions in tooltips or use AI prose as the only derivation.

## AI boundary

AI may translate a deterministic trace into plain language, but the trace remains authoritative and available without AI.

## Acceptance criteria

A reviewer can reproduce the result independently, identify every policy decision, and distinguish imported official values from local derivations and projections.
