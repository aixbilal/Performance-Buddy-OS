# Marks Entry

## Purpose

Capture sensitive assessment marks accurately, preserve their source, and prevent incomplete or provisional entries from becoming authoritative calculations.

## Record contract

A mark stores assessment item, course attempt, earned marks, maximum marks, status, source class/reference, entered and assessed dates, actor, verification state, note, and revision history. Original imported text is retained when normalization occurs.

## Validation

`CORE` checks numeric format, precision, non-negative maximum, earned bounds, bonus policy, item identity, duplicates, and scheme applicability. Zero is a value; missing is `UNKNOWN`; not held, excused, pending, and absent require distinct sourced statuses.

## Workflow

Manual or extracted marks enter a draft; differences and warnings are previewed; the user verifies context; publication creates an auditable record. Corrections never overwrite the prior value.

## Privacy and AI

Marks are sensitive. External processing requires explicit scoped permission. OCR or AI may extract candidates but cannot confirm accuracy or calculate official results independently.

## Acceptance criteria

Every published mark is traceable and reversible, duplicates are blocked, uncertainty is visible, and save failure cannot display an unpersisted value as committed.
