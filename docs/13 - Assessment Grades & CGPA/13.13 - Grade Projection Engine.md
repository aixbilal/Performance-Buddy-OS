# Grade Projection Engine

## Purpose

Estimate possible course outcomes from known marks and explicitly assumed future assessment results. Projections support planning and are never official grades.

## Scenario model

A projection snapshots verified earned marks, assessment scheme, unresolved items, assumed future scores or ranges, applicable grade policy, time, and scenario owner. Assumptions are visually distinct from facts.

## Outputs

Possible weighted-score range, possible grades, remaining marks required for selected thresholds, sensitivity to future assessments, and blockers caused by unknown policy or marks. Probability is not produced without a separately validated statistical model and adequate evidence.

## Rules

Projection arithmetic uses the same deterministic modules as actual calculations. Changing a mark or assumption creates a new scenario revision. Impossible assumptions are rejected; uncertain scheme applicability prevents a confident grade label.

## AI boundary

AI may explain trade-offs or suggest scenarios, but `CORE` performs the arithmetic and the user approves assumptions.

## Acceptance criteria

Every projected value identifies facts versus assumptions, recalculates deterministically, shows uncertainty, and carries a prominent `SCENARIO — NOT OFFICIAL` label.
