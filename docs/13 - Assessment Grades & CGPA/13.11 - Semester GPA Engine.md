# Semester GPA Engine

## Purpose

Calculate semester GPA (SGPA) from eligible course attempts under a verified grade and attempt-inclusion policy.

## General formula

Where the official policy uses credit weighting, `SGPA = Σ(eligible course credits × grade points) / Σ(eligible course credits)`. This is a generic formula contract, not confirmation that every CUI status or attempt is eligible.

## Inputs

Student term, verified course attempts, applicable credits, grade results, inclusion/exclusion decisions, grade policy, repeat policy when relevant, precision, and rounding rule.

## Rules

Zero eligible credits yields `UNRESOLVED/NOT APPLICABLE`, not division by zero. Unknown grade or credit blocks an official result unless policy explicitly permits exclusion. Each result lists included and excluded attempts with reasons.

## Output

Store numerator, denominator, unrounded value, displayed value, rounding, policy versions, input hashes, time, and result classification. Imported official SGPA remains a separate personal record available for reconciliation.

## Acceptance criteria

Approved fixtures reproduce expected SGPA exactly, exclusions are explainable, corrections trigger recomputation, and scenarios cannot masquerade as official results.
