# Theory Course Assessments

## Purpose

Define the assessment-scheme contract for a course classified by the official curriculum as theory or theory-dominant, without assuming CUI category weights.

## Contract

The scheme identifies course attempt, official course type, applicable template or override, categories, item rules, pass conditions, precision, source, and version. Category composition may include quizzes, assignments, projects, midterms, finals, or other sourced forms.

## Rules

Theory status comes from the versioned course definition. A timer, study session, or knowledge confidence record cannot create marks. Missing assessment items retain their factual status. Any separate final-exam or aggregate passing condition must be sourced and evaluated independently.

## Calculation boundary

`13.08` owns weighted arithmetic and `13.09` owns grade mapping. This document owns only the theory-scheme specialization and its validation requirements.

## Acceptance criteria

The complete scheme is source-linked, totals validate, exceptional items remain explicit, and theory-course results cannot fall back to an invented generic template.
