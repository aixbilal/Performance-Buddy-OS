# Grade Policy Model

## Purpose

Represent the official mapping from course results to letter grades, grade points, pass/fail outcomes, and special statuses for a defined applicability period.

## Model

A policy records institution/program/campus/cohort applicability, effective dates, scale, ordered score intervals or rules, grade symbols, grade points, pass status, rounding boundary, exceptional statuses, source sections, and verification state.

## Rules

- Intervals must be complete or explicitly partial, non-overlapping, and boundary-precise.
- Rounding occurs before or after mapping only as the official policy states.
- Incomplete, withdrawal, audit, pass/fail, transfer, or disciplinary codes require sourced semantics.
- Policy changes create versions and do not rewrite historical attempt outcomes.
- No common 4.0-scale assumption may substitute for verified CUI policy.

## Evaluation

The result stores raw score, applicable rounding, mapped grade, points, outcome, policy version, and source. Missing applicability produces `UNRESOLVED`.

## Acceptance criteria

Boundary tests cover every interval edge, mappings are source-linked, and the engine cannot guess a grade from an unverified scale.
