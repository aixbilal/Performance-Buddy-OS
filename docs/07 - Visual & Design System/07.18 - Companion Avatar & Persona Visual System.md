# 07.18 - Companion Avatar & Persona Visual System
## Purpose

Define a persistent, accessible visual identity for visible companions while keeping product information clearer than character decoration.

## System

The Performance Guide / Head Coach, Culture & Knowledge Guide, and Growth & Communication Guide each receive a stable avatar silhouette, restrained accent family, name, role label, and motion signature. Identity tokens are stored independently of provider/model configuration. Provider failover must never recolor, rename, or replace a persona.

Avatars support compact, standard, and reduced-motion states. Expressions communicate interaction state—available, thinking, needs review, offline/degraded—not simulated emotion or manipulation. Text labels never rely on color alone. Contrast, zoom, keyboard focus, screen-reader names, high-contrast themes, and reduced motion meet Phase 06 accessibility requirements.

Hidden specialists such as Academic, Development, Recovery, Language, and Research normally appear as labeled source chips or consultation records, not foreground characters. Future specialists use the same neutral specialist pattern unless explicitly promoted through governance and design review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Persona assets are stable across providers, devices, and sessions.
- The Head Coach is visually identifiable as synthesizer without implying unchecked authority.
- Hidden specialist participation is traceable but does not crowd the primary UI.
- Offline, permission, uncertainty, and review-required states remain legible without animation.
