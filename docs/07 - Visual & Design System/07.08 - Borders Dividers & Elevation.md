# Borders Dividers & Elevation

## Purpose

Define structural separators, focus outlines, selection boundaries, and restrained elevation behavior.

## Border roles

- Subtle separator for low-emphasis grouping.
- Standard component boundary.
- Strong boundary for selected or nested regions.
- Focus indicator with guaranteed visibility.
- Semantic error/warning boundary paired with text/icon.

Exact thickness, radius, and shadow values are tokenized and provisional pending rendering tests.

## Dividers

Use dividers when alignment/spacing alone cannot express grouping. Avoid lines between every item in comfortable lists. Dividers should not create false table structure or compete with content.

## Radius

Use a small controlled radius scale linked to component size and interaction. Excessive pill shapes are reserved for chips/status/filter controls where the shape communicates containment.

## Elevation

Elevation represents stacking and interaction, not importance or performance. Prefer tonal surface plus border for persistent panels; use shadow primarily for overlays and temporary layers. Glows are not a general elevation mechanism.

## Focus and selection

Focus outline remains visible over every surface and is not removed for aesthetic reasons. Keyboard focus and selection use distinct treatments. High-contrast/system modes may replace custom styling.

## Acceptance criteria

Boundaries survive dark theme, zoom, high contrast, dense tables, and overlapping layers; semantic color is not the only error signal.
