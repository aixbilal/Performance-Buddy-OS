# Mobile Design Patterns

## Purpose

Define future companion patterns for fast, accessible, offline capture and lightweight review.

## Status

This is `MOBILE` design direction, not initial CORE delivery. Exact native/cross-platform component approach remains `RESEARCH REQUIRED`.

## Patterns

- Bottom or platform-appropriate primary navigation.
- Quick-capture launcher and focused capture sheet/page.
- Today list with next action and compact schedule.
- Course/assignment/session quick views.
- Routine and expense one-step capture where safe.
- Pending/offline/sync-conflict queue.
- Notification deep-link destination.
- Lightweight review and search.

## Adaptation rules

Do not shrink desktop sidebars, data tables, or multi-panel dashboards. Reorder content into a single primary flow. Defer advanced configuration to desktop. Keep critical actions reachable, targets generous, text scalable, and keyboard/external-input support where the platform provides it.

## Capture states

Draft/extracted, locally saved, pending sync, conflicted, confirmed remote, and failed remain distinct. Camera/voice/AI extraction is reviewable before confirmation.

## Privacy

Sensitive values are minimized in app switcher, lock screen, and notifications. Permission prompts explain purpose at the moment of need.

## Acceptance criteria

Common captures complete quickly offline, layouts survive small screens and large text, conflicts are understandable, and mobile never bypasses domain validation or approval.
