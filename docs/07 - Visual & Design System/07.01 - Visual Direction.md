# Visual Direction

## Purpose

Set the intended visual character and the authority relationship among product requirements, design work, and references.

## Character

Performance Buddy OS should feel composed, private, intelligent, and durable. The interface favors dark neutral foundations, readable contrast, quiet structure, restrained accent use, and clear state hierarchy. It should not resemble a noisy gaming dashboard, corporate SaaS template, or science-fiction control panel.

## Visual hierarchy

The current task and next decision are strongest. Supporting evidence, relationships, and analytics are quieter but discoverable. Critical constraints are prominent without alarmism. AI content is labeled and visually separated from confirmed records.

## Reference authority

```text
Product requirements and UX rules
            ↓
Coherent buildable design system
            ↑
Reference images and generated concepts
```

References may inspire density, surface treatment, typography mood, charts, or atmosphere. They cannot override accessibility, domain ownership, interaction rules, platform feasibility, or reusable components.

## Dark-first boundary

Dark-first is the working direction, not permission for low contrast or pure-black glare. A light theme is not required for initial delivery but token architecture must not make it impossible. Exact palettes require visual/accessibility testing.

## Avoid

Excess glow, gradients without purpose, glass effects that reduce legibility, excessive rounded cards, permanent neon domain colors, decorative graphs, color-only status, and imagery behind dense reading content.

## Acceptance criteria

A new screen feels recognizably part of the product while remaining calm, readable, task-oriented, and honest about authority and state.
