# Documentation Freeze & Revision Policy

## Purpose

Define how documents become approved or frozen, what changes are allowed, and how controlled revisions prevent accidental baseline mutation.
## Lifecycle

`DRAFT → IN REVIEW → APPROVED → FROZEN`, with `SUPERSEDED → ARCHIVED` when replaced. Not every approved document must be frozen; freeze is reserved for baselines or decisions requiring strong stability.

## Frozen baseline rule

The v1.0 root tree cannot be renamed, moved, deleted, renumbered, merged, or expanded without explicit owner approval. A proposal must identify reason, affected paths, ownership changes, cross-references, migration, compatibility, and new version.

## Content revisions

Backward-compatible clarifications may increment a `v1.x` document/baseline revision when approved. Structural or breaking changes require `v2.0+`. Draft edits cannot claim authority until reviewed. Emergency corrections that prevent immediate harm may be made with a visible record and prompt retrospective approval, but convenience is not an emergency.

## Review gates

Confirm source authority, scope ownership, contradictions, labels, privacy, deterministic/AI boundaries, links, and acceptance criteria. Record approval and change log entry. Superseded documents move to Phase 99 only after references are migrated; never delete history merely to hide disagreement.

## Rejection

Rejected proposals retain enough rationale to prevent repeated debate and belong in the appropriate archive when no longer active.
