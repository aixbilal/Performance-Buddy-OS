# Decision Log

## Purpose

Record approved architectural and product decisions, their evidence, consequences, and supersession history without inventing unresolved technology choices.
## Decision process

A decision entry includes ID, title, status, date, owner, context, considered options, decision, rationale, consequences, affected documents, and supersession link. Status values are `PROPOSED`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, or `SUPERSEDED`.
