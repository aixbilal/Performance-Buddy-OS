# Source of Truth Rules

## Purpose

Define how authoritative project knowledge is created, ranked, cited, changed, and protected from unsupported interpretation.
## Authority order

1. Locked root blueprint for structural paths and names.
2. Approved Phase 00 governance documents.
3. Previously approved domain and architecture documents within their ownership boundaries.
4. Official sources and policies for external facts.
5. Explicit user-confirmed information.
6. Imported personal records, with provenance.
7. Research and reference material.
8. AI-generated interpretations.
9. Inferences and provisional assumptions.

The more specific authoritative owner governs within its scope, provided it does not contradict a higher-order control source.

## Required rules

- Every material factual claim must be traceable to a source class from `00.06`.
- AI output is never an official fact solely because it is plausible or well written.
- Official academic rules require an official source; personal records do not establish university-wide policy.
- Missing evidence must be represented as `OPEN`, `PROVISIONAL`, or `RESEARCH REQUIRED`.
- Derived values must retain links to inputs, formula version, and calculation time.
- A document may summarize another owner but must link to it and avoid redefining its rule.
- Conflicts are handled through `00.18`, not silently resolved.

## Human approval

The project owner approves baseline changes, locked decisions, and meaningful AI-proposed writes. Approval must identify what was accepted. Silence, file presence, or generated text is not approval.

## Acceptance criteria

A reader can distinguish verified fact, user configuration, imported evidence, derived result, interpretation, inference, and unresolved assumption; every authoritative rule has one owner; conflicts are visible and auditable.
