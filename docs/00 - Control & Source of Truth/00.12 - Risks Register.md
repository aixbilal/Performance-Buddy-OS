# Risks Register

## Purpose

Track product, data, architectural, safety, and delivery risks with concrete mitigations and review triggers.
## Scale

Likelihood and impact are `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, or `HIGH`. Priority is determined by combined exposure and irreversibility, not arithmetic alone.

## Initial register

| ID | Risk | L/I | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| RSK-001 | Architecture drifts from offline-first to cloud-required | M/H | Capability labeling, offline acceptance tests, Phase 33 ownership |
| RSK-002 | AI-generated academic rules are treated as official | M/H | Provenance classes and official-source gates |
| RSK-003 | LLM performs deterministic grade/financial calculations | M/H | Phase 23 validation and tested calculation engines |
| RSK-004 | Sensitive personal data is exposed to providers | M/H | Permission zones, minimization, approval, redaction |
| RSK-005 | Documentation duplicates authoritative rules | M/M | Ownership links and duplicate-responsibility audit |
| RSK-006 | Scope expands into premature SaaS/multi-user complexity | M/H | Personal-first boundary and non-goals |
| RSK-007 | AI recommendations sacrifice sleep or protected constraints | M/H | Hard validation before proposals are actionable |
| RSK-008 | AI-assisted implementation becomes unmaintainable by owner | M/H | Human learning checkpoints and code-understanding rules |
| RSK-009 | Local data loss or corruption | L/H | Backup, migrations, integrity constraints, recovery tests |
| RSK-010 | Rigid streaks create guilt or distort behavior | M/M | Rest/excused/maintenance states and calm language |

## Management

Each active risk must gain an owner and trigger before the owning phase is approved. Realized risks become incidents or issues; they are not removed from history. Acceptance of high residual risk requires explicit owner approval.
