# Focus Mode Overview

## Intent

Focus Mode is the calm execution surface for one current commitment. It reduces navigation and interruption while keeping the user in control. It is not a lock-in mechanism, surveillance surface, or proof that useful work occurred.

## Entry contract

A session may begin from a planned item, an entity, a quick action, or an explicitly unplanned start. Before activation, the system shows the session type, linked context, intended outcome or evidence target, and any relevant time or safety boundary. Missing optional data must not block a useful offline start.

## Active surface

The visual hierarchy is:

1. current task and intended evidence;
2. elapsed or remaining time with pause and finish controls;
3. source, course, project, exercise, or other context;
4. lightweight notes and evidence capture;
5. quiet access to the plan and exit.

The surface visibly distinguishes planned, unplanned, paused, interrupted, completing, saved, and failed-to-save states. Notification handling follows the interruption policy; urgent and safety-critical exceptions remain possible.

## Control and safety

The user may pause, finish early, change the completion outcome, or exit without losing recoverable data. Focus Mode must not use guilt, streak threats, or deceptive friction. Fitness sessions additionally defer to domain safety constraints; the focus surface cannot override recovery boundaries.

## Capability

The complete baseline is `CORE`: local, offline, and deterministic. `LOCAL`, `V1`, `V2`, or `V3` assistance may suggest a label, note, or next action, but is optional, visibly attributed, reversible, and never the authority for factual completion.
