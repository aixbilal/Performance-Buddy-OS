# Session Completion Evidence

## Principle

Completion is a claim supported by proportionate evidence, not a reward for elapsed time. Evidence requirements should match the consequence of the claim and remain lightweight for low-risk activities.

## Outcome states

Canonical outcomes are `Complete`, `Partial`, `Deferred`, `Skipped`, `Excused`, `Rest`, `Maintenance`, `Not Applicable`, and `Unknown`. Domain rules may restrict or refine their use without changing the shared meaning.

## Evidence model

Evidence records type, source, captured time, linked artifact or value, and optional user note. Sources include manual statement, artifact link, assessment result, system event, device import, and external integration. Derived or AI-produced evidence retains provenance and cannot masquerade as direct observation.

## Validation

`CORE` validates required fields, accessible links where locally possible, conflicting outcomes, and duplicate submissions. It does not infer mastery or correctness from duration, file existence, word count, commits, or sensor data alone. High-impact completion may require stronger domain-specific verification.

## Human authority and revision

The user confirms the outcome and can correct it later. Corrections preserve revision history and cause dependent analytics or plan reconciliation to recompute. AI may suggest evidence gaps or draft a summary, but cannot certify its own output.

## Failure state

When evidence cannot be saved, the completion remains a recoverable draft and must not appear as safely persisted.
