# University Study Sessions

## Purpose

University Study sessions specialize the shared lifecycle for courses, topics, assessments, and learning evidence. Academic documentation remains authoritative for curriculum, assessment, mastery, and grade semantics.

## Suggested context

A session may link course, topic, lecture, assignment, exam, knowledge item, and study method. The intended outcome should be observable: solve a problem set, retrieve concepts without notes, produce a summary, review errors, or complete a defined assignment segment.

## Evidence

Suitable evidence includes attempted and checked problems, retrieval results, an error log, submitted work, annotated notes, or a concise explanation in the user's own words. Reading time or video duration alone does not demonstrate learning. Self-reported confidence remains separate from tested performance.

## Closing prompts

The user can record outcome, what remains unclear, errors to revisit, and the next useful action. Partial work is recorded honestly rather than forced into complete/incomplete binary states. Assessment deadlines and fixed university commitments flow from their owning domains.

## AI-off compatibility

Practice can prohibit hints, solutions, summaries, or generated questions while retaining timer, notes, and manual evidence. Later review may use AI only with explicit user action and preserved provenance.
