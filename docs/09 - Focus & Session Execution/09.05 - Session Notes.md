# Session Notes

## Purpose

Session notes capture working context with minimal disruption. They support recall and evidence without turning every session into administrative work.

## Note model

A session can contain timestamped quick notes, a closing summary, blockers, questions, decisions, and links to durable artifacts. Notes may be plain Markdown and must remain available offline. Autosave uses local drafts and exposes save failure clearly.

## Durable knowledge boundary

Session notes are execution records, not automatically canonical knowledge. Promotion into the Knowledge domain is an explicit action that preserves a backlink to the session and avoids duplicate ownership. Likewise, decisions affecting architecture or product truth must be promoted to the proper controlled document rather than buried here.

## AI assistance

`LOCAL` or later intelligence may propose a summary, extract tasks, or suggest knowledge promotion. Proposed text is labeled, editable, and approved before becoming part of the record. AI cannot invent evidence, overwrite the user's raw notes, or treat a generated summary as proof of understanding.

## Privacy and retention

Notes inherit the privacy classification of their linked context and may be more restrictive. Sensitive spiritual, health, financial, or personal content is not exposed to cloud capability without explicit scope-aware consent. Retention and deletion follow platform data policy, with recovery where applicable.
