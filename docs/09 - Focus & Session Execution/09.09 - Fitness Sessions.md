# Fitness Sessions

## Purpose

Fitness sessions record strength, running, endurance, mobility, recovery, and other physical activity. Fitness & Recovery documentation owns training prescription, readiness, and safety semantics.

## Safety invariant

No timer, plan target, streak, or AI suggestion overrides pain, injury signals, medical guidance, environmental risk, or a protected recovery boundary. The user can stop immediately without punitive messaging. The product is not a medical device or emergency service.

## Suggested context and evidence

A session may link a workout, exercise, route, training block, or recovery activity. Evidence can include completed sets and repetitions, load, distance, duration, pace, perceived exertion, mobility work, or a manual completion statement. Wearable data is optional and must preserve source and uncertainty.

## Pauses and intervals

Rest intervals, deliberate workout blocks, and interruptions are distinct. Active duration does not alone indicate training quality. Implausible sensor or manual values are flagged, not silently corrected.

## Completion and recovery

Closing may capture completion state, modifications, discomfort, exertion, and recovery note. A shortened or recovery session can be the correct outcome. Intelligence may suggest adaptation only within declared capability and domain safeguards; the human makes the final decision.
