# Language Learning

## Purpose

Support practical German or Italian communication for living abroad, including everyday speaking, listening, vocabulary, and basic grammar rather than textbook completion alone.

## Baseline configuration

The normal target is approximately 30–40 minutes per day. A balanced session may include vocabulary, listening, speaking/pronunciation, reading, and basic grammar, with emphasis adjusted from evidence. During heavy midterm or terminal periods, the path may enter approximately 10–15 minutes of maintenance through light vocabulary, listening, or speaking review.

## Practical scope

Introductions; numbers, dates, and time; shopping and food; directions and transport; asking for help; accommodation; university and workplace basics; doctor/pharmacy basics; emergencies; and everyday conversation. The goal is functional real-world communication, progressing from beginner foundations toward greater independence.

## Path rules

Phase 11 owns the generic learning path. When both German and Italian are configured, one normally receives primary progression and the other light maintenance/exposure unless the user explicitly changes strategy. Speaking cannot be replaced by vocabulary-only streaks.

## Evidence

Recall, listening comprehension, recorded/self-reviewed speaking, live conversation, role-play, reading, writing, and source-grounded tests retain assistance and context. Time and app lessons alone do not prove ability.

## AI boundary

`V1` may conduct conversations, pronunciation-oriented practice, and quizzes with clear limitations. `V2` may identify neglected skills. AI cannot certify CEFR level without an appropriate assessment.

## Acceptance criteria

The path works offline manually, prioritizes real situations, supports exam maintenance without failure, balances both languages safely, and tracks skill dimensions separately.
