# Routine Priority & Flexibility

## Purpose

Keep meaningful routines sustainable when academics, development, fitness, travel, illness, exams, and recovery change available capacity.

## Priority model

Safety and protected constraints apply first. Prayer and essential personal care follow user-defined importance and context. Health-adjacent routines respect professional advice. Reading and language may shift between progression, maintenance, reduced, paused, or normal states.

## Flexibility rules

Minimum viable forms are explicit and do not masquerade as full progression. During exams, language may reduce from 30–40 minutes to roughly 10–15 minutes. A missed routine is not automatically rescheduled into an overloaded day. Rest, excused, partial, maintenance, not-applicable, and unknown remain honest states.

## Conflict handling

Show the constraint and bounded options: shorten, change form, move, maintain, defer, pause, or retain with acknowledged trade-off. No routine uses streak pressure to override sleep, injury/illness concern, or fixed commitments.

## Acceptance criteria

Adaptation preserves meaning and history, sensitive exceptions need no detailed reason, priorities remain user-controlled, and returning after disruption starts with the smallest useful step.
