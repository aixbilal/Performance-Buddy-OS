# Smoking Avoidance

## Purpose

Support smoking/tobacco avoidance and quitting efforts with privacy, compassion, and evidence-based referral rather than shame or punishment.

## Model

The user may record tobacco-free days/occurrences, use events, cravings, triggers, coping actions, lapse versus return, and support contacts. Detailed logging is optional. Nicotine/tobacco categories remain explicit where relevant.

## Rules

A lapse does not erase prior progress or trigger guilt language. Missing data is unknown. The app does not recommend medication, diagnose dependence, or promise a quit outcome. It may encourage professional cessation support; WHO states that all tobacco use is harmful and that counseling and medication can improve quitting success: [WHO tobacco fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tobacco).

## Safety and privacy

Withdrawal or health concerns route to qualified care; urgent symptoms require local emergency help. Records are highly private, excluded from public comparison and universal scores, and not shared with AI/providers without explicit consent.

## Acceptance criteria

The feature supports immediate return after a lapse, provides nonjudgmental options, protects privacy, and never substitutes gamification for evidence-based cessation help.
