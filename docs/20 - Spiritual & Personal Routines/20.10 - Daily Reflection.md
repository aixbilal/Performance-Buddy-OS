# Daily Reflection

## Purpose

Offer a private, lightweight space to notice what happened, what mattered, and what may help tomorrow without forcing disclosure or generating a psychological score.

## Prompt model

Prompts are optional, versioned, and user-configurable. Examples include: what went well, what felt difficult, what was learned, what needs attention, gratitude, and one next action. The user may answer with text, tags, a bounded self-rating, or skip.

## Rules

Reflection is not required to close a day. Empty and skipped remain distinct. AI summaries are optional derived proposals and cannot diagnose mood, motivation, faith, or mental health. Repeated distress language may justify a gentle suggestion to seek human support only under an approved safety design, not an automated diagnosis.

## Privacy

Reflection is among the most private categories: local by default, excluded from global snippets/AI/cloud unless explicitly allowed, and protected from notification previews.

## Acceptance criteria

Users control prompts and retention, raw text is never overwritten, no completion score depends on disclosure, and provider exposure is explicit and revocable.
