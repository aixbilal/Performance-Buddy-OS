# Phase 20 — Spiritual & Personal Routines

## Purpose

Phase 20 defines respectful, private, flexible tracking for prayer, hydration, nutrition habits, skincare, smoking avoidance, reading, language learning, and daily reflection. It supports consistency without converting personal, spiritual, or health-adjacent behavior into moral scores.

## Document map

| Range | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| 20.01–20.03 | Routine domain and prayer models |
| 20.04–20.07 | Health-adjacent personal habits |
| 20.08–20.10 | Reading, language, and reflection |
| 20.11–20.12 | Priority, flexibility, and privacy |

## Ownership boundaries

Phase 10 supplies tracker and recurrence mechanics. Phase 11 supplies learning paths. Phase 20 owns routine-specific meaning, privacy, flexibility, and evidence. Fitness owns training/recovery; medical diagnosis and treatment remain outside the product.

## Invariants

1. Prayer records cannot measure intention, sincerity, acceptance, or spiritual worth.
2. Private reflection and routine data never enter a universal performance score.
3. Missing records remain unknown; they are not automatically failures.
4. Rest, excused, maintenance, partial, and not-applicable states remain legitimate where applicable.
5. Health-adjacent habits use conservative tracking and never replace qualified advice.
6. Language learning supports German/Italian practical communication for 30–40 minutes normally and reduced maintenance during exams.
7. `CORE` works locally without AI; meaningful AI proposals require approval and privacy permission.
