# Prayer Tracking

## Purpose

Provide private, respectful recording of prayer occurrences as a personal aid without judging faith, intention, sincerity, quality, or religious acceptance.

## Model

A user-configured prayer routine can contain named occurrences, schedule/window references, completion state, capture time, optional private note, correction history, and privacy policy. Phase 20 does not hard-code jurisprudential rules or infer them from location/calendar data.

## Rules

- The user defines the relevant prayer set, labels, timing source, and applicable exceptions.
- A check records only the user’s chosen factual meaning.
- Missing remains `Unknown`, not skipped.
- Excused or not-applicable states use user-configured context without requiring sensitive detail.
- Retroactive entries retain entry time and never claim automated verification.
- Streak pressure, public comparison, guilt language, and spiritual scoring are prohibited.

## AI boundary

AI cannot determine religious obligation, intention, validity, or acceptance. Questions requiring religious authority should route to trusted qualified sources selected by the user.

## Acceptance criteria

Records are private by default, corrections are auditable, reminders are discreet, exceptions are flexible, and no output makes a spiritual judgment.
