# Reading

## Purpose

Support academic, technical, language, spiritual, and leisure reading without treating pages or minutes as proof of understanding.

## Model

Reading item/source, purpose, start/finish state, pages/chapters or duration where useful, session references, notes/bookmarks, language, privacy, and evidence. Source ownership and copyright permissions remain respected.

## Modes

Leisure, overview, deep study, reference, language practice, and review are distinct contexts. Completion may mean finishing a selected section, not necessarily the whole work.

## Rules

Page counts across formats are not directly comparable. Audiobooks and accessible formats are legitimate. Skimming is recorded honestly when useful. Notes require explicit promotion to Knowledge OS. Reading time alone does not establish recall or mastery; retrieval or application evidence is separate.

## Acceptance criteria

Reading remains enjoyable and flexible, format differences are visible, private material is protected, and streaks cannot pressure meaningless page accumulation.
