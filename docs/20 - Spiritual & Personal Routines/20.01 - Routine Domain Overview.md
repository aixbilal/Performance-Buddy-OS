# Routine Domain Overview

## Purpose

The Routine domain provides a calm home for repeated spiritual and personal practices that matter but should not be treated like productivity contests.

## Core model

Routine definition, occurrence, completion state, evidence, privacy class, schedule, flexibility policy, reminder policy, maintenance mode, and review. Tracker implementation comes from Phase 10; this phase owns meaning and restrictions.

## Principles

Capture should be quick and optional in detail. The system describes records neutrally, protects private categories, and supports restart after disruption. Streaks are hidden or secondary and cannot pressure prayer, smoking cessation, nutrition, skincare, or other sensitive behavior.

## Capability

Manual configuration, local reminders, occurrences, and records are `CORE`. `V1` may answer user-requested questions or draft routine changes; `V2` may surface patterns with consent. AI cannot infer private intent, diagnose behavior, or silently change schedules.

## Acceptance criteria

Every routine has one owner, clear evidence proportionality, valid flexibility states, privacy controls, and a complete offline workflow; routine records never become a moral ranking.
