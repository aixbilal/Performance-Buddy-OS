# Private Routine Data Handling

## Purpose

Apply strong privacy boundaries to prayer, reflection, smoking, nutrition, skincare, language recordings, and other personal-routine records.

## Classification

Private-by-default fields include prayer details, exception reasons, reflection text, tobacco events/triggers, health-adjacent symptoms, nutrition detail, skincare photos/reactions, voice recordings, and personal support notes. Metadata may inherit the same or stricter classification.

## Rules

Store locally by default; minimize collection; hide sensitive notification previews; partition search/indexes; exclude universal performance scoring; require explicit category- and task-specific consent for AI/cloud; and log provider exposure without copying sensitive content into logs.

## Revocation and export

Revocation stops future access and purges disallowed derived caches/embeddings. Exports preview included categories and support redaction. Deletion follows recoverability policy and never deletes unrelated owner-domain records silently.

## AI boundary

Permission for one routine or sync does not grant access to another or to AI processing. AI cannot infer unrecorded prayer, smoking, diet, mood, diagnosis, or personal worth.

## Acceptance criteria

Permission tests prevent cross-category leakage, users can inspect and revoke exposure, minimal offline workflows need no cloud, and private data never appears in public comparison or generic dashboards by default.
