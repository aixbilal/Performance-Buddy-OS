# Nutrition Habits

## Purpose

Track sustainable food-related habits and planning cues without becoming a calorie prescription, eating-disorder tool, or medical nutrition service.

## Habit examples

User-defined patterns may include regular meals, including vegetables/fruit, protein or whole-food choices, preparing food, mindful eating, or another culturally and financially appropriate behavior. General reference: [CDC healthy eating guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/nutrition/features/healthy-eating-tips.html).

## Rules

No food is assigned moral worth. The app avoids extreme restriction, compensatory exercise, rigid “clean/cheat” labels, body shaming, or a universal diet. Calories, macros, allergens, conditions, pregnancy needs, and treatment diets require appropriate source and professional context before use.

## Evidence and privacy

Simple attestation is normally sufficient; meal photos and detailed logs are optional sensitive data. Missing entries remain unknown. Patterns may be reviewed without turning individual meals into failure.

## Acceptance criteria

Habits are user-configurable and non-moralizing, reminders can be disabled, sensitive detail is minimized, and concerning eating behavior is not coached by AI as routine optimization.
