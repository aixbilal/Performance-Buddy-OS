# Skincare

## Purpose

Track a user-defined skincare routine and product tolerance without diagnosing skin conditions or prescribing treatment.

## Model

Morning/evening or event-based steps may record product category/name, order, application state, optional reaction note, start/stop dates, and source. Product formulation changes create a new version rather than altering history.

## Rules

The product does not recommend prescription medicines, diagnose acne/rashes, or infer allergies. New or worsening irritation, swelling, blistering, infection concern, or other significant symptoms should prompt stopping the suspected routine as appropriate and seeking qualified care. Patch-testing or specialist instructions are recorded only as user/professional guidance.

## Sun protection

General education may link to the [American Academy of Dermatology’s skin-care guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics); it is not individualized treatment.

## Acceptance criteria

Steps remain optional and editable, reactions are treated conservatively, private photos are not required, and AI cannot diagnose or prescribe from routine records.
