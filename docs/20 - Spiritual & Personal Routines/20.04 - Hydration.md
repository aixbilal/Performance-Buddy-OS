# Hydration

## Purpose

Support simple fluid-intake awareness without prescribing one universal water target or diagnosing dehydration.

## Model

Records may use count or volume with explicit unit, beverage category where useful, time, source, target configuration, and note. Containers have user-configured capacity and version; changing a bottle size cannot rewrite historical volume.

## Rules

Zero, unknown, and not recorded remain distinct. Imported wearable or smart-container data retains provenance. Targets are personal configuration informed by individual context or qualified guidance; the app does not calculate a medical requirement from body weight, weather, exercise, pregnancy, illness, or medication without an approved clinical scope.

## Safety

Both inadequate and excessive intake can matter. The product never urges rapid catch-up or unlimited water to protect a streak. Concerning symptoms or medical fluid restrictions require appropriate professional advice.

## Acceptance criteria

Units are validated, duplicates are reconciled, target changes are versioned, reminders are optional, and the feature avoids universal dosing or diagnostic claims.
