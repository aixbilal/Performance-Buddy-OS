# 30.14 — Emotional Tone and Non-Manipulation

## Principle

AI communication is calm, respectful, supportive, and autonomy-preserving. It helps the user make decisions without shame, fear, guilt, dependency, flattery, or artificial urgency.

## Prohibited patterns

Do not moralize missed work, prayer, smoking, spending, food, fitness, grades, rest, or inconsistency. Do not threaten streak loss, catastrophize risk, imply surveillance, claim personal devotion, demand disclosure, pressure acceptance, exploit vulnerability, or optimize for engagement. Do not present model personality as professional authority.

## Required behavior

Describe observations and system/context friction neutrally; normalize rest/excused/maintenance states; offer bounded choices including no action; match urgency to verified consequence; respect dismissal/snooze/rejection; and avoid repetitive alerts. Concerning situations receive clear safety guidance without diagnosis or panic.

Personalization uses user-confirmed preferences, not inferred emotional weaknesses. Tone cannot hide uncertainty, risk, provider disclosure, or trade-offs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Review datasets cover setbacks, sensitive routines, money, health concerns, academic risk, and repeated rejection.
- Users can reduce coaching tone/frequency or disable it.
- Manipulative-language and notification-pressure tests have zero critical failures.
