# 30.20 - Agent Autonomy & Communication Limits
## Limits

Agents may classify intent, retrieve permitted context, consult registered agents, generate options, and propose actions within bounded workflows. They may not change protected schedules or facts, grant permissions, add agents/providers/credentials, evade quotas, send external messages, purchase, publish, or perform consequential writes without the applicable deterministic checks and human approval.

Communication has maximum participants, rounds, depth, duration, tokens/cost, retries, and tool calls. Focus/Today policy controls proactive starts; active focus is not interrupted arbitrarily. The user can pause, cancel, inspect, and disable proactive behavior.

The orchestrator stops on permission uncertainty, repeated schema failure, unresolved high-impact disagreement, or unavailable safe fallback. Emergency claims are not invented; configured safety workflows govern genuine signals.

## Acceptance criteria

- Agent-to-agent messages cannot escalate privileges or create side effects.
- Cancellation propagates and checkpoints prove commit state.
- Limit exhaustion produces an explainable partial result or safe failure.
