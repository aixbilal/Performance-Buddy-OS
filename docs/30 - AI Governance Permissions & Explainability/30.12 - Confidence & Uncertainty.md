# 30.12 — Confidence and Uncertainty

## Distinctions

Model confidence, classifier probability, evidence coverage, source authority, statistical uncertainty, deterministic validation, and recommendation usefulness are different signals. None substitutes for another or for approval.

## Requirements

Material output states evidence coverage, missing data, assumptions, plausible alternatives, known confounders, freshness, and confidence only when it has an evaluated meaning. Use calibrated bands or plain-language levels tied to measured behavior; avoid decorative percentages and false precision.

Low confidence may trigger clarification, narrower scope, human review, alternative presentation, or abstention. High confidence cannot bypass permissions, safety, rules, citations, or approval. Correlation remains non-causal regardless of confidence.

Calibration is evaluated by task, consequence, domain, model/version, language, and data condition. Users can see what additional evidence would reduce uncertainty.

## Acceptance criteria

- Calibration, abstention, overconfidence, and subgroup/data-coverage tests pass declared thresholds.
- UI language is consistent with measured reliability.
- Unknown or unmeasured confidence is labeled unknown, not converted into certainty.
