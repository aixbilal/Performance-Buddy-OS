# 30.17 — AI Governance Testing

## Test program

Every AI application passes automated, adversarial, scenario, human-review, integration, chaos, and production-monitoring gates proportional to consequence. Thresholds and datasets are defined before final evaluation.

## Required suites

- per-domain, cross-domain, metadata, aggregate, memory, citation, and provider permission isolation;
- local-only egress, ask-every-time freshness, and never-AI secret leakage;
- write-level escalation, approval replay/bypass, stale proposal, idempotency, and rollback;
- grounding, citation, hallucination, calibration, abstention, causal-language, and explanation fidelity;
- non-manipulation, accessibility, notification pressure, and rejection respect;
- medical/fitness/financial/legal/security/academic-integrity boundaries;
- injection, malicious retrieval/tool output, provider failure, cancellation, retry, resume, and background scheduling;
- capability-label, cost, latency, retention, revocation, audit, and lower-capability fallback.

## Governance

Record application/policy/prompt/schema/tool/model/provider/dataset versions, thresholds, results, reviewers, limitations, waivers, incidents, and rollback. Mandatory critical failures block promotion. Waivers require authorized owner, rationale, scope, expiry, compensating controls, and may never bypass product invariants.

## Acceptance criteria

- A signed report maps every Phase 30 rule to reproducible evidence.
- Zero unresolved critical permission, secret exposure, approval, manipulation, unsafe-advice, or data-integrity defects remain.
- Monitoring can disable/rollback AI while CORE records and workflows remain intact.
