# 30.16 — Recommendation Audit Trail

## Purpose

Provide a tamper-evident, privacy-minimized record sufficient to explain how a material AI recommendation was produced, validated, decided, and acted upon.

## Required record

Run/application/capability/mode; user trigger or approved schedule; purpose; permission/consent and data-zone decisions; context manifest/provenance references; prompt/schema/method; provider/model/runtime/adapter; tool calls and normalized outcomes; recommendation/version; assumptions/confidence; Phase 23 result/reason codes; explanation shown; approval/rejection/defer/edit; owner command/idempotency; final outcome; timestamps; cost/latency; and retention/deletion state.

## Privacy and integrity

Use hashes/references and redaction rather than duplicating sensitive content. Audit metadata inherits sensitivity and cannot leak excluded categories. Access is permissioned. Append/correction events preserve history; secrets and private chain-of-thought are never logged.

## Acceptance criteria

- A material action can be traced end-to-end without relying on model recollection.
- Audit loss/failure blocks high-consequence commits when policy requires traceability.
- Export, retention, correction, deletion, and incident workflows are tested.
