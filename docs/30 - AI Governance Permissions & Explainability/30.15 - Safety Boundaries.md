# 30.15 — Safety Boundaries

## Protected areas

Safety boundaries cover health/medical, fitness load and injury context, sleep/recovery, mental-health crisis indicators, financial/legal matters, privacy/security, academic integrity, and physical/digital external actions.

## Rules

AI may provide general education, summarize user records, invoke deterministic tools, and propose conservative planning within domain policies. It cannot diagnose, prescribe, provide exercise clearance, recommend regulated financial products/transactions, determine legal obligations, facilitate cheating, expose secrets, or override protected constraints.

Urgent symptoms, immediate danger, self-harm/crisis language, suspected fraud, or other high-stakes situations follow a reviewed escalation response appropriate to available location/context, encourage qualified or emergency support, and avoid pretending the model assessed severity. Exact jurisdictional resources require maintained authoritative data.

Safety enforcement lives in application boundaries, permissions, tools, schemas, deterministic rules, output checks, UI, and testing. A disclaimer does not make prohibited advice safe.

## Acceptance criteria

- Domain experts/reviewers approve high-stakes policies and evaluation cases where required.
- Adversarial tests cover unsafe advice, override pressure, false reassurance, and secret exposure.
- Boundary responses are accurate, calm, useful, and mutation-free.
