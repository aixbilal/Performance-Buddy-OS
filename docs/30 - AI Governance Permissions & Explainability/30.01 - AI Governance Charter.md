# 30.01 — AI Governance Charter

## Charter

Performance Buddy OS uses AI only when it adds evaluated value while preserving safety, privacy, human agency, source authority, offline CORE, and domain integrity.

## Principles

1. **Purpose limitation:** use only data necessary for a declared application.
2. **Human control:** AI proposes; users approve meaningful change.
3. **Deterministic authority:** formulas, policies, permissions, and constraints are code/source governed.
4. **Data minimization:** availability does not justify inclusion.
5. **Location transparency:** local and cloud processing are visibly distinct.
6. **Explainability:** material outputs show evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, validation, and alternatives.
7. **Non-manipulation:** no shame, fear, dependency, urgency pressure, or engagement optimization.
8. **Accountability:** versions, decisions, tools, exposures, approvals, and outcomes are auditable.
9. **Reversibility:** disablement, revocation, rollback, correction, and deletion are designed paths.
10. **Progressive capability:** higher levels pass stronger gates without gaining policy authority.

## Governance precedence

Safety, law/policy, source authority, permissions, protected constraints, and human control outrank convenience, personalization, cost, and model preference. Material exceptions require documented authority, scope, expiry, and compensating control; product invariants cannot be waived casually.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every AI application maps to charter principles, owner, risks, and evidence.
- Violations can disable a feature independently of CORE.
- Governance changes are versioned, reviewed, communicated, and auditable.
