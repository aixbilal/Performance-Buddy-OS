# 30.11 — Evidence Citation

## Citation contract

Material claims cite source/entity ID, owner domain, title/label, location, revision/checksum, effective/retrieval time, authority class, and relevant excerpt or field reference where permitted. Derived evidence also cites method/version, input lineage, coverage, and confidence.

## Rules

Citations support the exact nearby claim. Official sources govern institutional facts; personal records govern user-entered state; AI summaries are derived and link to originals. A retrieval score is not authority. Conflicting sources remain visible under Phase 00 conflict rules.

Never fabricate citations, pages, quotations, tool results, or source access. Inaccessible sources cannot be used to explain an output to a user lacking permission. Stale, missing, partial, or contradictory evidence qualifies or blocks the claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- Automated checks resolve citations to the claimed revision and verify accessible source spans.
- Citation precision/coverage and unsupported-claim rates meet application thresholds.
- Permission revocation invalidates citations, caches, and dependent recommendations predictably.
