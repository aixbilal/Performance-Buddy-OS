# 30.03 — Human Approval Policy

## Definition

Approval authorizes one displayed AI proposal or clearly bounded batch. It is distinct from permission to access data and from confirmation of a user-originated action.

## Required preview

Show target, before/after effect, scope, reason, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, validation outcome, conflicts, side effects, provider exposure where relevant, reversibility, expiration, and recovery limits. Choices are unambiguous: approve, edit, reject, or defer where supported.

## Rules

Approval records actor, time, proposal/version, target versions, displayed impact, scope, and result. It expires and cannot be replayed after edits, data changes, permission changes, or target changes. Approval of one item, run, domain, provider, or batch does not authorize future actions.

Dangerous actions are not preselected or triggered accidentally by Enter. Stale proposals return to review. Rejection causes no write and a reason is optional. Withdrawal before commit is honored where technically possible.

## Acceptance criteria

- Keyboard, mobile, palette, API, retry, resume, and background paths cannot bypass approval.
- Accessibility tests cover focus, labels, screen readers, warnings, and status announcements.
- Approval logs prove exactly what the user saw and what committed.
