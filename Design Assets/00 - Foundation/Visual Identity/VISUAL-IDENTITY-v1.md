# Performance Buddy OS Visual Identity v1

## Core direction

Performance Buddy OS should feel premium, private, engineered, calm, and high-performance. Its visual character combines automotive-grade precision with restrained sci-fi depth while remaining practical and readable for everyday use.

## Visual language

- Matte black, graphite, and gunmetal form the foundation.
- Surfaces may use subtle metallic edges, precise seams, and recessed construction.
- Smoked or frosted transparency is used selectively, primarily where layering or context is useful.
- Illumination uses cool white and muted blue-grey tones.
- Glow communicates state, focus, activity, or progress rather than serving as decoration.
- Layered depth should be clear without excessive glass or visual haze.
- Typography must remain readable and natural for everyday tasks, with strong numeric clarity where metrics are shown.
- Panels should feel like purposeful instruments rather than generic SaaS cards.

The intended balance is approximately:

- **70%** restrained premium software
- **20%** precision instrument
- **10%** cinematic sci-fi character

## Avoid

- RGB or gaming appearance
- Cyberpunk neon
- Generic equal-card dashboard grids
- Excessive cyan
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Spaceship-cockpit imitation
- Decorative machinery or pipes
- Excessive gauges
- Meaningless charts or data
- Tiny, unreadable technical typography
- Constant animation

Every visual choice should support clarity, state, hierarchy, and a sense of controlled performance.
